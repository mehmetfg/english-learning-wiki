# Prepositions of Place and Movement — Yer ve Hareket Edatları

Bu sayfa iki ana kategoriye ayrılır:
1. **Yer edatları (static)** — bir şeyin nerede olduğunu gösterir
2. **Hareket edatları (movement/direction)** — bir şeyin nereye gittiğini veya nasıl hareket ettiğini gösterir

---

## Yapı / Structure

**Yer:** `özne + fiil (be/sit/stand/lie) + edat + yer`
> "The book is **on** the table."

**Hareket:** `özne + hareket fiili + edat + hedef`
> "She walked **into** the room."

---

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### YER EDATLARI (Static Position)

#### IN — İçinde, Kapalı Alanda

| Kullanım | Örnek |
|----------|-------|
| Kapalı alanlar, kaplar | in the box, in the room, in the bag |
| Şehirler, ülkeler, bölgeler | in London, in France, in the north |
| Araçlar (kapalı, özel) | in a car, in a taxi, in a helicopter |
| Kurumlar (hasta/öğrenci sıfatıyla) | in hospital (BrE), in prison, in bed |

> "She's sitting **in** the garden."
> "They live **in** Paris."
> "He's been **in** hospital for a week." (hasta olarak)

#### ON — Yüzey, Zemin, Hat

| Kullanım | Örnek |
|----------|-------|
| Yüzeyler | on the table, on the wall, on the floor |
| Katlar | on the first floor, on the top floor |
| Toplu taşıma | on a bus, on a train, on a plane, on a ship |
| Bisiklet, motosiklet, at | on a bike, on a horse, on a motorcycle |
| Kıyı şeridi, adalar | on the coast, on an island |
| Sokaklar (AmE) | on Fifth Avenue, on a street (AmE) |

> "Put the glass **on** the shelf."
> "I met her **on** the train."
> "They live **on** a small island."

#### AT — Nokta, Adres, Amaç Yeri

| Kullanım | Örnek |
|----------|-------|
| Belirli noktalar | at the door, at the corner, at the top/bottom/end |
| Adresler (ev numarası) | at 42 Baker Street |
| Amaç yerleri | at school/work/home/university/church |
| Toplantı yerleri | at the station, at the airport, at the cinema |
| Masada / kürsüde | at the desk, at the counter, at the table |

> "She's waiting **at** the bus stop."
> "He lives **at** 15 Oak Road."
> "The children are **at** school right now."

#### ABOVE / BELOW — Temas Olmadan Üst/Alt

> "The plane flew **above** the clouds."
> "The temperature dropped **below** zero."
> "The apartment **above** us is very noisy."

#### OVER / UNDER — Doğrudan Üst/Alt (Genellikle Kapsar)

> "There's a bridge **over** the river."
> "She put a blanket **over** the sleeping child."
> "The cat hid **under** the bed."
> "The document is **under** the pile of books."

#### BETWEEN — İki Nesne/Kişi Arasında

> "She sat **between** her two friends."
> "The café is **between** the bank and the pharmacy."
> "Can you keep this **between** us?" (mecazi)

#### AMONG / AMONGST — Grup İçinde (İkiden Fazla)

> "He was lost **among** the crowd."
> "**Among** the applicants, three were selected."
> "She felt at ease **among** friends." (BrE: amongst)

#### BESIDE / NEXT TO / BY — Yanında, Yakınında

> "She sat **beside** me during the concert."
> "The bakery is **next to** the post office."
> "Come and sit **by** me."
> "We have a house **by** the sea." (deniz kenarında)

#### NEAR / CLOSE TO — Yakınında

> "The hotel is **near** the city centre."
> "She lives **close to** the university."

#### OPPOSITE — Karşısında

> "The hotel is **opposite** the museum."
> "She sat **opposite** him at the table."

#### IN FRONT OF / BEHIND — Önünde / Arkasında

> "The taxi stopped **in front of** the building."
> "There's a garden **behind** the house."
> "Who's **behind** you in the queue?"

#### INSIDE / OUTSIDE — İçerisi / Dışarısı

> "It's warm **inside** the building."
> "The children are playing **outside** the house."

### HAREKET EDATLARI (Movement / Direction)

#### TO — Hedefe Doğru (Genel)

> "She went **to** school."
> "He flew **to** New York."
> "Walk **to** the end of the street."

**Özel durum:** home, here, there — edatsız:
> "I'm going **home**." (NOT "to home" ❌)

#### INTO — İçine Girmek (Eşiği Geçmek)

> "She walked **into** the room."
> "He dived **into** the pool."
> "The cat jumped **into** the box."

#### ONTO — Yüzeye Çıkmak

> "He climbed **onto** the roof."
> "She jumped **onto** the table."
> "The cat leaped **onto** the sofa."

#### FROM — Başlangıç Noktası

> "She came **from** Spain."
> "Take the book **from** the shelf."
> "The bus runs **from** the station to the airport."

#### OUT OF — İçinden Çıkmak

> "She walked **out of** the room."
> "He jumped **out of** the car."
> "Take the milk **out of** the fridge."

#### OFF — Yüzeyden Ayrılmak

> "He fell **off** the bike."
> "Take your shoes **off** the sofa."
> "The paint is peeling **off** the wall."

#### THROUGH — İçinden Geçmek

> "Drive **through** the tunnel."
> "Walk **through** the park."
> "Light passes **through** glass."

#### ACROSS — Karşıdan Karşıya Geçmek

> "Swim **across** the river."
> "She walked **across** the road carefully."
> "They sailed **across** the Atlantic."

#### ALONG — Hat Boyunca

> "Walk **along** the street."
> "They sailed **along** the coast."
> "Drive **along** the motorway."

#### AROUND / ROUND — Etrafında, Çevresinde

> "Walk **around** the lake."
> "The Earth moves **around** the Sun."
> "Turn **around** — someone is calling you."

#### UP / DOWN — Yukarı / Aşağı

> "Climb **up** the stairs."
> "She ran **down** the hill."
> "The elevator went **up** to the 10th floor."

#### PAST — Yanından Geçmek (Durmadan)

> "She walked **past** the shop without stopping."
> "Drive **past** the school and turn left."

#### TOWARDS — Yönünde (Ulaşmadan)

> "She walked **towards** the exit."
> "He moved **towards** the door."
> "They headed **towards** the mountains."

### ULAŞIM EDATLARI (Transport Prepositions)

Ulaşım araçlarıyla kullanılan edatlar genellikle karıştırılır:

| IN (kapalı, özel) | ON (toplu/açık) |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| in a car | on a bus |
| in a taxi / cab | on a train |
| in a helicopter | on a plane |
| in a van | on a ship / boat |
| — | on a bike / motorcycle |
| — | on a horse |

**Binmek/inmek:**
- IN: **get in / get out of** (arabaya)
- ON: **get on / get off** (otobüse, uçağa)

**BY + araç (artikelsiz):**
> "I came **by car**." / "She travels **by train**." / "He arrived **by taxi**."
> NOT: "by the car" ❌ / "by a bus" ❌

---

### KURUM İFADELERİ (Institution Expressions)

| İfade | Anlam |
|-------|-------|
| at school/work/home/university | o yerde, o kurumda (amaç olarak) |
| in hospital (BrE) / in the hospital (AmE) | hasta olarak yatıyor |
| in school (AmE) | öğrenci olarak devam ediyor |
| in prison / in jail | mahkum olarak |
| go to school/hospital/church/prison | amacıyla gitmek |

> "The children are **at** school." (okulda, öğrenci olarak)
> "She's **in** hospital." (BrE — hasta)
> "He went **to** prison for five years." (mahkum olarak)

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### "arrived at" vs "arrived in"

| Kullanım | Kural | Örnek |
|----------|-------|-------|
| **arrived at** | küçük yer, belirli nokta | "We arrived at the station/airport." |
| **arrived in** | büyük yer, şehir/ülke | "We arrived in London/France." |

### "on the corner" vs "at the corner"

- BrE: **on the corner** ("The shop is on the corner of the street.")
- AmE: **at the corner** ("Meet me at the corner of 5th and Main.")

### "in the street" vs "on the street"

- BrE: **in the street** ("There was a car parked in the street.")
- AmE: **on the street** ("She lives on Elm Street.")

### "by the river/road/sea"

"By" yer edatı olarak "yanında / kenarında" anlamına gelir:
> "They built a cottage **by** the lake."
> "We sat **by** the fire."

### in/on/at karşılaştırma özeti

| IN | ON | AT |
|----|----|----|
| Büyük alan, kapalı | Yüzey, hat | Nokta, adres |
| içinde (3D) | üzerinde (2D) | -de/-da (1D) |
| in the city | on the road | at the crossroads |

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "The keys are **in** the drawer." — Kapalı alan.
2. "She left her phone **on** the bench." — Yüzey.
3. "I'll meet you **at** the entrance." — Belirli nokta.
4. "They live **in** a small town **in** the south of France." — Şehir/bölge.
5. "He was sitting **on** the bus reading a newspaper." — Toplu taşıma.
6. "Let's meet **at** the coffee shop **on** the corner." — Nokta + yüzey.
7. "She walked **into** the meeting room without knocking." — Eşiği geçme.
8. "He climbed **onto** the roof to fix the antenna." — Yüzeye çıkma.
9. "The children ran **out of** the school when the bell rang." — İçeriden çıkış.
10. "He fell **off** his bike and hurt his knee." — Yüzeyden ayrılma.
11. "They drove **through** the tunnel for ten minutes." — İçinden geçiş.
12. "She swam **across** the river in record time." — Karşıdan karşıya.
13. "We walked **along** the seafront all evening." — Hat boyunca.
14. "I came **by** train; it was much faster than by car." — Ulaşım aracı.
15. "She arrived **in** Tokyo after a 12-hour flight." — Büyük yer: arrived in.
16. "The plane landed and we arrived **at** Heathrow." — Belirli yer: arrived at.
17. "He's **in** hospital recovering from surgery." (BrE) — Kurum.
18. "The school is **opposite** the library, next to the park." — Konum.
