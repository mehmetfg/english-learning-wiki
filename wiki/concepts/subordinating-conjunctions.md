# Subordinating Conjunctions — Bağımlı Bağlaçlar

Bağımlı bağlaçlar, tek başına cümle kuramayan **bağımlı (yan) cümlecikleri** ana cümleye bağlayan sözcüklerdir. Yan cümlecik ana cümleden önce de sonra da gelebilir; ancak **yan cümlecik önce geldiğinde virgül zorunludur**.

> "When I arrive, I'll call you." ✓ (virgül zorunlu — yan cümlecik başta)
> "I'll call you when I arrive." ✓ (virgül yok — yan cümlecik sonda)

---

## Yapı / Structure

```
[Bağlaç + Yan Cümlecik] + , + Ana Cümlecik
           VEYA
Ana Cümlecik + [Bağlaç + Yan Cümlecik]
```

Yan cümlecik **bağımlıdır**: "Because it was raining" — anlam tamamlanmamış, ana cümleye ihtiyaç var.

---

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### 1. ZAMAN — Time Conjunctions
`when`, `while`, `as`, `before`, `after`, `since`, `until` / `till`, `as soon as`, `once`, `whenever`, `by the time`, `the moment`, `the minute`, `no sooner...than`, `hardly/scarcely...when`

**KRİTİK KURAL:** Gelecek zaman bağımlı cümleciklerinde **will kullanılmaz** — Present Simple veya Present Perfect kullanılır.
- "I'll call **when I arrive**." ✓ — NOT "when I will arrive" ❌
- "**Once she has finished**, we can leave." ✓ — NOT "once she will finish" ❌
- "**As soon as the results are out**, I'll let you know." ✓

**Farklar:**
- **as** = iki süreç **eş zamanlı** ilerler: "She sang **as** she cooked."
- **when** = **belirli bir anda**: "He smiled **when** he saw her."
- **while** = **bir süre boyunca**: "**While** you were sleeping, I worked."
- **by the time** = **son tarihe kadar**: "**By the time** you get there, I'll have left." (Future Perfect)

### 2. SEBEP / NEDEN — Reason/Cause
`because`, `since`, `as`, `for` (arkaik), `now that`, `seeing that`, `given that`, `inasmuch as`

- **because** = en güçlü, en doğrudan sebep: "She passed **because** she studied hard."
- **since/as** = konuşmacı ve dinleyicinin zaten bildiği, açık sebep: "**Since** you're here, let's start." / "**As** it was raining, we stayed in."
- **now that** = değişen/yeni bir durumu ifade eder: "**Now that** she's left, I feel lonely."

> Not: Modern İngilizcede "because" cümle başına gelebilir. "**Because** he was tired, he left early." ✓

### 3. AMAÇ — Purpose
`so that`, `in order that`, `in order to`, `so as to`, `for fear that`, `in case`

- "She studied **so that** she could pass."
- "I came early **so as not to** miss anything." (olumsuz amaç)
- "He whispered **for fear that** someone might hear." (kaygı/önlem)

**IN CASE ≠ IF — kritik fark:**
- **in case** = şu an alınan önlem, yağmur yağsın ya da yağmasın: "Take an umbrella **in case** it rains." (şimdi al — ileride lazım olabilir)
- **if** = koşullu eylem: "Take an umbrella **if** it rains." (sadece yağarsa al)

### 4. SONUÇ — Result
`so...that`, `such...that`

- **so + sıfat/zarf + that**: "He was **so tired that** he fell asleep."
- **such + (a/an) + sıfat + isim + that**: "It was **such a boring film that** I left."
- "She spoke **so quietly that** nobody heard her."
- "There was **such a crowd that** we couldn't get in."

### 5. KARŞITLIK / TAVIZ — Concession
`although`, `though`, `even though`, `while`, `whereas`, `whilst` (BrE), `even if`

- **even though** = although'dan daha güçlü, gerçek durumu kabul eder: "**Even though** she was tired, she kept working."
- **even if** = varsayımsal taviz: "**Even if** he apologizes, I won't forgive him."
- **though** cümle sonuna da gelebilir: "She came, **tired though she was**." / "She came, **tired as she was**."

**KARŞITLIK — Contrast (iki paralel durumu karşılaştırır):**
- **while / whereas** = iki zıt durumu resmice karşılaştırır (`but`'tan daha resmi): "**While/Whereas** she is outgoing, her sister is shy."

### 6. KOŞUL — Condition
`if`, `unless`, `as long as`, `provided/providing (that)`, `on condition that`, `only if`, `whether...or not`, `even if`, `supposing/suppose (that)`, `assuming (that)`, `given (that)`

→ Tam işlem için bkz. [[Conditionals Concept]]

- **unless** = "if not" demektir: "**Unless** it rains, we'll go." = "**If** it **doesn't** rain, we'll go."

### 7. TARZ / BİÇİM — Manner
`as if`, `as though`, `as`, `the way`

- "She acts **as if** she owns the place." (gerçekmiş gibi davranan — küçümseme)
- "Do it **as** I showed you." (gösterdiğim gibi yap)
- "She talked **as though** she knew everything."
- **as if / as though + past subjunctive** = gerçek olmayan/varsayımsal: "He talks **as if he were** the boss." (değil ama öyle konuşuyor)
- **as if / as though + present** = gerçekleşmesi daha mümkün: "It looks **as if it is** going to rain."

### 8. KARŞILAŞTIRMA — Comparison
`than`, `as` (as...as yapısında)

- "She works harder **than** I do."
- "He is **as** tall **as** his father."
- "Run **as quickly as** possible."

### 9. YER — Place
`where`, `wherever`, `everywhere`

- "**Wherever** you go, I'll find you."
- "You can sit **where** you like."
- "She felt at home **everywhere** she went."

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### 1. although vs. despite/in spite of
- **although + cümle**: "**Although** she was tired, she worked." ✓
- **despite/in spite of + isim / -ing**: "**Despite** her tiredness, she worked." ✓ / "**In spite of** being tired, she worked." ✓
- **despite + that + cümle** ❌ (doğrudan cümle alamaz)

### 2. while vs. whereas
- **while** = hem zaman hem karşıtlık anlamı taşır; bağlam belirler
- **whereas** = yalnızca karşıtlık, daha resmi: "I like coffee, **whereas** she prefers tea."

### 3. unless ile double negative
- "**Unless** she **doesn't** come..." ❌ — unless zaten olumsuz içerir, çift olumsuz anlam bozar
- Doğrusu: "**Unless** she comes..." veya "**If** she **doesn't** come..." ✓

### 4. no sooner...than — Past Perfect
- "**No sooner had** I sat down **than** the phone rang." ✓ (Past Perfect + inversion)
- "**Hardly/Scarcely had** she arrived **when** the trouble started." ✓
- NOT "no sooner...when" ❌ — "than" zorunlu; "hardly/scarcely" ile "when" kullanılır

### 5. Gelecek zaman bağımlı cümleciklerinde will
- "After he **will finish**..." ❌ → "After he **finishes**..." ✓
- "By the time she **will arrive**..." ❌ → "By the time she **arrives**..." ✓

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

| İngilizce | Türkçe Not |
|-----------|------------|
| I'll call you **as soon as** I land. | İndiğim anda arayacağım. (Present Simple → gelecek) |
| **Although** it was cold, she wore a T-shirt. | Soğuk olmasına rağmen — gerçek durumu kabul eder |
| **Even though** he knew the answer, he said nothing. | although'dan güçlü karşıtlık |
| **Even if** I fail, I'll try again. | Başarısız olsam bile — varsayımsal |
| She left **before** I arrived. | Ben varmadan önce ayrıldı |
| **Since** you're already here, you might as well help. | Madem buradasın — bilinen gerçek |
| **Now that** the meeting is over, we can relax. | Toplantı bittiğine göre — yeni durum |
| Take notes **in case** you forget. | Unutma ihtimaline karşı — şimdi al |
| Take notes **if** you think you might forget. | Unutacaksan al — koşullu |
| He was **so** nervous **that** he couldn't speak. | so + sıfat + that → sonuç |
| It was **such** a hot day **that** we stayed inside. | such + a + sıfat + isim + that |
| She behaves **as though** nothing happened. | Hiçbir şey olmamış gibi — as though + past |
| **No sooner had** he left **than** she arrived. | Past Perfect + inversion |
| **Wherever** you go, problems follow. | Nereye gitsen sorunlar peşinden gelir |
| **By the time** we got there, the show had started. | Biz vardığımızda gösteri başlamıştı — Future/Past Perfect |
