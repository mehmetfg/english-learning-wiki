# Relative Clauses — İlgi Cümleleri

Relative clause (ilgi cümlesi), bir ismi ya da zamiri niteleyen, ona ek bilgi veren bağımlı bir cümleciktir. "who", "which", "that", "whose", "where", "when", "why" gibi bağlaçlarla (relative pronouns / adverbs) başlar. İki ana türü vardır: tanımlayıcı (defining) ve tanımlayıcı olmayan (non-defining).

---

## Yapı / Structure

### 1. Defining Relative Clauses — Tanımlayıcı İlgi Cümleleri

Virgül KULLANILMAZ. Cümle, hangi kişi/şeyden bahsedildiğini belirtir; bu bilgi olmadan anlam tamamlanmaz.

| Relative Pronoun/Adverb | Kullanım Alanı | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **who / that** | Kişiler | "The man **who/that** called you is waiting." |
| **which / that** | Şeyler, hayvanlar | "The book **which/that** I read was fascinating." |
| **whose** | Sahiplik | "The student **whose** essay won is here." |
| **where** | Yerler | "The city **where** I grew up is beautiful." |
| **when** | Zamanlar | "The year **when** she was born was 1990." |
| **why** | Nedenler | "That's the reason **why** I came." |

### 2. Non-Defining Relative Clauses — Tanımlayıcı Olmayan İlgi Cümleleri

Virgül ZORUNLUDUR. Ek, parantez niteliğinde bilgi verir; bu bilgi çıkarılsa da cümlenin temel anlamı değişmez. **"that" kullanılamaz.**

- "My sister, **who** lives in Paris, is visiting next week."
- "The Eiffel Tower, **which** was built in 1889, is in Paris."
- "Mount Everest, **where** many climbers have died, is 8,849 m tall."

---

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### "that" vs "which/who" — Önemli Kural

"that", defining clause'larda hem kişiler hem şeyler için kullanılabilir, ancak şu durumlarda YALNIZCA "which" veya "who" kullanılır (yani "that" kullanılamaz):

- **Non-defining clauses:** "My car, **which** I bought last year, broke down." ✓ / ~~"My car, that I bought last year..."~~ ✗
- **ALL, EVERYTHING, NOTHING, SOMETHING, ANYTHING** sonrasında "that" tercih edilir veya zorunludur:
  - "Everything **that** you said is true." (NOT: everything which)
  - "Nothing **that** he does surprises me."
- **Superlative** ve **ordinal number** sonrasında "that" tercih edilir:
  - "She's the best student **that** I've ever taught."
  - "That's the first time **that** I've heard this."
- **Preposition sonrasında**: "that" değil, "which/whom" kullanılır:
  - "The company for **which** he works is multinational." (formal)

### Relative Pronoun'u Çıkarma — Contact Clause

Relative pronoun, ilgi cümleciğinde **nesne** (object) görevindeyse çıkarılabilir. **Özne (subject) görevindeyse çıkarılamaz.**

- "The book **(that)** I read was fascinating." ✓ — "that" nesne (I read the book)
- "The man **who** called me..." ✗ — "who" özne, çıkarılamaz (the man called, not "I called the man")
- "The woman **(that/whom)** you met yesterday is my boss." ✓ — nesne

**Test:** Relative pronoun'u çıkar, kalan cümle gramatik mi? Evet → çıkarılabilir.

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### 1. "What" — Özel Relative Pronoun

"What" = "the thing that" anlamında kullanılır. Öncesinde bir antecedent (isim) YOKTUR.

- "**What** you said is true." (= The thing that you said is true.)
- "**What** surprised me was his calmness."
- DİKKAT: "I liked what he said." ✓ / "I liked the book what he wrote." ✗ (burada "that/which" kullanılır)

### 2. "Which" — Önceki Cümlenin Tamamına Gönderme

"Which" bazen yalnızca bir isme değil, önceki cümlenin tamamına atıfta bulunabilir:

- "He arrived late, **which** annoyed everyone." (geç gelmesi herkesi kızdırdı)
- "She passed all her exams, **which** made her parents proud."

### 3. Who vs Whom

**Whom** = nesne / preposition sonrası (giderek nadir kullanılıyor, özellikle konuşmada)

- "The person **to whom** you spoke is the director." (formal)
- "The person **(who/that)** you spoke to is the director." (informal)
- "**Whom** did you call?" → formal / "**Who** did you call?" → informal/günlük

### 4. Preposition Konumu

| Kullanım | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Informal (sona) | "The house **that** I live **in**" | Günlük konuşma |
| Formal (başa) | "The house **in which** I live" | Resmi yazı |
| Informal | "The person you talked **to**" | Pronoun çıkarılmış |
| Formal | "The person **to whom** you spoke" | Tam formal |

### 5. Quantifying Relative Clauses

Miktar ifadeleri + of + which/whom:

- "She has three dogs, **all of which** are friendly."
- "There were ten candidates, **none of whom** was suitable."
- "He suggested several ideas, **some of which** were brilliant."
- "I spoke to many people, **most of whom** agreed with me."

### 6. Reduced Relative Clauses — Participle Clause'a Dönüşüm

| Tam Relative Clause | Kısaltılmış Hali |
|---|---|
| "The woman **who is standing** there" | "The woman **standing** there" (present participle) |
| "The car **that was stolen**" | "The **stolen** car" / "The car **stolen** last night" (past participle) |
| "The results **that have been published**" | "The **published** results" |

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

**Defining:**

1. "The doctor **who treated** me was very kind." — Hangi doktor? Beni tedavi eden.
2. "Is this the book **that** you were looking for?" — Pronoun nesne, çıkarılabilir.
3. "The company **(that)** I work for has 500 employees." — Contact clause.
4. "She's the most talented person **that** I've ever met." — Superlative → "that" tercih edilir.
5. "Everything **that** glitters is not gold." — Everything → "that"
6. "The reason **(why/that)** I came is simple." — "why" veya "that" veya çıkarma da mümkün.
7. "Find a place **where** you feel comfortable."

**Non-Defining:**

8. "My mother, **who** is 65, still runs every morning." — Annem hakkında ek bilgi.
9. "The Great Wall of China, **which** stretches over 13,000 miles, is visible from space." — (Tartışmalı gerçek, ama grammatically doğru örnek.)
10. "Last summer, **when** I visited Tokyo, I fell in love with the city."
11. "He donated all his money to charity, **which** shocked his family." — Tüm cümleye atıf.

**Whose / Where / When:**

12. "The family **whose** house burned down received community support."
13. "That's the restaurant **where** we had our first date."
14. "I'll never forget the moment **when** I heard the news."

**Quantifying:**

15. "I read five articles, **two of which** were directly relevant."
16. "She interviewed thirty applicants, **the best of whom** was a recent graduate."
