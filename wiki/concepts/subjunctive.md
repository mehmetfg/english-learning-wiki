# Subjunctive — Dilek Kipi

Subjunctive (dilek kipi), İngilizce'de talep, öneri, zorunluluk veya varsayımsal durumları ifade etmek için kullanılan özel bir fiil kipidir. Günlük konuşmada azalmış olsa da, formal yazı ve Amerikan İngilizcesi'nde hâlâ aktif kullanılır.

---

## Yapı / Structure

### Present Subjunctive (Geniş Zamanlı Dilek Kipi)

Fiilin **yalın hali** (base form) kullanılır. 3. tekil şahıs için **-s eklenmez** — bu, en sık yapılan hatadır.

```
Subject + that + Subject + BASE FORM (no -s)
```

| Normal (Indicative)       | Subjunctive                        |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------|
| He studies hard.          | I suggest that he **study** harder. |
| She takes the medicine.   | The doctor recommended that she **take** the medicine. |
| Every student attends.    | It is essential that every student **attend** the meeting. |

**Olumsuz hali:** `not` doğrudan fiilden önce gelir (yardımcı fiil kullanılmaz).
- "I insist that he **not be** punished." (NOT: "isn't punished")
- "She requested that we **not discuss** this matter."

### Past Subjunctive (Geçmiş Zamanlı Dilek Kipi)

Tüm şahıslar için **"were"** kullanılır (was değil — bu kritik bir kuraldır).

```
If I/he/she/it/we/they were + ...
I wish I/he/she/it + were + ...
```

| Yapı | Örnek |
|------|-------|
| Type 2 Conditional | "If I **were** you, I would apologize." |
| Wish | "I wish I **were** taller." |
| If only | "If only she **were** here right now." |
| As if / As though | "She acts as if she **were** the boss." |
| It's time | "It's time we **were** leaving." |
| Would rather | "I'd rather you **were** honest with me." |

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### 1. Present Subjunctive Tetikleyen Fiil ve İfadeler

**Fiiller:**
- suggest, recommend, insist, demand, require, propose, request, urge, ask, command, order, advise, move (resmi toplantılarda)

**Kalıplar (It is + adjective + that):**
- it is important / necessary / essential / vital / crucial / imperative / advisable / desirable / fitting / mandatory / compulsory + **that**

> "It is vital that the information **remain** confidential."
> "The committee moved that the proposal **be** accepted."
> "She urged that he **reconsider** his decision."

### 2. Past Subjunctive Tetikleyen Yapılar

| Yapı | Not |
|------|-----|
| If I were... | Type 2 Conditional |
| I wish (that)... | Gerçekleşmesi istenilen ama mümkün olmayan durum |
| If only... | Wish'in daha duygusal/dramatik versiyonu |
| As if / As though | Gerçeğe aykırı karşılaştırma |
| It's time (that)... | Yapılması gereken ama ertelenen şey |
| I'd rather / I'd sooner | Tercih, başkasının yapmasını isteme |
| Suppose / Supposing | Varsayım |

### 3. BrE vs AmE Farkı

| Amerikan İngilizcesi (formal) | Britanya İngilizcesi (yaygın) |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| I suggest that he **study**. | I suggest that he **should study**. |
| It is important that she **be** told. | It is important that she **should be** told. |

AmE subjunctive'i tutarlı kullanır. BrE'de "should + infinitive" alternatif olarak kabul edilir, ancak formal BrE de subjunctive'e döner.

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### Hata 1: 3. Tekil Şahısa -s Eklemek
- ❌ "I suggest that he **studies** harder."
- ✓ "I suggest that he **study** harder."

### Hata 2: Indicative Kullanmak (was vs were)
- ❌ "If I **was** you, I would leave."
- ✓ "If I **were** you, I would leave."
- Not: "was" konuşma dilinde duyulabilir ama formal bağlamda yanlıştır.

### Hata 3: Demand/Insist/Suggest Sonrası Indicative
- ❌ "She demanded that he **leaves** immediately."
- ✓ "She demanded that he **leave** immediately."

### Hata 4: Olumsuzda "isn't/doesn't" Kullanmak
- ❌ "I insist that he **isn't** punished."
- ✓ "I insist that he **not be** punished."

### Özel Yapı: Inverted Subjunctive
Formal/edebi İngilizce'de "if" düşürülüp özne-yardımcı fiil yer değiştirir:
- "**Were it not for** your help, I would have failed." = "If it were not for your help..."
- "**Should you need** assistance, call me." = "If you should need assistance..."
- "**Had I known**, I would have come." = "If I had known..."

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "The manager **insisted** that every report **be** submitted by Friday."
   → "insist" + that = present subjunctive, "be" (not "is")

2. "It is **crucial** that he **not miss** the interview."
   → "not" doğrudan fiilden önce

3. "I **wish** I **were** better at mathematics."
   → wish + past subjunctive (were, not was)

4. "She speaks as if she **were** a native speaker."
   → as if + past subjunctive

5. "The doctor **recommended** that she **rest** for a week."
   → recommend + that = subjunctive (no -s, no should)

6. "It's **time** the government **took** action on this issue."
   → It's time + past subjunctive (took)

7. "I'd **rather** you **didn't** tell anyone about this."
   → would rather + past tense (not subjunctive here — past tense)

8. "The judge **ordered** that the defendant **remain** in custody."
   → order + that = present subjunctive

9. "**Were I** to win the lottery, I'd give half to charity."
   → Inverted conditional (formal)

10. "It is **essential** that all passengers **fasten** their seatbelts."
    → essential + that + base form

11. "She **proposed** that the meeting **be** postponed until next week."
    → propose + that = subjunctive

12. "If only I **were** ten years younger!"
    → if only + were (duygusal pişmanlık)

13. "He acted as though he **were** the only person in the room."
    → as though + were

14. "The contract **requires** that the buyer **provide** three references."
    → require + that = subjunctive

## Sabit İfadeler / Fixed Expressions

Subjunctive'in kalıplaşmış halde kaldığı deyimler:

| İfade | Anlam |
|-------|-------|
| So be it. | Öyle olsun. (kabullenme) |
| Be that as it may. | Öyle de olsa. (karşı argüman) |
| Come what may. | Her ne olursa olsun. |
| God save the King/Queen. | Tanrı Kral'ı/Kraliçe'yi korusun. |
| Long live the Queen! | Kraliçe yaşasın! |
| Heaven forbid! | Allah korusun! |
| Suffice it to say... | Şunu söylemek yeterli... |
| Far be it from me to... | Benim gibi biri için yersiz olur ama... |
| As it were. | Bir bakıma, tabiri caizse. |
| If this be true... | Eğer bu doğruysa... (arkaik) |
