# Passive Infinitives and Gerunds — Edilgen Mastar ve Ortaç

Edilgen (passive) yapılar yalnızca tam cümlelerle değil, **mastar (infinitive)** ve **-ing (gerund)** formlarıyla da oluşturulabilir. Bu yapılar, bir eylemin öznesi değil nesnesi konumundaki varlıkları merkezde tutar.

---

## Yapı / Structure

| Form | Yapı | Anlam |
|------|------|-------|
| Passive Infinitive (şimdi) | **to be + V3** | to be done, to be told |
| Passive Gerund (şimdi) | **being + V3** | being ignored, being seen |
| Passive Perfect Infinitive | **to have been + V3** | to have been warned |
| Passive Perfect Gerund | **having been + V3** | having been introduced |

---

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### 1. Passive Infinitive — "to be + V3"

Bir eylem henüz gerçekleşmemiş ya da gerçekleşmesi bekleniyor. Fiil ya da sıfatın ardından gelir.

**Yaygın bağlamlar:**

| Yapı | Örnek |
|------|-------|
| want / expect / need / hope + passive inf. | "She wants **to be promoted**." |
| modal + be + V3 | "This must **be done** today." / "The problem can **be solved**." |
| adjective + passive inf. | "It is difficult **to be understood**." |
| there is/are + passive inf. | "There is much **to be done**." |
| too...to yapısı | "It was too dark **to be seen**." |
| ready/available/free + passive inf. | "The food is ready **to be served**." |

> "The report needs **to be submitted** by Friday."
> "She expects **to be informed** about any changes."
> "The package is **to be delivered** by tomorrow."
> "There's nothing **to be worried** about."
> "The results are yet **to be confirmed**."

---

### 2. Passive Gerund — "being + V3"

Bir eylemin öznesi değil nesnesi olan -ing yapısı. Genellikle **fiil + preposition** ya da **preposition + gerund** yapılarında görülür.

**Yaygın bağlamlar:**

| Yapı | Örnek |
|------|-------|
| hate/love/enjoy/mind/dislike + passive gerund | "I hate **being ignored**." |
| avoid/resist/deny + passive gerund | "She avoided **being seen** near the building." |
| preposition + passive gerund | "After **being introduced**, they shook hands." |
| noun phrase + passive gerund | "The thought of **being rejected** terrified her." |
| can't stand/don't mind + passive gerund | "He doesn't mind **being criticized** constructively." |

> "I hate **being treated** like a child."
> "She managed to escape without **being noticed**."
> "He was used to **being asked** difficult questions."
> "**Being invited** to the event was an honour."
> "Despite **being warned**, he continued."

---

### 3. Passive Perfect Infinitive — "to have been + V3"

Geçmişte gerçekleşmiş (ya da gerçekleşmemiş) bir eylemi gösterir. Şimdiki an ile geçmiş eylem arasındaki ilişkiyi kurar.

| Yapı | Örnek |
|------|-------|
| seem/appear/believe/claim + passive perfect inf. | "She seems **to have been warned**." |
| passive + perfect combined | "The report is alleged **to have been falsified**." |
| should/ought/must + have been + V3 | "This should **have been done** earlier." |

> "He is believed **to have been murdered** in 1943."
> "The documents are thought **to have been destroyed**."
> "She claims **to have been cheated** out of her money."
> "The treasure is said **to have been hidden** here centuries ago."

---

### 4. Passive Perfect Gerund — "having been + V3"

Ana eylemden önce tamamlanmış bir edilgen eylemi ifade eder.

> "**Having been told** about the problem, she acted immediately."
> "**Having been rejected** twice, he gave up applying."
> "I'm glad of **having been chosen** for this role."
> "**Having been informed** of the delay, passengers rebooked their flights."

---

### 5. Reporting Structures — Raporlama Yapıları

Haber ve resmi dilde sık kullanılan bu yapı, belirsiz ya da genel bir özneyi (people, experts, reports...) gizler.

**İki temel kalıp:**

**Kalıp A:** `It + passive + that + clause`
> "**It is said that** she is innocent."
> "**It is believed that** the economy will recover."
> "**It is reported that** he has left the country."

**Kalıp B:** `Subject + passive + to-infinitive`
> "She **is said to be** innocent." (= People say she is innocent — şimdi)
> "He **is believed to have left** the country." (= People believe he left — geçmiş)
> "They **are thought to have been hiding**." (= People think they have been hiding)

**Geçmiş zaman için:**
> "The policy **was said to have failed**." (geçmişte söyleniyordu)
> "She **was believed to be** living abroad."

**Bu yapıda kullanılan fiiller:**
say, think, believe, report, know, consider, claim, allege, expect, suppose, understand, assume, estimate, find, regard

| Kalıp A | Kalıp B (Şimdi) | Kalıp B (Geçmiş) |
|---------|-----------------|------------------|
| It is said that she lives here. | She is said to live here. | She is said to have lived here. |
| It is believed that he is hiding. | He is believed to be hiding. | He is believed to have been hiding. |
| It is reported that they fled. | They are reported to have fled. | — |

---

### 6. NEED / WANT / REQUIRE + Gerund (Edilgen Anlam)

Bu yapıda gerund, **edilgen anlam** taşır — nesnenin bir şeyin yapılmasına ihtiyacı vardır:

```
[Özne: etkilenen nesne] + need/want/require + V-ing
```

> "The car **needs washing**." = "The car needs **to be washed**."
> "This shirt **needs ironing**." = "This shirt needs **to be ironed**."
> "The garden **wants weeding**." = "The garden needs **to be weeded**."
> "Your hair **needs cutting**." = "Your hair needs **to be cut**."
> "The report **requires editing** before submission."

**Not:** Bu yapıda gerundun öznel anlamı değil, nesnel (edilgen) anlamı vardır. "The car needs washing" ifadesinde araba yıkayan değil, yıkanması gereken nesnedir.

---

### 7. Passive Infinitive — Diğer Kalıplar

**"There is + to be + V3":**
> "There is nothing more **to be said**."
> "There is still work **to be done**."

**Sıfat öbeğinde:**
> "a problem **hard to be solved**" (resmi/edebi) → günlük dilde: "a hard problem to solve"
> "questions **impossible to be answered**" → daha doğal: "impossible-to-answer questions"

---

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### Algı Fiilleri + Passive (Perception Verbs)

Aktif yapıda algı fiilleri bare infinitive alır:
> "I **heard** him **say** her name." (aktif — bare infinitive)

Edilgen yapıya geçince "to" eklenir:
> "He was **heard to say** her name." (edilgen — to + infinitive)
> "She was **seen to enter** the building at midnight."
> "They were **observed to behave** strangely."

Bu kurala uyan fiiller: hear, see, watch, notice, feel (algı fiilleri)

### "be supposed to" — Modal Benzeri Yapı

> "It's **supposed to be** finished by now." (tamamlanmış olması gerekiyordu)
> "She's **supposed to be** the best in her field." (en iyi olduğu söylenir)
> "The meeting was **supposed to start** at 9." (9'da başlaması gerekiyordu ama başlamadı)

Bu yapı hem edilgen hem de "expected/required" anlamı taşır.

### "The house is to let" — Sabit İfade

"To let" = "for rent" anlamında İngilizce'deki yerleşik ifade:
> "Flat **to let**." / "Rooms **to let**."

Benzer sabit ifadeler: "for sale", "for hire"

### "be said to" — aktif vs edilgen karşılaştırma

| Aktif | Edilgen |
|-------|---------|
| "People say she is talented." | "She **is said to be** talented." |
| "They reported that he had escaped." | "He **was reported to have escaped**." |
| "Experts believe prices will rise." | "Prices **are believed to be** rising." |

### Passive Infinitive vs Passive Gerund seçimi

Hangi form kullanılacağı, **ana fiil ya da preposition**'a bağlıdır:

| Ana fiil/yapı | Form |
|---------------|------|
| want, need, expect, hope | passive infinitive (to be V3) |
| avoid, mind, enjoy, hate | passive gerund (being V3) |
| after, before, without (preposition) | passive gerund |
| modal verbs | passive infinitive (be V3, no "to") |

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "The report **needs to be submitted** before the deadline." — Passive infinitive.
2. "She hoped **to be selected** for the national team." — Passive infinitive.
3. "**Being ignored** in meetings is deeply frustrating." — Passive gerund as subject.
4. "He avoided **being photographed** at the event." — Passive gerund after verb.
5. "After **being introduced**, the speakers took their seats." — Passive gerund after preposition.
6. "The contract **is said to have been signed** in secret." — Reporting structure, perfect.
7. "She **is believed to be** living somewhere in the south of France." — Reporting structure, present.
8. "The ancient text **is thought to have been written** in the 9th century." — Perfect passive infinitive.
9. "**Having been warned** about the risks, she proceeded with caution." — Perfect passive gerund.
10. "The windows **need cleaning** — they're covered in dust." — need + gerund (passive meaning).
11. "The bike **wants fixing** before you can ride it." — want + gerund (passive meaning).
12. "He was **heard to mutter** something under his breath." — Perception verb, passive.
13. "The ceremony is **supposed to begin** at noon." — be supposed to.
14. "There's still a lot of work **to be done** before we can launch." — There is + to be + V3.
15. "**Being promoted** so quickly was both exciting and daunting." — Passive gerund as subject.
