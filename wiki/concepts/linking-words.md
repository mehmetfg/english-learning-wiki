# Linking Words — Bağlayıcı İfadeler

**Linking words** (bağlayıcı ifadeler), aynı zamanda *discourse markers*, *cohesive devices* ya da *transition words* olarak da bilinir. Cümleler ve paragraflar arasındaki mantıksal ilişkiyi kurar ve metni akıcı, tutarlı hale getirir.

**Bağlaçlardan (conjunctions) temel farkı:**
Bağlaçlar (and, but, because, although) iki cümleyi gramatik olarak doğrudan birleştirir. Bağlayıcı ifadelerin çoğu ise **zarftır (adverb)** ve iki ayrı cümleyi anlam bakımından bağlar; noktalama işaretlerine dikkat edilmesi gerekir.

---

## Yapı / Structure

```
[Cümle]. [Bağlayıcı ifade], [cümle].
[Cümle]; [bağlayıcı ifade], [cümle].
```

> "She was tired. **However**, she kept working." ✓
> "She was tired; **however**, she kept working." ✓
> ❌ "She was tired, however she kept working." — YANLIŞ (comma splice)

**Cümle ortasında (mid-sentence):**
> "She **therefore** decided to resign." (virgül gereksiz)
> "This is, **however**, not always the case."

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### EKLEME (Addition)

Ek bilgi, destek ya da benzer fikir ekler.

| İfade | Kullanım/Ton |
|-------|-------------|
| furthermore | resmi yazı, güçlü ekleme |
| moreover | resmi yazı, bir öncekinden daha güçlü |
| in addition (to this/that) | nötr-resmi |
| additionally | nötr-resmi |
| besides | yarı resmi, "üstelik" |
| also | nötr; cümle ortasına dikkat! |
| as well (as) | nötr; cümle sonunda veya isimle |
| not only...but also | vurgulu çift ekleme |
| what is more | konuşma diline daha yakın |
| on top of that | informal, "üstüne bir de" |
| equally | benzer ağırlıkta ek bilgi |
| similarly / likewise | aynı şekilde, benzer biçimde |

> "She is intelligent. **Furthermore**, she works extremely hard."
> "**In addition to** her job, she volunteers at a shelter."
> "He is an excellent teacher; **moreover**, he genuinely cares about his students."
> "**Not only** did she win the race, **but** she **also** broke the record."

**"ALSO" konumu:**
- Yardımcı fiilden sonra, ana fiilden önce: "She **is also** tired." / "I **have also** heard this."
- "Be"den sonra: "She **is also** a writer."
- ❌ "Also she is tired." — cümle başında "also" informal/hatalı

### KARŞITLIK / KABUL (Contrast / Concession)

Zıt ya da beklenmedik bilgi sunar.

| İfade | Kullanım |
|-------|---------|
| however | en yaygın, nötr-resmi |
| nevertheless | daha güçlü, "buna rağmen" |
| nonetheless | nevertheless ile aynı, biraz daha resmi |
| even so | "yine de, buna rağmen" (konuşma + yazı) |
| yet | "yine de" (resmi/edebi) |
| still | "yine de, hâlâ" (cümle ortasında) |
| on the other hand | farklı bir perspektif |
| in contrast | net zıtlık |
| by/in comparison | karşılaştırarak |
| conversely | tam tersi, tersine |
| on the contrary | tam aksine (karşı iddianın yanlışlığı) |
| having said that / that said | "bunu söyledikten sonra..." |
| be that as it may | "her ne olursa olsun" (resmi) |
| despite / in spite of (this/that) | buna rağmen + isim/-ing |
| all the same | yine de, ne olursa olsun |
| regardless | ne olursa olsun |

> "She studied hard. **However**, she failed the exam."
> "It was expensive. **Nevertheless**, we bought it."
> "He admitted his mistake. **Even so**, the damage was done."

**"on the contrary" vs "on the other hand":**

| İfade | Anlam | Örnek |
|-------|-------|-------|
| **on the contrary** | tam tersi doğrudur | "A: Was it boring? B: On the contrary, it was fascinating!" |
| **on the other hand** | farklı bir bakış açısı/taraf | "This job pays well; on the other hand, the hours are long." |

### SEBEP / NEDEN (Cause / Reason)

Bir sonuca yol açan nedeni gösterir.

| İfade | Yapı | Örnek |
|-------|------|-------|
| because of (this/that) | + isim | "Because of the rain, we stayed home." |
| as a result of | + isim | "As a result of the delay, we missed the connection." |
| due to | + isim/-ing | "Due to heavy traffic, we were late." |
| owing to | + isim/-ing (resmi) | "Owing to illness, the event was cancelled." |
| thanks to | + isim (olumlu) | "Thanks to her help, we finished on time." |
| on account of | + isim (resmi) | "On account of the bad weather, flights were grounded." |
| for this/that reason | genel sebep bağlantısı | "The road was icy. For this reason, we drove slowly." |
| the reason is that | açıklama | "The reason is that funding has been cut." |

**UYARI — "due to" yapısı:**
"due to" + isim/gerund gelir; yanına cümle (clause) gelmez:
> ✓ "Due to heavy traffic, we were late."
> ❌ "Due to we were stuck in traffic, we were late."
> ✓ "Because we were stuck in traffic, we were late." (clause için "because" kullan)

**"because of" vs "because":**
- because of + isim/gerund
- because + cümle (clause)

### SONUÇ / ETKİ (Effect / Result)

Bir eylem ya da durumun sonucunu gösterir.

| İfade | Ton | Örnek |
|-------|-----|-------|
| therefore | resmi-nötr | "The deadline passed. **Therefore**, the project was cancelled." |
| consequently | resmi | "She missed the flight; **consequently**, she lost the deal." |
| as a result | nötr | "He didn't study. **As a result**, he failed." |
| as a consequence | resmi | "**As a consequence**, the company went bankrupt." |
| hence | resmi/yazı | "The road was flooded; **hence** the delay." |
| thus | çok resmi/yazı | "The data was incomplete; **thus** no conclusions could be drawn." |
| so | informal | "It was late, **so** we left." (bağlaç olarak da kullanılır) |
| for this reason | nötr | "I was ill. **For this reason**, I stayed home." |
| this means that | açıklama | "Prices have risen. **This means that** consumers will spend less." |
| this leads to | zincir sonuç | "Stress **leads to** poor sleep, which in turn affects performance." |

### ÖRNEKLEME (Exemplification)

Genel bir ifadeyi somutlaştırır.

| İfade | Kullanım | Örnek |
|-------|---------|-------|
| for example (e.g.) | en yaygın | "Some fruits, **for example**, oranges, are rich in vitamin C." |
| for instance | for example ile aynı | "**For instance**, you could try meditation." |
| such as | liste için (virgülsüz) | "fruits **such as** apples, pears, and bananas" |
| like (informal) | günlük dil | "sports **like** football and basketball" |
| namely | özellikle belirtmek için | "Two cities, **namely** London and Paris, were selected." |
| in particular / particularly | özellikle | "I love jazz, **in particular** Miles Davis." |
| specifically | özellikle belirterek | "The study focuses **specifically** on teenage behavior." |
| to illustrate | açıklamak için | "**To illustrate**, consider the following case." |
| a case in point is | somut örnek | "**A case in point is** the 2008 financial crisis." |
| take...for example | Take the UK for example |

**"such as" ve "like" örnek verir, "for example" VERMEZ:**
> ✓ "fruits such as apples and bananas"
> ❌ "fruits for example apples" — "for example" sonrasına virgül gelir ve cümle bağlar

| İfade | Örnek |
|-------|-------|
| above all | "She is talented. **Above all**, she is dedicated." |

| indeed | "This is **indeed** a serious problem." |

| what is particularly striking is | "**What is particularly striking is** the speed of change." |

| İfade | Kullanım |
|-------|---------|

| to conclude | daha az resmi |
| to sum up | sunum ve konuşmada |

| in short / in brief | kısa özetle |
| to summarize | akademik |
| overall | genel değerlendirme |
| all in all | genel değerlendirme (nötr) |
| on the whole | genel olarak |

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### Noktalama — En Kritik Konu

Bağlayıcı ifadeler (however, therefore, nevertheless...) bağımsız cümleleri birleştirmek için kullanıldığında **virgülle bağlanamaz** — bu **comma splice** hatasıdır:

| Hata (Comma Splice) | Doğru |
|---------------------|-------|
| ❌ "She was tired, however she kept working." | ✓ "She was tired. However, she kept working." |
| ❌ "He was late, therefore we started without him." | ✓ "He was late; therefore, we started without him." |
| ❌ "It was cold, nevertheless they swam." | ✓ "It was cold. Nevertheless, they swam." |

### Cümle Ortasında Bağlayıcı İfade

"Therefore", "however", "nonetheless" gibi ifadeler cümle ortasında da kullanılabilir — virgül gereksinimi değişir:

> "She was tired. She **therefore** decided to rest." (virgülsüz, akıcı)
> "This is, **however**, not always the case." (virgül içinde)
> "He **nevertheless** continued working." (virgülsüz)

### "however" — bağlaç değil, zarf

"However" bir bağımsız bağlaç (conjunction) değildir; iki cümleyi gramatik olarak birleştiremez. "But/although" bunun için kullanılır:
> ✓ "It was expensive **but** I bought it." (bağlaç olarak but)
> ✓ "It was expensive. **However**, I bought it." (zarf olarak however — iki cümle)

### "for example" kullanım yeri

"for example" + örnek cümle gelir; isim listesinin yanına değil cümle başına ya da virgülle alınmış ayrı bir cümleye eklenir. Örneklerin yanına "such as" kullanılır:
> ✓ "Some languages, **such as** Japanese and Arabic, use different scripts."
> ✓ "Some languages are very different from English. **For example**, Japanese uses three writing systems."
> ❌ "Some languages **for example** Japanese and Arabic..." — hatalı kullanım

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "The project was challenging. **Furthermore**, we had very little time." — Ekleme.
2. "She speaks three languages. **Moreover**, she's learning a fourth." — Güçlü ekleme.
3. "He worked hard. **However**, he didn't get the promotion." — Karşıtlık.
4. "The review was negative. **Nevertheless**, the film broke box office records." — Kabul.
5. "**Due to** the storm, all flights were cancelled." — Due to + isim.
6. "The road was wet. **As a result**, the car skidded." — Sonuç.
7. "**Therefore**, further investigation is required before any conclusions can be drawn." — Resmi sonuç.
8. "Some animals, **such as** dolphins and elephants, show signs of self-awareness." — Örnekleme.
9. "**For instance**, you could start with just ten minutes of exercise a day." — Örnekleme.
10. "He is talented. **Above all**, he has the determination to succeed." — Vurgu.
11. "**In fact**, the problem is far more complex than it first appears." — Vurgu.
12. "**Firstly**, define the problem. **Then**, brainstorm solutions. **Finally**, implement the best one." — Sıralama.
13. "The economy was growing. **Meanwhile**, unemployment continued to rise." — Zaman ilişkisi.
14. "**In conclusion**, the evidence strongly supports the hypothesis." — Sonuç/özet.
15. "**All in all**, it was a productive and enjoyable conference." — Genel değerlendirme.
16. "A: Wasn't the concert boring? B: **On the contrary**, it was one of the best I've ever attended!" — On the contrary.
17. "The salary is good; **on the other hand**, the commute is exhausting." — On the other hand.
18. "It was cold and rainy. **Even so**, hundreds of people showed up." — Even so.
