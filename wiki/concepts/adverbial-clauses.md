# Adverbial Clauses — Zarf Cümleleri

Adverbial clause (zarf cümlesi), ana fiili, sıfatı ya da zarfı niteleyen, "ne zaman / nerede / neden / nasıl / ne ölçüde" sorularına yanıt veren bağımlı bir cümleciktir. Bir bağlaç (conjunction) ile başlar ve genellikle ana cümlenin önüne ya da arkasına eklenir.

**Türleri:** Zaman, neden, amaç, sonuç, kıyaslama, koşul, yer, tarz ve karşıtlık cümleleri.

---

## Yapı / Structure

| Tür | Bağlaç Örnekleri |
|---|---|
| Zaman (Time) | when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as, by the time, once, whenever, the moment |
| Neden (Reason) | because, since, as, for, seeing that, in that, now that |
| Amaç (Purpose) | so that, in order that, to, in order to, so as to, for fear that, in case |
| Sonuç (Result) | so...that, such...that |
| Karşıtlık (Concession) | although, though, even though, while, whereas, despite, in spite of |
| Koşul (Condition) | if, unless, provided that, as long as, on condition that |
| Tarz (Manner) | as if, as though, as, the way |
| Yer (Place) | where, wherever, everywhere |
| Kıyaslama (Comparison) | than, as...as |

---

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### 1. Zaman Cümleleri (Time Clauses)

Bağlaçlar: **when, while, as, before, after, since, until/till, as soon as, by the time, once, whenever, the moment (that)**

**KRİTİK KURAL — Gelecek Zaman:** Zaman cümleciklerinde gelecek anlamı Simple Present veya Present Perfect ile verilir. "will" KULLANILMAZ:

- "I'll call you **when I arrive**." ✓ (NOT: ~~when I will arrive~~ ✗)
- "**As soon as she finishes**, we'll start." ✓
- "**Once you've read** the report, we can discuss it." ✓ (Present Perfect)
- "**By the time you get** there, I'll have left." ✓ (Simple Present → gelecek)

**Fark: as / when / while**

| Bağlaç | Kullanım | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **as** | İki eylem aynı anda gelişir (process) | "**As** I was leaving, I saw him." |
| **when** | O anda, belirli bir noktada | "**When** I arrived, she was crying." |
| **while** | Uzun bir süre boyunca | "**While** I was sleeping, you called." |

**Diğer zaman bağlaçları:**
- "**Before** you leave, turn off the lights."
- "**After** finishing dinner, we watched a film."
- "**Since** moving to London, she's been much happier." (= since she moved)
- "Wait **until/till** I call you."
- "I'll help you **whenever** you need it."
- "**The moment (that)** I saw her, I knew something was wrong."
- "**No sooner** had she left **than** it started raining." (inversion — bkz. [[inversion]])

---

### 2. Neden/Gerekçe Cümleleri (Reason/Cause Clauses)

Bağlaçlar: **because, since, as, for, seeing that, in that, now that**

**Güç sırası:**
- **Because** — en güçlü, doğrudan neden: "She was late **because** the traffic was heavy."
- **Since/As** — nedenin zaten bilindiği varsayılır, daha yumuşak: "**Since** you're already here, you might as well stay."
- **For** — çok resmi/yazılı, nadiren kullanılır: "He could not sleep, **for** his mind was troubled."
- **Now that** — değişen duruma bağlı neden: "**Now that** she's gone, I feel lonely."
- **Seeing that** — göre/madem ki: "**Seeing that** no one objects, we'll proceed."
- **In that** — resmi, "şu açıdan": "The plan is flawed **in that** it ignores local conditions."

**DİKKAT:** "Because" parçası tek başına cümle olamaz:
- "She stayed home. **Because she was sick.**" ✗ (fragment — bağımlı cümle)
- "She stayed home **because she was sick**." ✓

---

### 3. Amaç Cümleleri (Purpose Clauses)

**Olumlu amaç:**
- "She studies **so that** she can get a good grade."
- "He left early **in order to** catch the first train."
- "I'll write it down **so as not to** forget." (olumsuz: so as not to)
- "She spoke slowly **in order that** everyone could understand."

**Olumsuz amaç:**
- "Speak quietly **so as not to** wake the baby."
- "She covered the window **for fear that** someone might see in."
- "Take an umbrella **in case** it rains."

**"In case" vs "If" — ÖNEMLİ FARK:**
- "**In case**" = önlem almak, önceden hazırlık: "I'll take my phone **in case** there's an emergency." (acil durum olursa diye — şu anda hazırlanıyorum)
- "**If**" = koşul: "I'll use my phone **if** there's an emergency." (acil durum olunca kullanacağım)
- In case → gelecekte "will" kullanılmaz: "I'll take water **in case we get** thirsty." ✓ (NOT: ~~in case we will get~~)

---

### 4. Sonuç Cümleleri (Result Clauses)

**so + sıfat/zarf + that:**
- "He was **so tired that** he fell asleep at the table."
- "She ran **so fast that** nobody could catch her."
- "It was **so cold that** the pipes froze."

**such + (a/an) + sıfat + isim + that:**
- "It was **such a boring film that** I left halfway through."
- "She has **such a beautiful voice that** everyone stops to listen."
- "There was **such heavy traffic that** we were two hours late."

**Fark: so vs such**
- "so" + sıfat/zarf
- "such" + isim grubu
- "so many/much/few/little" + isim de mümkün: "There were **so many people that** I couldn't move."

---

### 5. Karşıtlık/Taviz Cümleleri (Concession/Contrast Clauses)

**Clause ile:**
- "**Although/Though/Even though** she was tired, she kept working."
- "She kept working, **though** it was exhausting." (though cümle ortasına ve sonuna gelebilir)
- "**While/Whereas** he is outgoing, his brother is very shy." (karşıtlık)

**Phrase ile (isim/-ing):**
- "**Despite/In spite of** her tiredness, she kept working."
- "**Despite/In spite of** being tired, she kept working."
- "Despite the bad weather, the event continued."
- ✗ "Despite that she was tired" — informal/hatalı; "despite the fact that she was tired" ✓

**Sonuçtan önce gelen karşıtlık (bağımsız cümlelerde):**
- "She was tired; **however**, she kept working."
- "He studied hard. **Nevertheless**, he failed the exam."
- "The plan was risky; **nonetheless**, we proceeded."
- "It rained all day. **Even so**, we enjoyed the picnic."

---

### 6. Koşul Cümleleri (Condition Clauses)

→ Ayrıntılar için Conditionals sayfasına bakınız.

Temel bağlaçlar: **if, unless, provided (that), as long as, on condition that, supposing, assuming**

- "**If** it rains, we'll cancel the trip."
- "**Unless** you study, you won't pass." (= if you don't study)
- "You can borrow it **provided (that)** you return it."
- "**As long as** you're careful, it should be fine."

---

### 7. Tarz Cümleleri (Manner Clauses)

**as if / as though** — gerçek olmayan/varsayımsal karşılaştırma:
- "She talks **as if she knows** everything." (şimdiki/daha az kesin — belki gerçekten biliyor)
- "She talked **as if she had met** him before." (geçmiş — daha hipotetik, muhtemelen tanımıyor)
- "He spends money **as though he were** a millionaire." (were — subjunctive, kesinlikle değil)

**the way / as:**
- "Do it **the way** I showed you."
- "Do it **as** I showed you." (= in the manner that)

---

### 8. Yer Cümleleri (Place Clauses)

- "**Wherever** you go, I'll find you."
- "You can sit **where** you like."
- "She put the key **where** she could easily find it."
- "**Everywhere** she went, she made friends."

---

### 9. Kıyaslama Cümleleri (Comparison Clauses)

**than:**
- "She runs faster **than I do**." (tam cümle — formal) / "...faster **than me**." (informal)
- "The situation is worse **than we expected**."

**as...as:**
- "He's not **as tall as** his brother."
- "She works **as hard as** anyone I know."
- "The film wasn't **as good as** the book."

---

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### 1. Gelecek Zaman Cümlelerinde "will" Kullanımı

En yaygın hata: zaman ve koşul cümleciklerinde "will" kullanmak.

- "I'll call you **when I arrive**." ✓ NOT ~~when I will arrive~~ ✗
- "**As soon as she finishes**, we can go." ✓
- "We'll start **once you've decided**." ✓ (Present Perfect da kullanılabilir)

### 2. "Although" vs "Despite" — Clause mı, Phrase mi?

- "**Although** she worked hard, she failed." ✓ (clause — fiil var)
- "**Despite** working hard, she failed." ✓ (phrase — -ing)
- "**Despite the fact that** she worked hard, she failed." ✓ (fact that → clause)
- ~~"Despite she worked hard..."~~ ✗

### 3. Ters Çevrilmiş Karşıtlık (Inverted Concession)

- "**Though tired**, she kept working." = participle clause gibi işlev görür
- "**However tired she is**, she always smiles." — however + sıfat + özne + fiil sırası

### 4. "Be that as it may" — Sabit Karşıtlık İfadesi

- "**Be that as it may**, we still need to find a solution." (= Even if that is true / Öyle olsa bile)

### 5. "Since" — İki Farklı Anlam

- Zaman: "I haven't seen her **since** she moved." (o tarihten beri)
- Neden: "**Since** you're here, let me explain." (madem ki)

Bağlam genellikle anlamı netleştirir; ama belirsizse "because" veya "ever since" tercih edilir.

### 6. "Whereas" ve "While" — Karşıtlık mı, Zaman mı?

- "**While** she studied, he played games." — zaman (aynı anda)
- "**While/Whereas** she is hardworking, he is lazy." — karşıtlık
- Belirsizlik riski varsa "whereas" tercih edilir.

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "**When you get** to the station, call me." — zaman, gelecekte Simple Present ✓
2. "**By the time she arrives**, I'll have cooked dinner." — by the time + Simple Present
3. "**Since** nobody volunteered, I did it myself." — neden
4. "She took notes **so that** she wouldn't forget anything." — amaç
5. "Take an umbrella **in case** it rains." — önlem (in case)
6. "He was **so nervous that** he couldn't speak." — sonuç
7. "It was **such a difficult exam that** half the class failed." — such...that
8. "**Although** he tried his best, he couldn't win." — karşıtlık
9. "**Despite the cold weather**, they went hiking." — in spite of / despite
10. "She acts **as if she owns** the place." — tarz, as if
11. "**Wherever you are**, I'll support you." — yer
12. "She's not **as experienced as** her colleague." — kıyaslama
