# Indirect Questions — Dolaylı Sorular

Indirect questions (dolaylı sorular), bir soruyu doğrudan sormak yerine başka bir cümleye gömerek ifade etme yöntemidir. Doğrudan sorulara kıyasla daha kibar ve resmi bir ton yaratır. **En kritik kural: yardımcı fiil öne geçmez, normal cümle sırası kullanılır.**

---

## Yapı / Structure

### Temel Kural: Normal Word Order (Devrik Yok!)

```
Embedding phrase + question word / if / whether + SUBJECT + VERB (normal order)
```

| Doğrudan Soru (Devrik) | Dolaylı Soru (Normal Sıra) |
|------------------------|---------------------------|
| Where **does she live**? | I wonder where **she lives**. ✓ |
| What time **is it**? | Could you tell me what time **it is**? ✓ |
| When **did he leave**? | I don't know when **he left**. ✓ |
| How much **does it cost**? | Can you tell me how much **it costs**? ✓ |

**Yaygın hata:** devrik yapıyı korumak
- ❌ "Could you tell me what time **is it**?"
- ✓ "Could you tell me what time **it is**?"

---

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### 1. Gömme (Embedding) İfadeleri

**Soru biçiminde (? ile biter):**
- Could you tell me...?
- Do you know...?
- Can you tell me...?
- Would you mind telling me...?
- Do you have any idea...?
- Have you any idea...?

**İfade biçiminde (. ile biter):**
- I wonder...
- I'd like to know...
- I'm not sure...
- I have no idea...
- I'm trying to find out...
- It's unclear...
- I can't remember...
- I doubt...

### 2. Evet/Hayır Soruları → if veya whether

Bir sorunun doğrudan hali evet/hayır yanıtı gerektiriyorsa, dolaylı formda **if** veya **whether** kullanılır.

| Doğrudan | Dolaylı |
|----------|---------|
| "Is she coming?" | "I don't know **if/whether** she's coming." |
| "Did he call?" | "I'm not sure **if/whether** he called." |
| "Can you help?" | "She asked **if/whether** I could help." |

**whether vs if farkı:**
- İkisi çoğunlukla birbirinin yerine kullanılabilir
- **whether** daha resmidir
- Infinitive'den önce **sadece whether** kullanılır (if değil!):
  - ✓ "I can't decide **whether to go**."
  - ❌ "I can't decide **if to go**."
- Cümle başında **whether** kullanılır:
  - ✓ "**Whether or not** she comes doesn't matter."
  - ❌ "**If or not** she comes doesn't matter."

### 3. Tense Backshift (Zaman Kayması)

Giriş fiili geçmiş zamandaysa, dolaylı soru içindeki fiil de geçmişe kayar.

| Giriş Fiili | Doğrudan | Dolaylı |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| He asked... | "Where does she live?" | He asked where she **lived**. |
| She wondered... | "Can you come?" | She asked if I **could** come. |
| I wanted to know... | "Is he free?" | I wanted to know if he **was** free. |

**Giriş fiili şimdiki zamanda ise backshift gerekmez:**
- "I wonder where she **is**." (şu an gerçekten merak ediliyor — backshift yok)
- "I wonder where she **was** last night." (geçmiş zaman, değişmez)

### 4. Embedded Questions — Cümle İçine Gömülü Sorular

Bir ifadenin parçası olarak:
- "The question is **whether we can afford it**."
- "**What I'd like to know** is why he left."
- "The problem is **how we're going to pay for it**."
- "That depends on **what you mean by 'free'**."

---

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### Hata 1: Devrik Yapıyı Korumak
- ❌ "Do you know **what time is it**?"
- ✓ "Do you know **what time it is**?"

### Hata 2: if + infinitive
- ❌ "I couldn't decide **if to accept** the offer."
- ✓ "I couldn't decide **whether to accept** the offer."

### Hata 3: do/does/did'i Korumak
- ❌ "Can you tell me where **does she work**?"
- ✓ "Can you tell me where **she works**?"

### Hata 4: Yanlış Soru İşareti
- Giriş bir soru cümlesi ise ? koyulur, değilse nokta:
  - "I wonder where he is." (? yok — ifade)
  - "Do you know where he is?" (? var — soru)

### Özel Durum: "Tell me" Sonrası
- "Tell me + indirect question" = emir cümlesi, yardımcı fiil yok:
  - ✓ "Tell me where you were."
  - ❌ "Tell me where were you."

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "Could you tell me **where the nearest pharmacy is**?"
   → "Where is the nearest pharmacy?" → normal sıra, is sona

2. "I have no idea **what time the meeting starts**."
   → devrik değil: "what time it starts" değil "what time the meeting starts"

3. "Do you know **whether the museum is open on Sundays**?"
   → evet/hayır sorusu → whether kullanımı

4. "She asked me **if I had eaten** anything."
   → backshift: "have you eaten" → "had I eaten"

5. "I'm not sure **how long it will take**."
   → "How long will it take?" → normal sıra ile gömme

6. "He wanted to know **who had called** while he was out."
   → who + normal sıra + backshift (has called → had called)

7. "Can you tell me **why you were late**?"
   → devrik yok: "why were you late" ❌ → "why you were late" ✓

8. "I can't decide **whether to stay or go**."
   → infinitive öncesi: whether zorunlu

9. "**What I'd like to know** is how much the repairs will cost."
   → embedded question as subject

10. "The report didn't explain **why the project failed**."
    → embedded question in statement

11. "I wonder **if he's still angry** with me."
    → güncel merak → backshift yok

12. "She asked **whether the position was still available**."
    → whether + backshift (is → was)

13. "Please let me know **when you arrive**."
    → "when you arrive" (normal sıra)

14. "It's unclear **who is responsible** for this decision."
    → embedded question → normal sıra
