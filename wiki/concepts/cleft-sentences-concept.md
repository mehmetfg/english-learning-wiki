# Cleft Sentences — Yarık Cümleler

Cleft sentence (yarık cümle), basit bir cümleyi iki ayrı yan cümleye bölerek bir öğeyi ön plana çıkarır ve ona özel vurgu verir. "Cleft" kelimesi "bölünmüş, yarılmış" anlamına gelir — cümle adeta ikiye bölünür. Üç ana türü vardır: it-cleft, wh-cleft (pseudo-cleft) ve all-cleft.

**Temel Amaçlar:**
1. Bilgi yönetimi — eski bilgiyi yeni bilgiden ayırmak
2. Karşıtlık — "o değil, bu" vurgusu
3. Düzeltme — yanlış varsayımı düzeltmek

---

## Yapı / Structure

### 1. It-Cleft

**Yapı:** It + be + [vurgulanan öğe] + bağımlı cümle

Temel cümle: "John broke the window yesterday in the garden."

Bu cümledeki herhangi bir öğeyi vurgulayabiliriz:

| Vurgulanan Öğe | It-Cleft Cümlesi | Anlamı |
|---|---|---|
| **Özne (Subject)** | "It was **John** who broke the window." | John kırdı, başkası değil |
| **Nesne (Object)** | "It was **the window** that John broke." | Pencereyi, kapıyı değil |
| **Zaman (Time)** | "It was **yesterday** that John broke the window." | Dün, başka bir günde değil |
| **Yer (Place)** | "It was **in the garden** that John broke the window." | Bahçede, başka bir yerde değil |

**Bağımlı cümlede kullanılan bağlaçlar:**
- Kişiler için: **who** veya **that**
- Şeyler için: **which** veya **that**
- Zaman için: **when** veya **that**
- Yer için: **where** veya **that**
- Neden için: **why** veya **that**

**Soru Formları:**
- "**Was it John who** broke the window?"
- "**Who was it that** called?"
- "**What is it that** you want?"

**Zaman Uyumu:**
- "**It is** exercise **that** keeps her young." (genel gerçek — geniş zaman)
- "**It was** in 1969 **that** humans first walked on the moon." (geçmiş olay)
- "**It will be** next year **that** the project is completed." (gelecek)

---

### 2. Wh-Cleft (Pseudo-Cleft — Sözde Yarık Cümle)

**Yapı:** What + cümle + be + [vurgulanan öğe]

Vurgulanan öğe cümle SONUNA gelir. "What" burada "the thing that" anlamındadır.

- "**What I need** is a rest." (= The thing I need is a rest.)
- "**What he did** was (to) apologize." (= The thing he did was apologize.)
- "**What surprised me** was her reaction."
- "**What John did** was break the window." (vurgulanan eylem — bare infinitive)
- "**What she wants** is more time." (özne)
- "**What really bothers me** is his attitude."

**Ters Wh-Cleft (Reverse Pseudo-Cleft):**

Vurgulanan öğe cümle BAŞINA gelir — daha da güçlü vurgu:
- "**A rest** is what I need." (İhtiyacım olan şey bir dinlenmedir.)
- "**Her determination** is what made her succeed."
- "**More time** is what we need, not more pressure."

---

### 3. All-Cleft

**Yapı:** All + [cümle] + be + [vurgulanan öğe]

"All" = "the only thing" (tek istenen/yapılan şey) anlamıyla sınırlama ifade eder:

- "**All I want** is a cup of tea." (Tek istediğim bir fincan çay.)
- "**All she did** was (to) smile." (Tek yaptığı şey gülümsemekti.)
- "**All you need** is love."
- "**All I'm asking** is a little patience."
- "**All that remains** is to sign the contract."

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### Vurgu ve Bilgi Yönetimi

Cleft cümleler, konuşanın hangi bilgiyi önemli/yeni olarak sunduğunu gösterir. Dinleyicinin zihninde zaten var olan bilgiyi (old information) bağımlı cümleye, yeni/vurgulanan bilgiyi (new information) ön plana koyar.

- "It was **Paris** where they got married." — Paris bilgisi yeni/vurgulanan
- "It was **Maria** who organized everything." — Maria vurgulanıyor

### Karşıtlık ve Düzeltme

Yanlış bir varsayımı düzeltmek için çok etkili:

- "It wasn't the **car** that was stolen — it was the **bike**."
- "It was **her** who told me, not him." (O değil, o söyledi)
- "It's **respect**, not money, that he wants."

### Söylev Açıcısı (Discourse Opener)

Wh-cleft, özellikle "What's interesting is..." veya "What I find remarkable is..." kalıplarıyla konuşmada ve yazıda yaygındır:

- "**What's interesting about this case** is that nobody reported it."
- "**What I find remarkable** is how quickly she recovered."
- "**What happened next** was completely unexpected."

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### 1. Prepositional Phrase Vurgusu

It-cleft, preposition gruplarını da vurgulayabilir:
- "**It was with great sadness that** we heard the news."
- "**It was at midnight that** the alarm went off."
- "**It was out of respect that** I kept quiet."

### 2. Neden Vurgusu

- "**It is because she is tired** that she is irritable." (neden vurgulanıyor)
- "**It was because of the rain** that we cancelled."

### 3. "The thing is..." — İnformal/Kısaltılmış Yapı

Günlük konuşmada tam wh-cleft yerine kısaltılmış hali kullanılır:
- "**The thing is**, I don't really know." (= What the thing is, is that...)
- "**The problem is**, we've run out of time."
- "**The truth is**, she never liked him."

### 4. "It" Zamiriyle Karışma

It-cleft'teki "it" gerçek bir ismi temsil etmez — kurgusal öznedir:
- "**It** was John who did it." — buradaki "it" kurgusal (cleft yapısı)
- "**It** was raining." — buradaki "it" hava durumu için gerçek "it"

### 5. Vurgulanan Öğe Çoğul Olduğunda — Fiil Uyumu

- "**It is the students who ARE** responsible." (not: ~~is~~)
- "**It was the books that WERE** missing."
- Vurgulanan öğeye göre fiil çekimi yapılır.

### 6. It-Cleft Soru Formu

- "**Who was it that** called last night?"
- "**What was it that** you wanted to tell me?"
- "**Was it really John who** said that?"

### 7. Konuşma vs Yazı

- It-cleft, yazı dilinde daha sistemli kullanılır.
- Sözlü dilde aynı vurgu ses tonu (stress) ile sağlanır: "**JOHN** broke the window." (not: "It was John who...")
- Wh-cleft ise sözlü dilde de doğal gelir: "What I need is a break."

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

**It-Cleft:**
1. "**It was Shakespeare who** wrote 'Hamlet', not Marlowe." — özne vurgusu, düzeltme
2. "**It is hard work that** leads to success, not luck." — karşıtlık
3. "**It was only then that** I realised my mistake." — zaman vurgusu
4. "**It is in London that** our head office is based." — yer vurgusu
5. "**It was with great effort that** she climbed to the top." — prepositional phrase
6. "**It is because he cares** that he speaks honestly." — neden vurgusu

**Wh-Cleft:**
7. "**What we need** is a completely new approach." — nesne vurgusu
8. "**What bothers me** is the lack of communication."
9. "**What she did** was (to) resign without warning."
10. "**What's remarkable** is that no one was injured."

**Reverse Wh-Cleft:**
11. "**A new strategy** is what this company needs." — başa vurgu

**All-Cleft:**
12. "**All I ask** is that you be honest with me."
13. "**All he did** was shake his head and walk away."
