# Emphasis — Vurgu ve Pekiştirme

Emphasis (vurgu/pekiştirme), bir cümledeki bilginin önemini, duygusallığını veya gücünü artırmak için kullanılan çeşitli dilbilgisel stratejilerdir. Doğru vurgu hem konuşmada hem yazıda anlam nüansı yaratır.

---

## Yapı / Structure

### 1. DO/DOES/DID ile Olumlu Vurgu

Olumlu cümlelerde zaten var olan bir eylemin gerçekliğini ya da samimiyetini pekiştirmek için yardımcı fiil **do/does/did** eklenir. Yalnızca **olumlu** cümlelerde çalışır.

```
Subject + do/does/did + BASE FORM
```

| Normal | Vurgulu |
|--------|---------|
| "I like it." | "I **do** like it!" |
| "She tried." | "She **did** try!" |
| "He loves her." | "He **does** love her." |

**Emir cümlelerinde:**
- "**Do** come in!" (buyurun, gelin)
- "**Do** be careful!" (lütfen dikkatli ol)
- "**Do** sit down." (oturun)

**Önemli kısıtlama:** Zaten bir yardımcı fiil varsa "do" eklenmez:
- ❌ "I **do** am trying." (am zaten var)
- ✓ "I **am** really trying." (zarf ile vurgu)

### 2. Fronting — Öne Alma

Normalde cümlenin sonunda veya ortasında bulunan unsuru cümlenin başına almak vurgu yaratır.

| Normal Sıra | Fronted |
|-------------|---------|
| "I really like **this book**." | "**This book** I really like." |
| "She opened the door carefully." | "**Carefully** she opened the door." |
| "Anything is possible with patience." | "**With patience**, anything is possible." |
| "I've never seen such a mess." | "**Such a mess** I've never seen." |

**Negatif Fronting (Inversion gerektirir):**
Negatif bir unsur öne geldiğinde özne-yardımcı fiil yer değiştirir:
- "**Never have I** seen such bravery."
- "**Rarely does** he admit his mistakes."
- "**Not only did** she win, but she also broke the record."
- "**Under no circumstances** should you open that door."
- "**Hardly had** I arrived when the trouble started."

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### 3. Yoğunlaştırıcılar / Intensifiers

**Gradable adjectives (derecelendirilebilir sıfatlar) için:**

| Yoğunlaştırıcı | Seviye | Örnek |
|----------------|--------|-------|
| slightly | çok hafif | "slightly tired" |
| fairly | orta | "fairly good" |
| rather | orta-güçlü | "rather impressive" |
| pretty (informal) | orta | "pretty good" |
| quite | iyi ölçüde | "quite interesting" |
| very | güçlü | "very tired" |
| extremely | çok güçlü | "extremely difficult" |
| incredibly | çok güçlü | "incredibly beautiful" |

**Non-gradable / Absolute adjectives için:**

| Yoğunlaştırıcı | Kullanım |
|----------------|---------|
| absolutely | "absolutely perfect" / "absolutely exhausted" |
| completely | "completely unique" / "completely empty" |
| utterly | "utterly ridiculous" / "utterly exhausted" |
| totally | "totally wrong" / "totally unnecessary" |
| perfectly | "perfectly clear" / "perfectly normal" |
| quite (before absolutes) | "quite perfect" = "completely perfect" |

### 4. Gradable vs Non-Gradable Adjectives

**Kritik ayrım:** Absolute (mutlak) sıfatlar "very" ile kullanılmaz.

| Absolute Sıfat | Yanlış | Doğru |
|----------------|--------|-------|
| perfect | ❌ "very perfect" | ✓ "absolutely perfect" |
| unique | ❌ "very unique" | ✓ "completely unique" |
| empty | ❌ "very empty" | ✓ "completely empty" |
| full | ❌ "very full" | ✓ (genellikle "quite full" informal) |
| dead | ❌ "very dead" | ✓ "completely dead" |
| essential | ❌ "very essential" | ✓ "absolutely essential" |
| impossible | ❌ "very impossible" | ✓ "absolutely impossible" |
| exhausted | gradable olmayan yorgunluk | ✓ "absolutely exhausted" |
| furious | gradable olmayan öfke | ✓ "absolutely furious" |
| freezing | gradable olmayan soğuk | ✓ "absolutely freezing" |

> **Pratik kural:** Bir sıfatın "yarısı" ya da "tamamen" diyebileceğiniz bir ölçüsü varsa gradable'dır ve "very" alır. Yoksa absolute'dur ve "absolutely/completely/utterly" alır.

### 5. Emphatic Pronouns — Pekiştirme Zamirleri

Myself/yourself/himself/herself/itself/ourselves/yourselves/themselves kullanım amacı:

**Özne ile aynı eylem (reflexive):** "She cut **herself**."
**Pekiştirme (emphatic):** Özne ya da nesne vurgusu için
- "I did it **myself**." (başkasına değil, kendi kendime)
- "She **herself** admitted the mistake."
- "The president **himself** signed the document."
- "The building **itself** wasn't damaged, only the windows."

**Preposition + reflexive:**
- "He completed it **by himself**." (yalnız başına)
- "She's not **herself** today." (iyi hissetmiyor)

### 6. "On Earth / In the World / Ever" ile Soru Vurgusu

Soru sözcüklerine eklenerek şaşkınlık veya çaresizlik ifade edilir:

- "What **on earth** is that noise?"
- "Where **in the world** have you been all day?"
- "Who **ever** told you that?"
- "Why **on earth** would she say that?"
- "How **in the world** did you manage that?"

### 7. ALL / WHOLE ile Vurgu

| Yapı | Anlam | Örnek |
|------|-------|-------|
| all + time expression | süre boyunca | "I waited **all day**." / "She talked **all night**." |
| the whole + noun | tamamen, kesintisiz | "**The whole class** laughed." / "I read **the whole book**." |
| all the + noun | hepsi | "**All the students** passed." |

**Fark:**
- "all day" = o günün tüm saatleri boyunca (süre)
- "the whole day" = gün boyunca, kesintisiz (bütünlük hissi)
Pratikte genellikle birbirinin yerine kullanılır.

### 8. Tekrar ile Vurgu

- "It was **very, very** cold that night."
- "She worked **and worked** until she got it right."
- "He's **miles and miles** away."
- "The answer is **no, no, absolutely no**."

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### Hata 1: "quite" Kafa Karışıklığı
"quite" gradable ve absolute sıfatlarla farklı anlam taşır:
- "quite **good**" = fairly good (orta düzeyde iyi)
- "quite **perfect**" = completely perfect (tamamen mükemmel)
Bağlam çok önemli — absolute sıfatla "quite" = "completely" demektir.

### Hata 2: do/does/did + Var Olan Yardımcı Fiil
- ❌ "I **do** am trying really hard."
- ✓ "I **am** really trying hard." (zarf vurgusu)
- ✓ "I **really am** trying hard." (really öne)

### Hata 3: "very" ile Absolute Adjectives
- ❌ "That's **very** impossible."
- ✓ "That's **absolutely** impossible."
- ❌ "It was **very** perfect."
- ✓ "It was **absolutely** perfect."

### Hata 4: Emphatic Pronoun ≠ Reflexive
- ❌ "Please contact **myself** for further information." (burada reflexive/emphatic yanlış)
- ✓ "Please contact **me** for further information."
- ✓ "I **myself** was surprised by the result." (emphatic: correct)

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "I **do** understand your frustration — I've been there myself."
   → emphatic do + emphatic pronoun

2. "**Never have I** witnessed such a spectacular performance."
   → negatif fronting + inversion

3. "The project was **absolutely** brilliant from start to finish."
   → absolute adjective → absolutely (not very)

4. "**This kind of behaviour** I simply cannot tolerate."
   → fronting for emphasis

5. "She **herself** was shocked by the committee's decision."
   → emphatic pronoun mid-sentence

6. "I've been waiting **all morning** for your response."
   → all + time expression

7. "What **on earth** were you thinking when you said that?"
   → on earth for dramatic emphasis

8. "He **does** have a point, even if we don't like it."
   → emphatic does in affirmative

9. "The results were **completely** unexpected."
   → completely + non-gradable "unexpected"

10. "**Not only** did she fail the exam, but she also lost her scholarship."
    → not only... but also = negatif fronting + inversion

11. "The whole **concept** needs to be reconsidered."
    → whole for completeness emphasis

12. "It was **incredibly** difficult, but she managed it **by herself**."
    → incredibly (intensifier) + by herself (emphatic)

13. "**Do** take a seat — we'll be right with you."
    → emphatic do in invitation/command

14. "She's **utterly** exhausted after the marathon."
    → utterly + exhausted (extreme state)
