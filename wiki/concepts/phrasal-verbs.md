# Phrasal Verbs — Eylem+Edat/Zarf Birleşimleri

Phrasal verb, bir fiil ile bir ya da daha fazla parçacığın (particle — edat veya zarf) birleşiminden oluşan ve tek başına fiilden farklı, genellikle deyimsel bir anlam taşıyan yapılardır. "Look" (bakmak) + "after" → "look after" (bakmak/ilgilenmek); bu anlam tek tek kelimelerin toplamından türetilemez. İngilizce'nin en zorlayıcı konularından biridir çünkü aynı fiil farklı parçacıklarla tamamen farklı anlamlar kazanabilir.

---

## Yapı / Structure

```
Fiil + Parçacık(lar)
look  + after       = look after (bakmak/ilgilenmek)
give  + up          = give up (vazgeçmek)
run   + out + of    = run out of (tükenmek)
```

Parçacıklar iki kategoriye girer:
- **Adverb particle (zarf):** up, down, out, in, off, on, away, back, over, through
- **Preposition:** after, for, into, with, at, about

---

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### Tip 1: Geçişli + AYRILABİLİR (Transitive Separable)

Nesne, fiil ile parçacık arasına girilebilir. Nesne zamir ise (it, them, him...) **mutlaka** araya girmek zorundadır.

```
"Turn off the TV."          ✓ (parçacık sonra)
"Turn the TV off."          ✓ (parçacık ayrılmış)
"Turn it off."              ✓ (zamir — zorunlu ayrılma)
"Turn off it."              ❌ (zamir parçacıktan önce gelemez)
```

| Phrasal Verb | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| turn off/on | kapatmak/açmak | "Turn the lights off before you leave." |
| pick up | almak/toplamak/öğrenmek | "Can you pick me up at seven?" |
| put off | ertelemek / caydırmak | "Don't put off what you can do today." |
| call off | iptal etmek | "They called the match off due to rain." |
| fill in | doldurmak (form) | "Fill in the blanks carefully." |
| look up | aramak (sözlük) / saygı duymak | "Look the word up in a dictionary." |
| make up | uydurmak / barışmak / makyaj yapmak | "She made up a convincing excuse." |
| bring up | yetiştirmek / söz açmak | "He was brought up by his grandparents." |
| set up | kurmak / hazırlamak | "They set up a new company last year." |
| write down | not almak | "Write down the address before you forget." |

---

### Tip 2: Geçişli + AYRILMAZ (Transitive Inseparable)

Nesne her zaman parçacığın arkasına gelir — hiçbir koşulda ayrılamaz (zamir de dahil).

```
"Look after the children."  ✓
"Look the children after."  ❌
"Look after them."          ✓ (zamir de parçacıktan sonra)
"Look them after."          ❌
```

| Phrasal Verb | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| look after | bakmak, ilgilenmek | "She looks after her elderly parents." |
| go through | geçirmek / incelemek | "He's been through a lot this year." |
| come across | rastlamak | "I came across an old photo yesterday." |
| run into | tesadüfen karşılaşmak | "I ran into my ex at the supermarket." |
| take after | birine benzemek (ebeveyn) | "She takes after her mother." |
| get over | atlatmak / iyileşmek | "It took him months to get over the breakup." |
| look into | araştırmak | "We'll look into the matter immediately." |
| put up with | katlanmak, tahammül etmek | "I can't put up with this noise anymore." |
| go along with | kabul etmek / katılmak | "He went along with the plan reluctantly." |

---

### Tip 3: Geçişsiz (Intransitive)

Nesne almaz; parçacık ayrılamaz çünkü zaten nesne yoktur.

| Phrasal Verb | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| break down | bozulmak / ağlamak | "The car broke down on the motorway." |
| fall out | tartışmak / anlaşamamak | "They fell out over money." |
| give in | teslim olmak / boyun eğmek | "After hours of arguing, she gave in." |
| turn up | ortaya çıkmak / gelmek | "He turned up two hours late." |
| set off | yola çıkmak | "We set off at dawn to beat the traffic." |
| check in | giriş yapmak | "Passengers must check in two hours before." |
| take off | kalkmak (uçak) / başarıya ulaşmak | "The plane took off on time." |

### Tip 4: Üç Kelimeli Phrasal Verbs (Her Zaman Ayırılamaz)

```
Fiil + Parçacık + Edat
```

| Phrasal Verb | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| run out of | tükenmek | "We've run out of milk — can you buy some?" |
| look forward to | dört gözle beklemek | "I look forward to seeing you next week." |
| put up with | katlanmak | "I won't put up with this behavior." |
| come up with | üretmek / bulmak (fikir) | "She came up with a brilliant solution." |
| get on with | geçinmek / devam etmek | "Do you get on with your colleagues?" |
| catch up with | yetişmek / güncel kalmak | "I need to catch up with the latest news." |
| cut down on | azaltmak | "He's trying to cut down on caffeine." |
| stand up for | savunmak | "You have to stand up for what you believe." |
| make up for | telafi etmek | "I'll make up for the lost time this weekend." |
| go along with | uymak / desteklemek | "I'll go along with whatever you decide." |

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### 1. Zamiri her zaman araya al (ayrılabilir phrasal verbs)
```
"I looked it up."     ✓
"I looked up it."     ❌  (zamir parçacıktan önce gelemez)
"I picked her up."    ✓
"I picked up her."    ❌
```

### 2. Üç kelimeli phrasal verbs asla ayrılmaz
```
"I look forward to seeing you."     ✓
"I look it forward to."             ❌
"She ran out of time."              ✓
"She ran time out of."              ❌
```

### 3. "look forward to" — ardından -ing gelir, infinitive değil
```
"I look forward to hearing from you."  ✓
"I look forward to hear from you."     ❌
(Çünkü "to" burada edat, sonsuz ek değil)
```

### 4. Literal vs Idiomatic kullanım
```
"She ran up the hill."          → Literal: tepeden yukarı koştu
"She ran up a huge debt."       → İdiomatic: büyük borç biriktirdi
"He looked over the fence."     → Literal: çitin üzerinden baktı
"He looked over the report."    → İdiomatic: raporu inceledi
```

### 5. Phrasal verb + noun (bileşik isimler)
Phrasal verb'lerden türeyen isimler genellikle bitişik veya tire ile yazılır:
```
break up  → a breakup
check in  → check-in (havalimanı)
turn on   → a turn-on
set up    → a setup / set-up
workout   → a workout
takeoff   → a takeoff
```

### 6. "be through" / "go through" — geçişli/geçişsiz fark
```
"She's been through a lot."           → geçirmek (deneyim)
"We need to go through the contract." → incelemek
"Are you through with the report?"    → bitirmek (AmE)
```

### 7. Put on — çok anlamlı
```
"Put on your coat."           → giymek
"She's put on weight."        → kilo almak
"They're putting on a show."  → sahnelemek
"Stop putting on an act."     → numara yapmak
```

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "I **looked up** the word in the dictionary." / "I **looked** the word **up**." / "I **looked it up**." — üçü de doğru.
2. "She **looks after** her younger siblings every afternoon." — ayrılamaz.
3. "The meeting has been **called off** due to the snowstorm." — iptal edildi.
4. "He **came up with** a brilliant idea during the brainstorming session." — üç kelimeli, ayrılamaz.
5. "Don't **put off** important decisions — **put them off** ❌ → wait, you CAN: 'Don't put them off.'" ✓
6. "I **ran out of** patience after waiting for two hours." — tükenmek.
7. "She **takes after** her grandmother — same eyes, same smile." — benzemek.
8. "The new startup **took off** faster than anyone expected." — fırlamak.
9. "Can you **turn down** the volume? I'm trying to work." — kısmak.
10. "He **fell out** with his best friend over a misunderstanding." — küsmek.
11. "I can't **put up with** the constant interruptions any longer." — katlanamamak.
12. "They **set up** the company in 2018 and it's been thriving since." — kurmak.
13. "**Work out** the total cost before you commit to the project." — hesaplamak.
14. "She **brought up** an interesting point at the end of the meeting." — gündeme getirmek.
15. "I **missed out on** the party because I had to work late." — kaçırmak.
