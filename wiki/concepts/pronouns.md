# Pronouns — Zamirler

Pronouns (zamirler), bir ismin tekrarını önlemek için kullanılan sözcük sınıfıdır. İngilizce'de zamirler, kişi, sayı, cinsiyet, görev (özne/nesne) ve belirsizlik/belirlilik açısından farklı kategorilere ayrılır.

---

## Yapı / Structure

### Personal Pronouns — Kişisel Zamirler

| Kişi | Subject (Özne) | Object (Nesne) |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| 1. tekil | I | me |
| 2. tekil | you | you |
| 3. tekil (eril) | he | him |
| 3. tekil (dişil) | she | her |
| 3. tekil (nesne/tarafsız) | it | it |
| 1. çoğul | we | us |
| 2. çoğul | you | you |
| 3. çoğul | they | them |

**Kullanım:**
- Subject pronouns: fiilin öznesi olarak → "**He** gave it to me."
- Object pronouns: fiilin nesnesi, preposition'dan sonra → "He gave it to **me**."

**Formal vs Informal:**
- "It's **me**." (informal, günlük konuşma — standart kabul edilir)
- "It is **I**." (formal, dilbilgisel olarak "doğru" ama aşırı resmi)
- Pratikte: "It's me/him/her/us/them" → informal standart

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### 1. Possessive Adjectives vs Possessive Pronouns

| Kişi | Possessive Adjective (sıfat) | Possessive Pronoun (zamir) |
|------|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| I | my | mine |
| you | your | yours |
| he | his | his |
| she | her | hers |
| it | its | (kullanılmaz) |
| we | our | ours |
| they | their | theirs |

**Fark:**
- **Possessive adjective** + isim: "This is **my** book."
- **Possessive pronoun** tek başına: "This book is **mine**."
- ❌ "This is **mine** book." (possessive pronoun + noun = yanlış)
- ✓ "This is **my** book." / "This book is **mine**."

### 2. Reflexive Pronouns — Dönüşlü Zamirler

| Kişi | Reflexive |
|------|-----------|
| I | myself |
| you (tekil) | yourself |
| he | himself |
| she | herself |
| it | itself |
| we | ourselves |
| you (çoğul) | yourselves |
| they | themselves |
| one (genel) | oneself |

**Kullanım 1 — Reflexive (özne = nesne):**
- "She cut **herself**." (kendini kesti)
- "He introduced **himself**." (kendini tanıttı)
- "The cat cleaned **itself**." (kendini temizledi)

**Kullanım 2 — Emphatic (pekiştirme):**
- "I'll do it **myself**." (başkasına değil, kendi kendime)
- "The president **himself** signed the document."
- "The building **itself** wasn't damaged."
- "She **herself** admitted the error."

**Kullanım 3 — "by + reflexive" (yalnız başına):**
- "He lives **by himself**." (tek başına yaşıyor)
- "She finished the project **by herself**." (yardım almadan)
- "Did you do that all **by yourself**?"

**Reflexive KULLANILMAZ bu fiillerden sonra:**
Bazı fiiller İngilizce'de reflexive istemez (Türkçe'den gelen alışkanlık hata yaratır):
- ❌ "I feel **myself** good."
- ✓ "I feel **good**."
- ❌ "She relaxed **herself**."
- ✓ "She **relaxed**."
- ❌ "They met **each other** themselves." (each other yeterli)
- ✓ "They **met**." / "They met **each other**."

### 3. Each Other / One Another — Karşılıklı Zamirler

- "They helped **each other**." (ikisi birbirini yardım etti)
- "The students encouraged **one another**." (birbirlerini)
- Geleneksel kural: each other = 2 kişi; one another = 3+ kişi
- Modern kullanım: ikisi de birbirinin yerine geçer, fark giderek azalıyor

### 4. Demonstrative Pronouns — İşaret Zamirleri

| | Yakın | Uzak |
|-|-------|------|
| Tekil | this | that |
| Çoğul | these | those |

- "**This** is my house." (yakın, tek)
- "**That** is her car." (uzak, tek)
- "**These** are my children." (yakın, çoğul)
- "**Those** were the best days." (uzak, çoğul)

### 5. Indefinite Pronouns — Belirsiz Zamirler

**Every- serisi** (her, hepsi — tekil fiil):
everyone, everybody, everything, everywhere

**Some- serisi** (bazı, bir miktar — olumlu cümleler ve teklifler):
someone, somebody, something, somewhere

**Any- serisi** (herhangi biri — soru ve olumsuz):
anyone, anybody, anything, anywhere

**No- serisi** (hiç kimse, hiçbir şey — negatif anlam):
no one / nobody, nothing, nowhere

**Diğer belirsizler:**
each, every, both, either, neither, all, any, some, none, many, few, much, little

**Subject-Verb Agreement ile indefinite pronouns:**
- "Everyone **has** their own opinion." (tekil fiil + çoğul zamir — şimdi formal'de standart)
- "Somebody **is** at the door."
- "Nobody **was** there."
- "Each of the students **has** a book." (each → tekil)
- "All of the students **have** books." (all → çoğul)

### 6. IT — Özel Kullanımlar

**Hava/doğa olayları:**
- "**It** is raining." / "**It**'s cold today." / "**It** snowed last night."

**Zaman:**
- "**It**'s three o'clock." / "**It**'s Tuesday."

**Mesafe:**
- "**It**'s five miles to the city." / "**It**'s a long way."

**Preparatory it (öne çıkan yapı):**
- "**It** is important **to study** regularly." (gerçek özne = to study)
- "**It** was obvious **that she was lying**." (gerçek özne = that she was lying)
- "**It** took me three hours **to finish**."

**Cleft sentences (bölünmüş cümleler — vurgu):**
- "**It was John** who broke the window." (John'u vurguluyor)
- "**It was the window** that John broke." (window'u vurguluyor)

### 7. THEY — Tekil Cinsiyet-Nötr Kullanım

Modern İngilizce'de "they/them/their" tekil için cinsiyetten bağımsız kullanılır. Bu artık formal İngilizce'de de standart sayılmaktadır.

- "Someone called. **They** didn't leave a message."
- "If a student fails, **they** can retake the exam."
- "Ask the manager — **they** can help you."
- "Everyone should bring **their** own lunch." (klasik kullanım + modern onay)

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### Hata 1: who vs whom
- "**Who** did you call?" (informal, konuşma dilinde kabul edilir)
- "**Whom** did you call?" (formal, dilbilgisel olarak doğru — object)
- Test: cevap "him/her" ise → whom; "he/she" ise → who
  - "You called **him**." → "**Whom** did you call?" ✓

### Hata 2: "Between you and me/I"
- ❌ "Between you and **I**, I think she's wrong."
- ✓ "Between you and **me**, I think she's wrong." (preposition'dan sonra object pronoun)
- Yanlış hypercorrection (İngilizce'de çok yaygın hata): "and I" daha kibar sanılıyor ama yanlış.

### Hata 3: "than me/I"
- Informal: "She's taller than **me**." (çoğunlukla kabul edilir)
- Formal: "She's taller than **I** am." (clause tamamlanınca subject gerekli)
- "He works harder than **she** does." (formal)

### Hata 4: Reflexive Misuse — "Myself" Yanlış Kullanımı
- ❌ "Please send the report to **myself**." (object pronoun gerekli)
- ✓ "Please send the report to **me**."
- ❌ "**Myself** and John attended the meeting."
- ✓ "**John and I** attended the meeting."
- Reflexive, yalnızca aynı cümle içinde öznesiyle bağlantılı olduğunda kullanılır.

### Hata 5: it's vs its
- **it's** = it is / it has
- **its** = iyelik (no apostrophe!)
- ❌ "The dog wagged **it's** tail."
- ✓ "The dog wagged **its** tail."

### Hata 6: Possessive Pronoun + Apostrophe
Possessive pronounlar hiçbir zaman apostrophe almaz:
- ❌ "her's" / "their's" / "your's" / "it's" (iyelik olarak)
- ✓ hers / theirs / yours / its

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "**He** gave the book to **her**, but she already had **one**."
   → subject → object → indefinite pronoun

2. "This house is **ours** — we bought it last year."
   → possessive pronoun (standalone)

3. "She hurt **herself** when she fell off the bike."
   → reflexive (özne = nesne)

4. "The manager **themselves** apologized for the mistake."
   → singular they (gender-neutral)

5. "**It** was Maria who discovered the error."
   → cleft sentence for emphasis

6. "**Everyone** needs to submit **their** application by Friday."
   → singular they (indefinite pronoun)

7. "He built that fence all **by himself** in one weekend."
   → by + reflexive = yalnız başına

8. "**Between you and me**, I don't think this plan will work."
   → preposition → object pronoun (me, not I)

9. "**Whom** did the committee select as the new director?"
   → formal object: whom (him was selected → whom)

10. "**These** are the keys I mentioned; **those** over there belong to Sarah."
    → demonstrative: near vs far

11. "Neither of the candidates **was** satisfactory."
    → neither → singular verb

12. "The company launched **its** new product last week."
    → its (possessive, no apostrophe)

13. "We always help **each other** during difficult times."
    → reciprocal pronoun

14. "**It**'s important that you understand the consequences."
    → preparatory it + that-clause

15. "I can do **this** — I've done harder things **by myself** before."
    → demonstrative + reflexive emphatic
