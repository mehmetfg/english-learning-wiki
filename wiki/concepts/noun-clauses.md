# Noun Clauses — İsim Cümleleri

Noun clause (isim cümlesi), cümle içinde bir ismin yaptığı işi üstlenen bağımlı bir cümleciktir. Özne (subject), nesne (object), tümleç (complement) ya da preposition'ın nesnesi (object of preposition) olabilir. Başlıca türleri: that-clauses, wh-clauses, whether/if clauses ve what-clauses.

---

## Yapı / Structure

### 1. That-Clauses

"That" bağlacıyla başlar. "That" çoğunlukla nesne konumunda atlanabilir, ama özne konumunda atlanamaz.

**Özne (Subject) olarak:**
- "**That she passed the exam** surprised everyone."
- "**That he refused to apologize** was shocking."
- Not: Günlük konuşmada özne olarak that-clause yerine "It + be + adj + that" yapısı tercih edilir (aşağıya bkz.)

**Nesne (Object) olarak:**
- "I think **(that)** she is right." — "that" isteğe bağlı
- "She believes **(that)** he is telling the truth."
- "He said **(that)** he would come."

**Sıfatlardan (Adjectives) sonra:**
- "I'm glad **that** you came."
- "She's surprised **that** he didn't call."
- "It's important **that** he **attend** (attend — subjunctive) the meeting."

**İsimlerden (Nouns) sonra:**
- "The fact **that** he lied is disturbing."
- "The news **that** they won surprised us."
- "There's a rumour **that** the company is closing."
- "The idea **that** we are alone in the universe is frightening."

---

### 2. Wh-Clauses — Dolaylı Soru Cümleleri (Indirect Questions)

Soru kelimesi (who, what, where, when, why, how, which, whose) ile başlar.

**KRİTİK KURAL — Kelime Sırası:** Dolaylı sorularda SORU SIRASI KULLANILMAZ. Normal cümle sırası: **soru kelimesi + özne + fiil**

| Doğrudan Soru | Dolaylı Soru (Noun Clause) |
|---|---|
| Where **does she live**? | I don't know **where she lives**. ✓ |
| What **do you want**? | Tell me **what you want**. ✓ |
| Why **did he leave**? | I wonder **why he left**. ✓ |
| When **does the train arrive**? | Can you tell me **when the train arrives**? ✓ |
| How **much does it cost**? | Do you know **how much it costs**? ✓ |

**YANLIŞ (soru sırası dolaylı cümlede):**
- "I don't know where **does she live**." ✗
- "Tell me what **do you want**." ✗

**Özne, nesne, tümleç, preposition nesnesi:**
- Özne: "**Why he left** remains a mystery." (literary/resmi)
- Nesne: "I understand **what you mean**."
- Tümleç: "The question is **who should go first**."
- Preposition nesnesi: "I was amazed at **what she said**."

---

### 3. Whether / If Clauses — Evet/Hayır Dolaylı Soruları

Evet/hayır soruların dolaylı hali. "Whether" resmi, "if" gündelik kullanım için tercih edilir.

- "I don't know **if/whether** she's coming."
- "She asked **whether** I had eaten." (resmi)
- "Can you tell me **if** the shop is open?"

**Whether...or not:**
- "Tell me **whether or not** you agree."
- "Tell me **whether** you agree **or not**."

**"Whether to + infinitive" yapısı:**
- "I can't decide **whether to go** or stay."
- "She didn't know **whether to laugh or cry**."
- "whether to + inf" — if ile kullanılamaz: ~~"if to go"~~ ✗

**Whether vs If — Fark:**
- Özne konumunda: yalnızca "whether": "**Whether** he comes is uncertain." (NOT: ~~If he comes is uncertain.~~)
- Infinitive öncesinde: yalnızca "whether": "I can't decide **whether to go**." (NOT: ~~if to go~~)
- Preposition sonrasında: yalnızca "whether": "It depends on **whether** she agrees."
- Cümle sonunda veya nesne konumunda: her ikisi de kullanılabilir

---

### 4. What-Clauses — Nominal Relative Clauses

"What" burada relative pronoun işlevi görür: **"the thing(s) that"** anlamında. Öncesinde antecedent (isim) yoktur.

- "**What you need** is a good rest." (= The thing that you need)
- "**What surprised me** was his attitude."
- "That's exactly **what I meant**."
- "I'll buy **what you recommend**."
- "**What he said** doesn't make sense."

"Whatever, whoever, whichever, however, wherever, whenever" de benzer işlev görebilir:
- "**Whatever you decide**, I'll support you."
- "Give it to **whoever needs it** most."

---

### 5. To-Infinitive Clauses

Bazen ayrı bir kategori olarak incelenir. Belirli soru kelimeleriyle birlikte "what/how/where/when/whether + to-infinitive" yapısı çok yaygındır:

- "I don't know **how to fix it**."
- "The question is **what to do** next."
- "She couldn't decide **where to sit**."
- "He told me **when to arrive**."
- "I learned **how to cook** Italian food."

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### Noun Clause'un Cümledeki Görevleri

| Görev | Örnek |
|---|---|
| **Özne** | "**That she lied** is unforgivable." |
| **Nesne** | "I know **(that)** he is right." |
| **Özne tümleci** | "The truth is **that we failed**." |
| **Preposition nesnesi** | "I was shocked by **what he said**." |
| **Appositive (açıklama)** | "The fact **that he resigned** shocked us." |

### "It" ile Kurgusal Özne Yapısı

That-clause özne konumunda kullanıldığında, günlük dilde "It + be + adj/V3 + that..." yapısı tercih edilir:

- "**That she passed** surprised everyone." → "**It** surprised everyone **that she passed**."
- "**That he is right** is obvious." → "**It** is obvious **that he is right**."
- "**It** is important **that he attend** (subjunctive) the meeting."
- "**It** is essential **that** all forms **be completed** (subjunctive) by Friday."

### Subjunctive Mood (Dilek Kipi)

"It is important/vital/essential/necessary/suggested/recommended + that" sonrasında **subjunctive** (fiilin yalın hali, kişiye göre değişmez) kullanılır:

- "It is important that he **attend** the meeting." (NOT: ~~attends~~)
- "She recommended that he **see** a doctor." (NOT: ~~sees~~)
- "It is essential that the report **be** ready by Monday."
- "We insisted that she **apologize**."

Bu yapı özellikle AmE'de yaygın; BrE'de "should + V1" da kullanılır:
- "It's important that he **should attend**." (BrE alternatif)

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### 1. That'sız Noun Clause

Nesne konumunda "that" sıklıkla atlanır, ancak şu durumlarda genellikle bırakılır:
- Uzun veya karmaşık cümlelerde
- Cümle başında (özne konumunda) — zorunlu
- İki nesne yan yana geldiğinde: "I told him **that** that was wrong." (İki "that" — biri bağlaç, biri işaret zamiri)

### 2. "The fact that" vs "The idea that"

Her ikisi de doğru appositive yapılar:
- "The fact **that** smoking causes cancer is well established."
- "The idea **that** money brings happiness is debatable."
- "The claim **that** he was there is false."
- "The belief **that** hard work pays off..."

### 3. Wh-Clause Özne Konumunda — Literary

- "**Why he left** remains a mystery." (literary/formal)
- "**How she managed it** is beyond me."
- Günlük dilde: "Nobody knows why he left." şeklinde nesne konumu tercih edilir.

### 4. Reported Speech ile İlişki

Noun clauses, reported (indirect) speech'in temelini oluşturur:
- "He said, 'I am tired.'" → "He said **(that)** he was tired."
- "She asked, 'Where do you live?'" → "She asked **where I lived**."
- "He asked, 'Are you coming?'" → "He asked **whether/if I was coming**."

### 5. "Whether to + inf" — "If" Kullanılamaz

- "I can't decide **whether to go** or stay." ✓
- ~~"I can't decide if to go or stay."~~ ✗

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "**That he won the award** surprised his colleagues." — that-clause özne
2. "I believe **(that)** honesty is the best policy." — that-clause nesne
3. "**It** is clear **that** more funding is needed." — kurgusal özne
4. "I don't know **where she went** after the party." — wh-clause (soru sırası yok!)
5. "Tell me **what you think** about the proposal." — wh-clause nesne
6. "She asked **whether the meeting** had been rescheduled." — whether clause
7. "I couldn't decide **whether to accept** the offer or not." — whether + infinitive
8. "**What I find** most interesting is his approach." — what-clause özne
9. "That's **what I've been** trying to explain." — what-clause tümleç
10. "The news **that the war had ended** spread quickly." — appositive that-clause
11. "It's vital **that all passengers wear** their seatbelts." — subjunctive
12. "I'll give it to **whoever arrives** first." — whoever clause
