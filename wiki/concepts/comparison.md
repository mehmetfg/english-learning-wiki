# Comparison — Karşılaştırma Yapıları

İngilizcede karşılaştırma; eşitlik, üstünlük, üstünlük derecesi ve zıt karşılaştırma olmak üzere çeşitli yapılarla ifade edilir. Doğru yapının seçimi hem gramer hem de nüans açısından önemlidir.

---

## Yapı / Structure

### 1. AS...AS (Eşitlik Karşılaştırması)

**Yapı:** as + sıfat/zarf + as

| Kullanım | Örnek |
|---|---|
| Olumlu eşitlik | "She's **as tall as** her brother." |
| Olumsuz (not as/so...as) | "He's **not as** clever **as** she is." / "It's **not so** bad." |
| Miktar ile | "Eat **as much as** you like." / "Take **as many as** you need." |
| ASAP gibi deyimler | "as soon as possible" / "as fast as possible" |

**TWICE / THREE TIMES AS...AS:**
- "This book is **twice as expensive as** that one."
- "She works **three times as hard as** her colleague."

---

### 2. COMPARATIVE + THAN (Üstünlük Karşılaştırması)

**Yapı:** sıfat/zarf-er / more + sıfat/zarf + than

| Register | Kullanım |
|---|---|
| Formal | "She is taller **than I** (am)." |
| Informal | "She is taller **than me**." — her ikisi de kabul görür |

**NOT...AS...AS = COMPARATIVE karşılığı:**
- "She isn't as fast as him." = "He is faster than her."

---

### 3. THE + COMPARATIVE, THE + COMPARATIVE (Paralel Artış)

**Yapı:** The + comparative + subject + verb, the + comparative + subject + verb

| Örnek | Anlam |
|---|---|
| "**The more** you practice, **the better** you get." | Pratik arttıkça beceri artar. |
| "**The harder** I work, **the luckier** I seem." | Çok çalışmak şans gibi görünür. |
| "**The sooner**, **the better**." | Ne kadar erken, o kadar iyi. |
| "**The more the merrier**." | Kalabalık olunca daha eğlenceli. |

---

### 4. DOUBLE COMPARATIVE (Giderek Artan Değişim)

**Yapı:** comparative + and + comparative / more and more + adj/adv

| Örnek | Açıklama |
|---|---|
| "It's getting **hotter and hotter**." | 1 heceli sıfatlar |
| "The situation is becoming **more and more** complicated." | 2+ heceli sıfatlar |
| "She's working **harder and harder**." | 1 heceli zarf |

---

### 5. SUPERLATIVE (En Üstünlük)

**Yapı:** the + sıfat/zarf-est / the most + sıfat/zarf

**Superlative + Present Perfect (yaygın kalıp):**
- "This is the **most interesting** book I've **ever** read."
- "She's the **best** teacher I've **ever had**."

**Superlative + IN (yer/grup):**
- "the **tallest** building **in** the world"
- "the **best** student **in** the class"

**Superlative + OF (zaman/küme):**
- "the **coldest** day **of** the year"
- "the **best of** all options"

---

### 6. Comparative'ı Güçlendirme (Modifying Comparatives)

| Güçlendirici | Etki | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| much / far / a lot / considerably | Büyük fark | "**much** better" / "**far** more interesting" |
| a bit / slightly / a little | Küçük fark | "**a bit** taller" / "**slightly** more expensive" |
| any / no (soru/olumsuzda) | Ölçü | "Is it **any** better?" / "**no** worse than before" |

---

### 7. İsimleri Karşılaştırma (Comparing Nouns)

| Yapı | İsim türü | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **more** + any | countable/uncountable | "more books" / "more water" |
| **fewer** + countable (formal) | sayılabilen | "**fewer** mistakes" / "**fewer** people" |
| **less** + uncountable | sayılamayan | "**less** time" / "**less** money" |

> NOT: "less people" — gayri resmi konuşmada yaygın; formal metinlerde "fewer people" tercih edilir.

---

### 8. LIKE vs AS (Benzetme)

| Yapı | Kullanım | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **like** + isim/zamir | Benzetme (sıfat gibi) | "She sings **like** a professional." |
| **like** + isim/zamir | Görünüş | "He looks **like** his father." |
| **as** + cümle (clause) | Eylem karşılaştırması | "Do **as** I say." / "**As** I mentioned earlier..." |
| **as** + sıfat + **as** | Eşitlik | "**as** fast **as** a cheetah" |
| **the same...as** | Aynılık | "She has **the same** job **as** her mother." |

---

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### SUPERIOR / INFERIOR / SENIOR / JUNIOR / PRIOR → TO (than değil!)
- "Her work is **superior to** mine." ✓ / "superior **than**" ❌
- "This model is **inferior to** the previous one." ✓
- "She is **senior to** him by five years." ✓

### PREFER → TO (than değil!)
- "I **prefer** tea **to** coffee." ✓
- "I **prefer** tea **than** coffee." ❌
- "I **prefer** to walk **rather than** take the bus." ✓

### DIFFERENT FROM / TO / THAN
- **different from** — BrE formal ve genel kural
- **different to** — BrE informal: "It's different **to** what I expected."
- **different than** — AmE: "It's different **than** I expected."

### COMPARED TO vs COMPARED WITH
- **compared to** = benzetme (iki şeyin benzer olduğunu vurgular): "Compared **to** a cheetah, humans are slow."
- **compared with** = zıt karşılaştırma (farkları vurgular): "Sales this year, **compared with** last year, are much higher."

### AS MUCH AS / AS MANY AS (Beklenti karşılaştırması)
- "It could cost **as much as** £500." → 500 sterline kadar çıkabilir.
- "**As many as** 50 people attended." → 50 kişiye kadar.

### NOT SO...AS vs NOT AS...AS
- Her ikisi de aynı anlama gelir; "not so...as" biraz daha formal/literary.

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "She's **as confident as** ever." → Eşitlik.
2. "He's **not as** experienced **as** she is." → Olumsuz eşitlik.
3. "This laptop is **twice as** powerful **as** my old one." → Çarpan karşılaştırma.
4. "**The more** I learn, **the more** I realize I don't know." → Paralel artış.
5. "Traffic is getting **worse and worse** every year." → Double comparative.
6. "It's **more and more** difficult to find affordable housing." → Double comparative.
7. "This is **by far** the **most** challenging project I've worked on." → Superlative güçlendirici.
8. "She's **considerably** better at math than her brother." → Large difference.
9. "The new version is **slightly** faster than the old one." → Small difference.
10. "**Fewer** cars on the road means **less** pollution." → Fewer vs less.
11. "She runs **like** the wind." → Like + noun.
12. "**As** the report shows, sales have improved." → As + clause.
13. "We arrived at **the same** time **as** them." → The same...as.
14. "His attitude is **superior to** everyone else's." → Superior + to.
15. "I **prefer** hiking **to** cycling." → Prefer + to.
16. "The results are **different from** what we expected." → Different from.
17. "**Compared with** last quarter, performance has improved." → Contrast focus.
18. "**Compared to** a marathon, a 5K is very manageable." → Similarity focus.
19. "**The sooner** you start, **the less** you'll have to rush." → Parallel increase.
20. "As **many as** 200 candidates applied for the position." → Upper limit estimate.
