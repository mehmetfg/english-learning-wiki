# Adjectives — Sıfatlar

Sıfatlar isimleri niteleyen veya tanımlayan sözcüklerdir. İngilizcede sıfatın cümledeki konumu ve özelliği — nitelediği ismin öncesinde mi, fiilden sonra mı kullandığı — anlam ve gramatikal doğruluk açısından kritiktir.

---

## Yapı / Structure

### Attributive vs Predicative Konum

| Konum | Açıklama | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **Attributive** (isimden önce) | İsmin hemen önünde gelir | "a **beautiful** house" |
| **Predicative** (fiilden sonra) | be/linking fiilden sonra gelir | "The house **is beautiful**." |

#### Yalnızca ATTRIBUTIVE Olan Sıfatlar
Bunlar isimden sonra veya fiil tamamlayıcısı olarak kullanılamaz:
- **main, chief, principal** → "the **main** reason" ✓ / "the reason is **main**" ❌
- **utter, sheer, mere** → "**utter** nonsense" / "**sheer** luck" / "a **mere** child"
- **former, latter** → "the **former** president"
- **elder, eldest** (aile için) → "my **elder** brother"
- **inner, outer, upper, lower** → "the **inner** city"

#### Yalnızca PREDICATIVE Olan Sıfatlar (a- ön eki başlayanlar)
Bu sıfatlar ismin önüne gelemez:
- **afraid, asleep, awake, alive, alone, alike, aware, ashamed**
- **content, glad, ill, ready, sorry, well, worth**
- "the **afraid** child" ❌ → "the child **is afraid**" ✓
- "an **asleep** baby" ❌ → "the baby **is asleep**" ✓

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### Bileşik Sıfatlar (Compound Adjectives — Hyphenated)

İsimden önce geldiğinde tire kullanılır; fiilden sonra geldiğinde genellikle tire kalkar:

| İsimden önce (tire ile) | Fiilden sonra (tiresiz) |
|---|---|
| "a **well-known** actor" | "The actor **is well known**." |
| "a **ten-year-old** child" | "The child **is ten years old**." |
| "a **two-hour** movie" | "The movie lasts **two hours**." |
| "a **far-fetched** idea" | "The idea seems **far-fetched**." |
| "a **good-looking** man" | "He **is good-looking**." |
| "a **thought-provoking** question" | — |
| "a **best-selling** book" | — |

### Gradable vs Non-Gradable (Derecelendirilebilir mi?)

| Tür | Özellik | Kullanılan yoğunlaştırıcılar | Örnek |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gradable** | Derece kabul eder | very, rather, quite, more/most | "very tired", "quite happy" |
| **Non-gradable/Absolute** | Derece kabul etmez | absolutely, completely, utterly, totally | "absolutely perfect", "completely empty" |

Non-gradable sıfatlar: **perfect, unique, dead, empty, full, essential, impossible, excellent, terrible, enormous, tiny**

> "**very** perfect" ❌ → "**absolutely** perfect" ✓
> "**very** unique" ❌ → "**completely** unique" ✓

### Karşılaştırma Dereceleri (Comparative & Superlative)

#### Kısa Sıfatlar (1 hece + çoğu 2 heceli): -er / -est
| Temel | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| tall | taller | tallest |
| big | bigger | biggest |
| happy | happier | happiest |
| nice | nicer | nicest |

Yazım kuralları:
- -e ile bitenler: -r / -st ekle: nice → nicer/nicest
- ÜNSÜZü+SESLİ+ÜNSÜZü (CVC): son ünsüzü çift yaz: big → bigger/biggest
- -y ile bitenler: y→i yap: happy → happier/happiest

#### Uzun Sıfatlar (çoğu 2+ heceli): more / most
- "more interesting" / "most interesting"
- "more carefully" / "most carefully"
- "more beautiful" / "most beautiful"

#### Düzensiz Formlar
| Temel | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| good | better | best |
| bad | worse | worst |
| far | farther / further | farthest / furthest |
| little | less | least |
| many / much | more | most |

#### Double Comparative (Paralel artış)
"The more you practice, the better you get."
"The harder I work, the luckier I seem."
"The sooner, the better." / "The more the merrier."

#### Karşılaştırıcıları Güçlendirme
- **much / far / a lot / considerably** → büyük fark: "**much** better" / "**far** more interesting"
- **slightly / a little / a bit** → küçük fark: "**slightly** taller" / "**a bit** more expensive"

#### AS...AS Yapısı
- "She's **as tall as** her sister."
- "It's **not as** expensive **as** I thought."
- "She's **twice as** fast **as** him."

---

### Sıfatlar İsim Olarak (The + Adjective)

the + sıfat = bir grup insan:
- "**the elderly**" / "**the rich**" / "**the unemployed**" / "**the homeless**" / "**the injured**"
- Her zaman çoğul anlam taşır, çoğul fiil alır: "**The homeless are** in need of support."

---

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### ELDER / ELDEST vs OLDER / OLDEST
- **elder/eldest** → yalnızca aile üyeleri için (formal/British); "than" ile kullanılamaz
- **older/oldest** → herkese uygulanabilir
- "He's **older** than me." ✓ / "He's **elder** than me." ❌
- "my **elder** brother" ✓ / "my **older** brother" ✓

### FURTHER vs FARTHER
- **farther** → fiziksel mesafe: "The station is **farther** than I thought."
- **further** → mecazi/ek anlamda: "**further** education" / "any **further** questions?"
- Günlük kullanımda "further" her iki anlamda da kabul edilir.

### LATTER / FORMER
- İkiden bahsederken önce geçene **former**, sonra geçene **latter** denir:
- "I prefer coffee to tea. The **former** keeps me alert; the **latter** relaxes me."

### WORTH
- Edat gibi davranır — gerund (isim-fiil) veya isim alır:
- "It's **worth reading**." (gerund) ✓
- "It's **worth** the effort." (isim) ✓
- "It's **worth to read**." ❌

### "OLD ENGLISH TEACHER" Belirsizliği
- "a old English teacher" → hem "yaşlı İngilizce öğretmeni" hem de "Eski İngilizce öğretmeni" olabilir.
- Bağlam önemlidir.

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "It was an **utter** disaster." → Yalnızca attributive.
2. "The baby is **asleep**." → Yalnızca predicative.
3. "She lives **alone**." → Predicative (adverb gibi de işlev görebilir).
4. "He's a **well-known** scientist." → Compound, isimden önce.
5. "He is **well known** in his field." → Compound, fiilden sonra — tire yok.
6. "This is an **absolutely** perfect solution." → Non-gradable + intensifier.
7. "The **more** you eat, the **worse** you feel." → Double comparative.
8. "She's **far more** talented than her classmates." → Modified comparative.
9. "It's **slightly** colder today than yesterday." → Small difference.
10. "**The rich** should pay higher taxes." → Adjective as noun — group.
11. "My **elder** sister lives in London." → Elder — family only.
12. "This is **older** than the other one." → Older — general use.
13. "I need **further** information." → Further — figurative/additional.
14. "His house is **farther** from work." → Farther — physical distance.
15. "The book is **worth** reading." → Worth + gerund.
16. "It's **not as** difficult **as** it looks." → AS...AS negative.
17. "The **unemployed** need better support systems." → Adjective as noun.
18. "It was a **thought-provoking** documentary." → Compound before noun.
19. "The **former** option is safer; the **latter** is faster." → Former/Latter reference.
20. "It was **sheer** luck that we found it." → Attributive-only intensifier.
