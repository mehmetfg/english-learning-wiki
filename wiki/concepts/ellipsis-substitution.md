# Ellipsis and Substitution — Kısaltma ve Yerine Koyma

Ellipsis (kısaltma) ve substitution (yerine koyma), bağlamdan anlaşılan kelimelerin tekrarını önlemek için kullanılan dilbilgisel stratejilerdir. Metni daha akıcı, konuşmayı daha doğal kılarlar.

---

## Yapı / Structure

### ELLIPSIS — Kısaltma

Bağlamdan açıkça anlaşılan sözcüklerin atlanmasıdır.

#### 1. Özne Kısaltması (Subject Ellipsis — Informal)
Gayri resmi konuşmada özne atlanabilir:
- "[I'm] Coming home soon."
- "[I] Forgot to mention — the meeting is cancelled."
- "[Are you] Ready to go?"

#### 2. Fiil Öbeği Kısaltması (Verb Phrase Ellipsis — En Yaygın)
Tekrar eden fiil öbeği atlanır, yardımcı fiil bırakılır:

| Örnek | Kısaltılan kısım |
|---|---|
| "She can swim and he **can** too." | [swim] atlandı |
| "Did you finish?" "Yes, I **did**." | [finish] atlandı |
| "Are you coming?" "I **might**." | [come] atlandı |
| "She left, and so **did** he." | [leave] atlandı |
| "She hadn't eaten, and **neither had** he." | [eaten] atlandı |

#### 3. Karşılaştırma Kısaltması (Comparative Ellipsis)
- "She works harder than I **[do/work]**."
- "He ate more than expected **[him to eat]**."

#### 4. TO-Kısaltması (To-Substitution — Infinitive Ellipsis)
Fiil atılır ama **to** kalır:
- "I was going **to** [phone her] but I couldn't **[phone her]**." → "I was going **to** but couldn't."
- "Would you like to come?" → "I'd love **to**." ✓
- "Would you like to come?" → "I'd love." ❌ — to atılamaz!

> KRITIK KURAL: Fiili atlasanız bile "to"yu bırakın.

---

### SUBSTITUTION — Yerine Koyma

Tekrar eden sözcük yerine bir yedek sözcük kullanılır.

#### ONE / ONES (Sayılabilen isim yerine)

| Kullanım | Örnek |
|---|---|
| Tekil sayılabilen | "I need a pen. Do you have **one**?" |
| Çoğul sayılabilen | "I like the red **ones**." |
| Sıfatlı | "Do you prefer the big one or the small **one**?" |

> DİKKAT: "one/ones" sayılamayan isimler için kullanılamaz:
> - "I need milk. Do you have **some**?" ✓ (not "one") ❌

#### DO / DO SO (Fiil öbeği yerine)

| Kullanım | Örnek | Formalite |
|---|---|---|
| **do** | "She studies hard and so **does** her sister." | Genel |
| **do so** | "He promised to help and he **did so**." | Daha formal |
| **do it** | "She promised to call and she **did it**." | Informal |

> "do so" = daha resmi; "do it" = daha az resmi; ikisi çoğu durumda birbirinin yerine geçer.

#### SO (That-clause yerine — think/believe/hope/suppose/expect/afraid gibi fiillerden sonra)

| Fiil | Örnek |
|---|---|
| think | "Will it work?" → "I think **so**." |
| believe | "Is he coming?" → "I believe **so**." |
| hope | "Will you pass?" → "I hope **so**." |
| suppose | "Is it too late?" → "I suppose **so**." |
| expect | "Will they agree?" → "I expect **so**." |
| afraid | "Is it cancelled?" → "I'm afraid **so**." |

#### NOT (Olumsuz SO yerine)

| Fiil | Olumsuz | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| hope | I hope not | "Will it rain?" → "I hope **not**." |
| afraid | I'm afraid not | "Any seats left?" → "I'm afraid **not**." |
| think | I don't think so | "Is he lying?" → "I don't think **so**." |
| suppose | I suppose not | "Can we delay it?" → "I suppose **not**." |

> ❌ "I don't hope so." → ✓ "I hope not."

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### Anaphoric vs Cataphoric Referans

| Tür | Yön | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **Anaphoric** | Geriye bakar (en yaygın) | "I met a girl. **She** was lovely." (she = az önce söylenen) |
| **Cataphoric** | İleriye bakar (daha az yaygın) | "Although **she** was tired, Maria kept working." (she = ileride gelecek) |

---

### GAPPING (Koordinatlı Cümlelerde Kısaltma)

Koordinatlı cümleler arasında tekrar eden fiil atlanır:

| Örnek | Kısaltılan |
|---|---|
| "John likes jazz and Mary [likes] classical." | [likes] atlandı |
| "She went to Paris and he [went] to London." | [went] atlandı |
| "Tom ordered fish; Sarah [ordered] soup." | [ordered] atlandı |

---

### SO...NEITHER / NOR Inversion (Tersyüz edilmiş ellipsis)

Bir önceki cümleye katılım ifade ederken yardımcı fiil özneye göre çevrilir:

**Olumlu → SO + yardımcı + özne:**
- "I love coffee." → "**So do I**." / "**So does** she."

**Olumsuz → NEITHER / NOR + yardımcı + özne:**
- "I haven't seen it." → "**Neither have I**." / "**Nor have** they."
- "She can't swim." → "**Neither can** he."

---

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### "One" Sayılamayan İsimler İçin Kullanılamaz
- "We need more paper. Do you have **some**?" ✓
- "We need more paper. Do you have **one**?" ❌

### "To" Fiilsiz Kalabilir Ama Atılamaz
- "Are you planning to leave?" → "I'd prefer not **to**." ✓ (leave atlandı, to kaldı)
- "I'd prefer not." ❌

### "I don't think so" vs "I think not"
- "I don't think so" → Daha yaygın (informal)
- "I think not" → Daha formal/literary; aynı anlam.
- "I don't hope so" → YANLIŞ — "I hope not" kullanın.

### DO SO — Spesifik Eylem Gerektirir
"Do so" ancak bağlamda belirli bir eylem varsa kullanılabilir:
- "She promised to clean up, and she **did so** immediately." ✓
- Belirsiz bağlamda "do so" garip durur → "do it" tercih edilir.

### NEITHER / NOR İnversionunda Yardımcı Fiil Seçimi
Bir önceki cümlenin yardımcı fiiliyle uyum sağlanır:
- "I was late." → "**So was** I." ✓
- "They can help." → "**So can** we." ✓
- "He's been working hard." → "**So have** I." ✓

### Ellipsis Formal Yazıda Dikkatli Kullanılmalı
Özne kısaltması (subject ellipsis) çok gayri resmidir; formal yazıda (akademik, iş) genellikle kaçınılır.

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "She can play the piano and her brother **can** too." → VP ellipsis.
2. "Would you like to come?" / "I'd love **to**." → To-substitution — to kalır.
3. "He said he'd finish it, and he **did**." → VP ellipsis.
4. "I haven't tried it." / "**Neither have** I." → Neither inversion.
5. "She loves hiking." / "**So does** he." → So inversion.
6. "I need a new bag. This **one** is falling apart." → One substitution.
7. "I prefer the leather **ones** over the fabric ones." → Ones substitution.
8. "Do you have any coffee?" / "I'm afraid **not** — we ran out." → Negative substitute.
9. "Is the project on track?" / "I believe **so**." → So substitution.
10. "He promised to apologize, and he **did so** the very next day." → Do so formal.
11. "John reads thrillers; his wife [reads] historical fiction." → Gapping.
12. "They flew to Tokyo; she [flew] to Seoul." → Gapping.
13. "Although **she** seemed confident, Maria was nervous." → Cataphoric reference.
14. "I met an interesting man. **He** turned out to be a novelist." → Anaphoric.
15. "She works harder than I **[do]**." → Comparative ellipsis.
16. "Some students passed; **others** [didn't pass]." → Ellipsis + others pronoun.
17. "Will you be attending?" / "I suppose **so**." → So after suppose.
18. "She was going to call but **couldn't** [call]." → VP ellipsis.
19. "I need milk. Do you have **some**?" → Some (not one) for uncountable.
20. "Can she drive?" / "I don't think **so**." → Negative think + so.
21. "They were exhausted; **so were** the coaches." → So inversion.
22. "I haven't read it." / "**Nor have** I." → Nor inversion variant.
