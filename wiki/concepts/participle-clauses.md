# Participle Clauses — Ortaç Cümleleri

Participle clause (ortaç cümlesi), bir ortaç (participle) ile başlayan, bağımlı bir cümleciktir. Tam bir subordinate clause'un (bağımlı cümle) kısaltılmış, daha ekonomik halidir. Resmi yazı dilinde ve edebi metinlerde sıkça kullanılır.

Üç türü vardır:
1. **Present participle clause** (V-ing)
2. **Past participle clause** (V3)
3. **Perfect participle clause** (having + V3)

**ALTIN KURAL:** Participle clause'un öznesi, ana cümlenin öznesiyle AYNI olmalıdır.

---

## Yapı / Structure

### 1. Present Participle Clauses (-ing)

Şimdiki zaman ortacı (-ing) ile kurulan cümlecikler, eş zamanlılık, neden, sonuç veya koşul ifade eder.

**a) Eş Zamanlı Eylemler (Simultaneous Actions) — Aynı Özne**

Ana cümledeki eylemle aynı anda gerçekleşen eylemi ifade eder:

- "**Walking down the street**, I saw an old friend." (Caddede yürürken → yürüyen de, gören de ben)
- "**Smiling broadly**, she accepted the award."
- "He sat in the corner, **reading a book**."

"While" veya "as" ile kısaltma:
- "While she was cooking dinner, she sang." → "**Cooking dinner**, she sang."

**b) Neden/Gerekçe (Reason/Cause)**

"Because/Since/As" bağlacının yerine geçer:

- "**Feeling tired**, she went to bed early." (Yorgun hissettiği için)
- "**Not knowing what to do**, I asked for help." (Ne yapacağımı bilmediğim için)
- "**Having no money**, he couldn't buy food."

**c) Sonuç (Result)**

Ana eylemden doğan sonucu ifade eder (genellikle cümle sonunda "thus/thereby" ile):

- "She worked hard, **thus becoming** the best in her class."
- "He fell asleep at the wheel, **causing** a serious accident."

**d) Koşul (Condition) — "if" Yerine**

- "**Turning left**, you'll find the bank." (Sola dönerseniz...)
- "**Using this method**, you can solve the problem easily." (Bu yöntemi kullanırsanız...)

**Olumsuz:** Participle'dan önce "not" gelir:

- "**Not knowing** the answer, she remained silent."
- "**Not wanting to cause** trouble, he said nothing."

---

### 2. Past Participle Clauses (V3)

Geçmiş zaman ortacı (V3) ile kurulan cümlecikler, genellikle pasif anlam taşır.

**a) Pasif Anlam**

- "**Written in 1847**, the novel is still popular today." (= which was written in 1847)
- "**Destroyed by fire**, the building had to be rebuilt."
- "**Exhausted by the journey**, she fell asleep immediately."

**b) Koşul/Zaman (Condition/Time)**

- "**Given more time**, I could do better." (Daha fazla zaman verilseydi)
- "**Left alone**, children can get into trouble."
- "**Told the truth**, she would have understood."

**c) Sıfat İşlevi (Adjective Function) — Reduced Relative Clause**

- "**Spoken** language differs from **written** language."
- "The documents **required for the application** must be submitted by Friday." (= which are required)

---

### 3. Perfect Participle Clauses (having + V3)

Ana eylemden ÖNCE tamamlanan eylemi gösterir. Zaman sırası önemlidir.

- "**Having finished** the work, she went home." (Önce bitirdi, sonra gitti.)
- "**Having lived** in Paris for ten years, he speaks fluent French."
- "**Having read** the report, I can now answer your questions."

**Passive Perfect Participle (having been + V3):**

- "**Having been rejected** twice, he gave up applying."
- "**Having been told** the news, she broke down in tears."

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### Bağlaçlarla Birlikte Kullanım

Anlam netliği için bağlaç bırakılabilir:

| Bağlaç | Örnek |
|---|---|
| **When** | "**When speaking** in public, she feels nervous." |
| **While** | "**While waiting** for the bus, I read my book." |
| **Before** | "**Before leaving**, check all windows." |
| **After** | "**After finishing** university, he moved abroad." |
| **Since** | "**Since joining** the company, he's been promoted twice." |

### Reduced Relative Clauses (Kısaltılmış İlgi Cümleleri)

Relative clause → participle clause dönüşümü:

| Tam Cümle | Kısaltılmış |
|---|---|
| "The man **who is talking** to the manager is my boss." | "The man **talking** to the manager is my boss." |
| "Anyone **who wants** to join should register." | "Anyone **wanting** to join should register." |
| "The documents **that are required** for the application..." | "The documents **required** for the application..." |
| "The letter **that was written** in 1900..." | "The letter **written** in 1900..." |

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### 1. Dangling Participle — En Yaygın Hata

Participle clause'un öznesi, ana cümlenin öznesiyle FARKLI olursa "dangling participle" (sarkan ortaç) hatası oluşur.

**YANLIŞ:**
- "**Walking down the street**, the trees were beautiful." ✗
  (Ağaçlar yürümüyor — subject uyuşmazlığı)
- "**Having finished** the exam, the room was quiet." ✗
  (Oda sınav bitirmedi)

**DOĞRU:**
- "**Walking down the street**, **I** noticed the beautiful trees." ✓
- "**Having finished** the exam, **the students** left quietly." ✓

### 2. Sabit İfadeler (Fixed Expressions) — Özne Kuralına İstisna

Bazı participle ifadeleri kural dışı "absolute" yapılardır — özne uyuşması aramak gerekmez:

- "**Strictly speaking**, this is not correct."
- "**Generally speaking**, the situation is improving."
- "**Considering** the circumstances, she did well."
- "**Given that** prices have risen, we must cut costs."
- "**Judging by** his face, he was not pleased."
- "**Assuming** everything goes well, we'll be done by Friday."
- "**Having said that**, there are still some concerns." (söylev işaretçisi)
- "**Taking everything into account**..."
- "**Providing/Provided** (that)..." — koşul ifadesi

### 3. Passive Participle Clause

- "**Being told** to leave, he was upset." (= Because he was told to leave)
- "**Seen from a distance**, the castle looks magnificent."
- "**Faced with** no other option, she agreed."

### 4. Absolute Participle Clauses — Farklı Özne

Genellikle kabul edilen bir istisna: "with + noun + participle" yapısı (absolute clause):

- "She sat there, **with her eyes closed**."
- "He spoke quietly, **with his hands shaking**."
- "**The meeting over**, everyone left quickly." (literary/formal)
- "**Weather permitting**, we'll have the barbecue outside."

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "**Hearing the news**, she burst into tears." — Haberi duyunca (neden)
2. "**Not having an umbrella**, I got completely soaked." — Şemsiyem olmadığı için
3. "He sat at the table, **staring at the wall**." — eş zamanlı eylem
4. "**Trained as a lawyer**, she knew exactly what to say." — geçmişte eğitilmiş (past participle)
5. "**Having spent** all his money, he had to borrow some." — Önce harcadı, sonra borç istedi
6. "**Having been warned** about the danger, they took a different route." — Passive perfect
7. "The car **parked outside** belongs to my neighbour." — reduced relative clause (passive)
8. "The woman **sitting in the corner** is the CEO." — reduced relative clause (active)
9. "**While preparing** for the exam, she found several useful resources."
10. "**Before submitting** the form, read the instructions carefully."
11. "**Given the evidence**, the verdict seems clear." — sabit ifade
12. "**Strictly speaking**, you should have informed us in advance."
13. "She left early, **thus avoiding** the rush-hour traffic." — sonuç
14. "**Exhausted after the marathon**, he slept for twelve hours."
