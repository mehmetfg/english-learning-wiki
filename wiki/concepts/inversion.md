# Inversion — Devrik Cümle Yapısı

Inversion (devrik cümle yapısı), normal İngilizce cümle sırasının (özne + yardımcı fiil) tersine çevrilmesidir: **yardımcı fiil + özne + yüklem**. Vurgu, resmiyet ve belirli zarfsal ifadeler nedeniyle kullanılır. Özellikle yazılı dilde, edebi metinlerde ve resmi konuşmalarda karşılaşılır.

**Normal sıra:** I have never seen such chaos.
**Inversion:** Never **have I** seen such chaos.

---

## Yapı / Structure

**Temel Yapı:** [Zarf/Bağlaç] + yardımcı fiil + özne + ana fiil

- Yardımcı fiil varsa: sadece yardımcı fiil öne alınır
- Yardımcı fiil yoksa: "do/does/did" eklenir (Simple Present/Past için)

**Örnekler:**
- "Rarely **does** she make mistakes." (= She rarely makes mistakes.)
- "Never **have I** felt so alive." (= I have never felt so alive.)
- "Not only **did** he lie..." (= He not only lied...)

---

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### 1. Olumsuz Zarflar Cümle Başında (Negative Adverbials)

Bu zarflar veya zarfsal ifadeler cümle başına geldiğinde ZORUNLU inversion uygulanır.

#### Never / Rarely / Seldom / Barely / Scarcely / Hardly

- "**Never have I** seen such a mess."
- "**Rarely does** he make mistakes."
- "**Seldom do** we get the chance to relax."
- "**Barely had** she sat down when the phone rang."
- "**Scarcely had** he arrived when trouble started."
- "**Hardly had** I fallen asleep when the alarm went off."

#### Hardly/Scarcely...when, No sooner...than

Geçmişte art arda yaşanan olaylar — Past Perfect + inversion:

- "**Hardly had I arrived when** the trouble started." (= I had hardly arrived when...)
- "**No sooner had she left than** he appeared." (= She had no sooner left than...)
- "**Scarcely had** they settled in **when** the next crisis hit."

**DİKKAT:** "than" (No sooner...than) / "when" (Hardly/Scarcely...when) — karıştırılmamalı:
- "No sooner...than" ✓ / ~~"No sooner...when"~~ ✗

#### Not only...but also

- "**Not only did he lie**, but he also stole money."
- "**Not only is she** talented, but she is also incredibly hardworking."
- "**Not only have they** failed to deliver, but they've also missed every deadline."

#### Not until

- "**Not until I saw** her face did I realize something was wrong."
- "**Not until he was forty** did he learn to drive."
- "**Not until the very end** did the truth emerge."

#### At no time / Under no circumstances / On no account / In no way / Nowhere

- "**At no time did** she apologize for her behaviour."
- "**Under no circumstances should** you open that door."
- "**On no account are** you to tell anyone about this."
- "**In no way is** this acceptable."
- "**Nowhere have** I felt more at home than here."

#### Little

"Little" olumsuz anlam taşıdığında inversion yapılır:
- "**Little did he know** what was about to happen." (Neredeyse hiç bilmiyordu ne olacağını.)
- "**Little did she realize** how important that decision would be."
- "**Little do they know** that we're planning a surprise."

---

### 2. Only + Zarfsal İfade Cümle Başında

"Only" + zarf/zarf öbeği/bağımlı cümle cümle başına geldiğinde:

- "**Only then did** I understand the truth."
- "**Only now do** I realize my mistake."
- "**Only when I read** the letter did I realize the truth." (= only + zaman cümlesi)
- "**Only by working hard can** you succeed."
- "**Only in this way can** the problem be solved."
- "**Only after the meeting did** she feel reassured."
- "**Only once** had she visited the city before."

**DİKKAT:** "Only" tek başına bir ismi/nesneyi vurgularsa inversion YOKTUR:
- "**Only John** knew the answer." (John'u vurguluyor, inversion yok)
- "**Only the president** can authorize this." (inversion yok)

---

### 3. So / Neither / Nor — Olumlu ve Olumsuz Ekleme

Bir önceki cümleye katılmak için kısa inversion yapıları:

**Olumlu cümleye katılmak (So + yardımcı + özne):**
- "I like coffee." → "**So do I.**"
- "She has finished." → "**So have I.**"
- "He was tired." → "**So was she.**"
- "They will come." → "**So will we.**"

**Olumsuz cümleye katılmak (Neither/Nor + yardımcı + özne):**
- "I don't like tea." → "**Neither/Nor do I.**"
- "She hasn't called." → "**Neither has he.**"
- "He wasn't there." → "**Nor was she.**"

**DİKKAT — "me too / me neither" ile karışma:**
- "I love this film." → "**Me too.**" (informal) / "**So do I.**" (formal)
- "I don't understand it." → "**Me neither.**" (informal) / "**Neither do I.**" (formal)

---

### 4. Koşul Cümlelerinde Inversion (Conditional Inversion)

"If" kaldırılır, yardımcı fiil öne alınır. Resmi ve yazı dilinde yaygın.

| Koşul Türü | If'li Normal Hali | Inversion ile Formal Hali |
|---|---|---|
| **Type 1** | "If you should need help, call me." | "**Should you need** help, call me." |
| **Type 2** | "If I were you, I would apologize." | "**Were I you**, I would apologize." |
| **Type 3** | "If I had known, I would have called." | "**Had I known**, I would have called." |

**Daha fazla örnek:**
- "**Should you require** any assistance, do not hesitate to contact us."
- "**Were it not for** your support, I would have failed." (= If it were not for your support)
- "**Were she** to resign, the company would be in trouble."
- "**Had we left** earlier, we would have caught the train."
- "**Should the situation** change, please notify us immediately."

---

### 5. Yer Zarfları Cümle Başında (Place Adverbials — İngilizce/Edebi)

Geçişsiz (intransitive) fiillerle, edebi dilde:

- "**On the sofa sat** a large cat." (= A large cat sat on the sofa.)
- "**Here comes** the bus!" (= The bus comes here.)
- "**There goes** the last train." (= The last train goes there.)
- "**Round the corner came** a cyclist."
- "**Into the room walked** the detective."

**KRİTİK KURAL:** Özne ZAMİR ise inversion YAPILMAZ:
- "**Here he comes.**" ✓ (NOT: ~~Here comes he.~~ ✗)
- "**There she goes.**" ✓ (NOT: ~~There goes she.~~ ✗)
- "**Here it is.**" ✓ — fakat "Here is the book." ✓ (isim → inversion var)

---

### 6. Doğrudan Alıntı Sonrasında (After Direct Speech) — Edebi

Modern İngilizce'de nadir, edebi metinlerde:
- "'I am exhausted,' **said she**." — arkaik/edebi
- "Modern kullanım: 'I am exhausted,' **she said**." — standart

---

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### 1. "So + Sıfat" → Inversion YOK

"So" bir sıfatı güçlendirdiğinde (emphasizer olarak) inversion olmaz:
- "**So beautiful** was the sunset that we stopped to watch." ✓ (edebi — inversion VAR, fakat bu özel yapı)
- "It was **so** beautiful." ✓ (normal — inversion yok)
- "**Such was** her determination that she succeeded." ✓ — "such" başta → inversion VAR

### 2. "Such" Cümle Başında → Inversion VAR

- "**Such was** her determination that she succeeded."
- "**Such is** the power of habit."
- "**Such were** the circumstances that we had no choice."

### 3. "Never before" — İkili Vurgu

- "**Never before had I** felt so alive."
- "**Never again will I** make that mistake."
- "**Never in my life have I** been so embarrassed."

### 4. "No sooner" → Past Perfect Zorunlu

Informal konuşmada Simple Past kullanılabilir, ama resmi dilde Past Perfect:
- "**No sooner had** she arrived **than** the meeting started." ✓ (formal)
- "**No sooner did** she arrive **than** the meeting started." — (informal alternatif)

### 5. "Not only" — İkinci Cümlede Inversion YOK

- "**Not only did he lie**, but he also stole." ✓
- "Not only did he lie, but **also he stole**." ✓ (ikinci cümlede normal sıra)
- ~~"Not only did he lie, but also did he steal."~~ ✗

### 6. Vurgulu Ön Konumlandırma (Fronting) vs Inversion

"Beautiful she was." — sıfat öne alınmış, ama inversion YOK (yardımcı fiil özne önünde değil). Bu "emphatic fronting" — cleft cümleden farklı, inversion'dan farklı:
- "**Beautiful she was**, no one could deny it." (arkaik/edebi)
- "**This I cannot accept.**" — nesne öne alınmış, inversion yok

### 7. "Only if" vs "Only when" — Her İkisi de Inversion Yapar

- "**Only if you study hard will you** pass." ✓
- "**Only when** she left **did** he realize her value." ✓
- "If only I had known!" — "if only" = keşke (inversion YOK — dilek ifadesi)

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

1. "**Never have I** encountered such rudeness in a professional setting."
2. "**Rarely does** the committee meet more than twice a year."
3. "**No sooner had** the concert ended **than** the crowd rushed for the exits."
4. "**Not only did** the team lose, but they were also relegated."
5. "**Not until** she retired **did** she have time to travel."
6. "**Under no circumstances should** passengers leave their bags unattended."
7. "**Little did** anyone suspect that the butler was innocent."
8. "**Only after** years of practice **did** the technique become second nature."
9. "**Only by** cooperating **can** we hope to solve this crisis."
10. "**Should you** encounter any problems, please call our helpline."
11. "**Were I** in your position, I would take the offer."
12. "**Had they** listened to the warnings, the disaster could have been avoided."
13. "**Such was** the chaos that nobody knew what to do."
14. "I enjoy jazz. **So does** my sister."
15. "He can't swim. **Neither can** I."
16. "**Here comes** the bride!"
17. "**Never before had** the stadium seen such an extraordinary performance."
