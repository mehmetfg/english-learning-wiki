# Reported Speech — Dolaylı Anlatım

Dolaylı anlatım (reported speech / indirect speech), birinin söylediği sözü doğrudan aktarmak yerine kendi cümlenize dahil etmektir. Türkçede "-dığını söyledi, -acağını belirtti" gibi yapılarla karşılık bulur. İngilizcede zaman kaydırması (backshift), zamir değişimleri ve kelime sırası değişiklikleri dikkat gerektirir.

---

## Yapı / Structure

### TENSE BACKSHIFT — Zaman Kaydırması

Raporlama fiili (said, told, asked vb.) **geçmiş zamandaysa**, aktarılan söz de bir adım geriye kayar.

| Doğrudan Anlatım | Dolaylı Anlatım | Kural |
|---|---|---|
| "I **like** it." | She said she **liked** it. | Present Simple → Past Simple |
| "I **am working**." | She said she **was working**. | Present Continuous → Past Continuous |
| "I **worked** hard." | She said she **had worked** hard. | Past Simple → Past Perfect |
| "I **have seen** it." | She said she **had seen** it. | Present Perfect → Past Perfect |
| "I **had finished**." | She said she **had finished**. | Past Perfect → değişmez |
| "I **will** help." | She said she **would** help. | will → would |
| "I **can** do it." | She said she **could** do it. | can → could |
| "It **may** rain." | He said it **might** rain. | may → might |
| "I **must** go." | He said he **had to** go. | must → had to |
| "I **shall** try." | She said she **would** try. | shall → would |
| "**Would/Could/Might/Should**" | Değişmez | Modal geçmişleri değişmez |

---

## Kullanım Alanları / Uses

### Backshift YAPILMAZ

**1. Raporlama fiili present (şimdiki) zamandaysa:**
> "He **says** he is tired." — Yorgun olduğunu söylüyor.

**2. Çok yakın zamanda söylenmiş söz:**
> "She just said she **is** on her way." — Az önce yolda olduğunu söyledi.

**3. Genel gerçekler / bilimsel olgular:**
> "The teacher said that water **boils** at 100°C."

**4. Hâlâ geçerli olan söz:**
> "She said she **lives** in Paris." — Hâlâ Paris'te yaşıyor.

---

### REPORTING VERBS — Raporlama Fiilleri

**V + that-clause:**
claim, admit, deny, agree, announce, complain, explain, boast, confess, insist, promise, warn

> "He **admitted** (that) he had made a mistake."
> "She **denied** (that) she had taken the money."
> "They **promised** (that) they would help."

**V + sb + that-clause:**
tell, remind, warn, inform, convince, assure, persuade

> "She **reminded** me that the deadline was on Friday."
> "He **warned** us that the roads were icy."

**V + sb + to-infinitive:**
tell, ask, order, beg, urge, warn, advise, remind, encourage, invite, forbid, instruct

> "She **told** him **to leave**."
> "He **asked** me **to wait**."
> "The doctor **advised** her **to rest**."

**V + V-ing:**
suggest, recommend, admit, deny, propose, mention, confess

> "She **suggested** going to the cinema."
> "He **admitted** stealing the money."

### PRONOUN & TIME/PLACE CHANGES

**Zamirler:**
| Doğrudan | Dolaylı |
|---|---|
| I | he/she |
| me | him/her |
| my | his/her |
| we | they |
| you | I/he/she/they (bağlama göre) |

**Zaman ve yer ifadeleri:**
| Doğrudan | Dolaylı |
|---|---|
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before |
| tomorrow | the next day |
| this week | that week |
| last week | the week before |
| next week | the following week |
| here | there |
| this | that |
| these | those |
| ago | before |
| tonight | that night |

> "I'll see you **tomorrow**." → She said she would see him **the next day**.
> "I was **here** **yesterday**." → He said he had been **there** **the day before**.

### QUESTIONS in Reported Speech

Dolaylı sorularda **kelime sırası değişir** (inversion yok), soru işareti kullanılmaz.

**Wh- Questions:**
> "Where **do** you live?" → She asked me where I **lived**.
> "What **did** he say?" → She asked what he **had said**.
> "Why **did** you leave early?" → She asked why I **had left** early.

**Yes/No Questions → if / whether:**
> "**Are** you coming?" → He asked **if** I was coming.
> "**Did** she finish?" → She asked **whether** he **had finished**.
> "**Can** you help me?" → She asked **if** I **could** help her.

**Commands → tell/ask + sb + to-infinitive:**
> "**Leave** immediately!" → He **ordered** them **to leave** immediately.
> "**Don't** touch that!" → He **warned** them **not to** touch it.

## Edge Cases & Yaygın Hatalar

### 1. "Still true" — Backshift isteğe bağlı
> "She said she **is** a teacher." — Hâlâ öğretmen (mantıksal olarak doğru)
> "She said she **was** a teacher." — O sırada öğretmendi (artık olmayabilir)

### 2. Past Perfect değişmez
> "I had already eaten." → She said she **had already eaten**. (değişmez)

### 3. Soru cümlesinde kelime sırası hatası
> ❌ "She asked where **was** he going."
> ✓ "She asked where **he was** going."

### 4. Zaman zarfı değişikliğini unutmak
> ❌ "She said she would call me **tomorrow**."
> ✓ "She said she would call me **the next day**."

### 5. "Whether" vs "if"
> "I'm not sure **if** he's coming." ✓
> "I'm not sure **whether** he's coming (or not)." ✓ (daha resmi)
> "**Whether or not** he comes, we'll start." ✓
> ❌ "**If or not** he comes, we'll start." (yanlış)

### 6. Reporting verb pattern hataları
> ❌ "She suggested **to go** to the park."
> ✓ "She suggested **going** to the park."

> ❌ "She told **that** she was tired." (tell + sb zorunlu)
> ✓ "She told **me** that she was tired."
> ✓ "She **said** (that) she was tired."

> ❌ "She **said** me that she was happy." (said'den sonra nesne gelmez)
> ✓ "She **told** me that she was happy."

## Örnek Cümleler / Examples

**Backshift — Statement:**
1. "I love jazz." → He said he **loved** jazz. — Cazı sevdiğini söyledi.
2. "We are planning a trip." → She said they **were planning** a trip.
3. "I finished the report." → He told me he **had finished** the report.
4. "She has never been to Japan." → He said she **had never been** to Japan.
5. "I will call you tonight." → She said she **would** call me **that night**.
6. "It might snow tomorrow." → He said it **might** snow **the next day**.

**No Backshift — General truth:**
7. "The sun rises in the east." → The teacher said the sun **rises** in the east.

**Questions:**
8. "Where do you work?" → She asked me where I **worked**.
9. "Have you eaten?" → He asked **if** I **had** eaten.
10. "Will you marry me?" → He asked her **whether** she **would** marry him.

**Commands:**
11. "Open the window!" → She **told** him **to open** the window.
12. "Don't be late!" → He **warned** us **not to** be late.

**Reporting verbs:**
13. "I'm sorry, I broke the vase." → She **admitted** (that) she **had broken** the vase.
14. "I didn't steal anything!" → He **denied** stealing anything.
15. "Why don't we take a break?" → She **suggested** taking a break.
