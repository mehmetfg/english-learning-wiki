## 8 Gramer Yapısı (Felsefe metnine uygun seçilmiş)

1. **Past Simple (Narrative)** — biyografi/anlatı için temel; düzensiz fiil vurgusu
2. **What if... (Hypothetical Question)** — makalenin açılışı; Türkçe "ya...sa?" yapısı
3. **Reported Speech (Basic)** — "He said that he could not be certain..."
4. **Gerunds vs Infinitives** — "decided to doubt" / "started thinking" / "enjoy reading"
5. **Even if / Even though** — "Even if a demon was tricking him..."
6. **Cannot — Logical Impossibility** — Descartes\'in çekirdek argümanı: "you cannot doubt without being"
7. **Cause/Effect Linkers** — so / because / that\'s why / therefore
8. **"That" Clauses** — "He realised that...", "I think that..."
