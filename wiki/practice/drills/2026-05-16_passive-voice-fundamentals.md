# Passive Voice — Gap-Fill Drill (10-Q)

**Talimat:** Her cümlede parantezdeki fiili, köşeli parantezdeki ipucuna göre doğru passive (veya — dikkat — gerekiyorsa active) forma çevirip boşluğa yaz.

İlgili kavram: [[Passive Voice Concept]] · [[Passive Infinitives and Gerunds]]

---

## Sorular

1. The Mona Lisa __________ (paint) by Leonardo da Vinci. *[past simple passive]*

2. Look! A new bridge __________ (build) over the river right now. *[present continuous passive]*

3. The package __________ (already / deliver) to the wrong address. *[present perfect passive]*

4. The results __________ (announce) next Monday. *[future "will" passive]*

5. Helmets __________ (must / wear) on the construction site at all times. *[modal passive]*

6. This contract needs __________ (sign) before Friday. *[passive infinitive]*

7. She hates __________ (interrupt) while she is speaking. *[passive gerund]*

8. The minister __________ (say) to have resigned over the scandal. *[reporting structure — perfect form]*

9. My grandfather __________ (bear) in a small village in 1942. *["born" — permanent passive]*

10. ⚠️ A serious accident __________ (happen) on the highway yesterday morning. *[dikkat: bu fiil intransitive — uygun formu seç]*

---

## Cevap Anahtarı

> Aşağıdaki bölümü cevaplarını yazdıktan sonra aç.

<details>
<summary>Cevapları göster</summary>

1. **was painted**
   *Past simple passive: was/were + V3.*

2. **is being built**
   *Present continuous passive: is/are + being + V3.*

3. **has already been delivered**
   *Present perfect passive: has/have + been + V3; "already" yardımcı ile V3 arasında.*

4. **will be announced**
   *Future "will" passive: will + be + V3.*

5. **must be worn**
   *Modal passive: modal + be + V3. ("should be worn" / "have to be worn" da kabul edilir.)*

6. **to be signed**
   *Passive infinitive: to + be + V3. "needs signing" de geçerli alternatif (need + gerund = passive anlam).*

7. **being interrupted**
   *Passive gerund: being + V3. "hate / like / mind / enjoy" sonrası gerund.*

8. **is said**
   *Reporting structure: subject + is said/believed/thought + **to have** + V3. Geçmiş eylem için "to have resigned" perfect infinitive kullanılır; yardımcı fiil **is said**.*

9. **was born**
   *"Born" sadece passive formda var: was/were born. Active "bore" doğum bağlamında kullanılmaz.*

10. **happened**  ⚠️ TUZAK
    *"Happen" intransitive — passive yapılmaz. Yaygın hata: ❌ *was happened*. Doğru: active **happened**. Aynı kural: arrive, occur, exist, die, fall, rise.*

</details>
