## Kitap Hakkında

**Atomic Habits** is a book about how small habits can change your life. It was written by James Clear and published in 2018. The book became very popular because it explains a simple idea in a clear and practical way. The idea is this: you do not need to make big changes to have a better life. You only need to make very small changes and do them every day. Over time, these tiny changes become very powerful.

The word "atomic" has two meanings. First, it means very small like an atom, the smallest thing in the world. Second, it means very powerful like atomic energy. So an "atomic habit" is a habit that is very small but also very strong.
