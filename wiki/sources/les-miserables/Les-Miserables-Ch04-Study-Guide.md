## 3.1 Past Simple vs. Past Continuous

### The Rule

We use **Past Simple** for completed actions in the story things that happened and finished.
> *Tholomyès left. He wrote a funny letter. He disappeared.*

We use **Past Continuous** for background situations things that were already happening when the main action occurred. It paints the scene.
> *Snow was falling. The machines were working. The candle was burning.*

We also use Past Continuous for a longer action that was **interrupted** by a shorter Past Simple action.
> *She was walking home when Javert stopped her.*

## 3.2 Modal Verbs: COULD and HAD TO

### The Rule

**COULD** = was able to / was possible
> *She could not make the numbers balance.*
> *She could see that the defence lawyer was failing.*

**HAD TO** = was necessary / was obligated (no choice)
> *She had to keep walking. She had no choice.*
> *She had to find cheaper accommodation.*

**COULDN'T** = was not able to / was not possible
> *She couldn't stop sending money to Montfermeil.*

## 3.4 Reported Thoughts: "She thought..." / "She told herself..."

### The Rule

In this chapter, the narrator often shows us what characters are thinking inside their heads. This is called **free indirect thought** but at A2/B1 level, we call it simply **reported thought**.

The pattern is:
> **She thought + that + sentence (tense shifted back)**

Direct thought: *"I have no choice."*
Reported thought: *She thought that she had no choice.*

Direct thought: *"Things will get better."*
Reported thought: *She told herself that things would get better.*

The key verb tense changes:
| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| am / is / are | was / were |
| have / has | had |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| must | had to |

## 3.5 Descriptive Writing: Adjectives and Noun Phrases

### How the Chapter Describes People

Good descriptive writing uses adjectives carefully and specifically. Look at how Chapter 4 describes Fantine:

> *"She had long, golden hair and clear blue eyes. She was thin, not because she was sick, but because she had never had very much food."*

Notice:
- Two adjectives before the noun: **long, golden hair**
- Two adjectives before the noun: **clear blue eyes**
- The writer explains WHY she is thin this makes it more interesting

And Tholomyès:
> *"He was tall, with dark hair and a wide smile. He was clever in a way that some people found impressive and others found exhausting."*

Notice:
- Physical description first, then personality
- A contrast: **some people found X / others found Y** this shows he is complex
