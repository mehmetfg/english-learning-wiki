# 01 How to Get Rich (Without Getting Lucky)

## Giriş

31 Mayıs 2018'de Naval Ravikant, Twitter'da art arda ~40 tweet yayınladı. Başlık: *"How to Get Rich (Without Getting Lucky)"*. Her tweet bir aforizmaydı kısa, sert, tek başına ayakta duran bir cümle. Tweetstorm viral oldu; milyonlarca insan ezberledi, çevirdi, alıntıladı. İki yıl sonra [[naval-almanack/index|The Almanack of Naval Ravikant]] kitabı bu zinciri çekirdek alarak yazıldı.

Aşağıdaki 40 aforizma Naval'ın orijinal sırasıyla verilmiştir. İngilizcesi kasten basittir Naval, karmaşık fikirleri ilkokul kelimesiyle anlatma sanatını ustalıkla uygular. **Bu yüzden "sticky English" için ideal kaynaktır**: hem dil basit, hem fikir derin.

> 💡 Her aforizmayı tek tek oku. Aralarda dur. Birini ezberle. Aşağıdaki glossary'ye terim için bak. Kitabı sırayla baştan sona okur gibi okuma bu **referans** bir metindir.

## The Tweetstorm 40 Aphorisms

### Foundations (1–5)

> **1.** Seek wealth, not money or status. Wealth is having assets that earn while you sleep. Money is how we transfer time and wealth. Status is your place in the social hierarchy.

*(Servet, para ve statü farklı şeylerdir. Servet = sen uyurken kazanan varlık.)*

> **2.** Understand that ethical wealth creation is possible. If you secretly despise wealth, it will elude you.

> **3.** Ignore people playing status games. They gain status by attacking people playing wealth-creation games.

> **4.** You're not going to get rich renting out your time. You must own equity a piece of a business to gain your financial freedom.

> **5.** You will get rich by giving society what it wants but does not yet know how to get. At scale.

### Long-Term Games (6–11)

> **6.** Pick an industry where you can play long-term games with long-term people.

> **7.** The internet has massively broadened the possible space of careers. Most people haven't figured this out yet.

> **8.** Play iterated games. All the returns in life, whether in wealth, relationships, or knowledge, come from compound interest.

*(Hayattaki tüm getiriler bileşik faizden gelir servet, ilişki, bilgi.)*

> **9.** Pick business partners with high intelligence, energy, and, above all, integrity.

> **10.** Don't partner with cynics and pessimists. Their beliefs are self-fulfilling.

> **11.** Learn to sell. Learn to build. If you can do both, you will be unstoppable.

### Specific Knowledge (12–17)

> **12.** Arm yourself with specific knowledge, accountability, and leverage.

> **13.** Specific knowledge is knowledge you cannot be trained for. If society can train you, society can train someone else and replace you.

> **14.** Specific knowledge is found by pursuing your genuine curiosity and passion rather than whatever is hot right now.

> **15.** Building specific knowledge will feel like play to you but will look like work to others.

*(Sana oyun gibi gelen ama başkalarına iş gibi görünen şey orası senin alanın.)*

> **16.** When specific knowledge is taught, it's through apprenticeships, not schools.

> **17.** Specific knowledge is often highly technical or creative. It cannot be outsourced or automated.

### Accountability (18–19)

> **18.** Embrace accountability, and take business risks under your own name. Society will reward you with responsibility, equity, and leverage.

> **19.** The most accountable people have singular, public, and risky brands.

### Leverage (20–28)

> **20.** "Give me a lever long enough, and a place to stand, and I will move the earth." Archimedes

> **21.** Fortunes require leverage. Business leverage comes from capital, people, and products with no marginal cost of replication (code and media).

> **22.** Capital means money. To raise money, apply your specific knowledge with accountability, and show resulting good judgment.

> **23.** Labor means people working for you. It's the oldest and most fought-over form of leverage. Labor leverage will impress your parents, but don't waste your life chasing it.

> **24.** Capital and labor are permissioned leverage. Everyone is chasing capital, but someone has to give it to you. Everyone is trying to lead, but someone has to follow you.

> **25.** Code and media are permissionless leverage. They're the leverage behind the newly rich. You can create software and media that works for you while you sleep.

*(Kod ve medya = izinsiz kaldıraç. Yeni zenginlerin sırrı.)*

> **26.** An army of robots is freely available it's just packed in data centers for heat and space efficiency. Use it.

> **27.** If you can't code, write books and blogs, record videos and podcasts.

> **28.** Leverage is a force multiplier for your judgement.

### Judgement & Learning (29–32)

> **29.** Judgement requires experience, but can be built faster by learning foundational skills.

> **30.** There is no skill called "business." Avoid business magazines and business classes.

> **31.** Study microeconomics, game theory, psychology, persuasion, ethics, mathematics, and computers.

> **32.** Reading is faster than listening. Doing is faster than watching.

### Time & Standards (33–36)

> **33.** You should be too busy to "do coffee," while still keeping an uncluttered calendar.

> **34.** Set and enforce an aspirational personal hourly rate. If saving time costs less than your hourly rate, outsource it. If doing something would earn less than your hourly rate, don't do it.

> **35.** Work as hard as you can. Even though who you work with and what you work on are more important than how hard you work.

> **36.** Become the best in the world at what you do. Keep redefining what you do until this is true.

### Closing (37–40)

> **37.** There are no get-rich-quick schemes. That's just someone else getting rich off you.

> **38.** Apply specific knowledge, with leverage, and eventually you will get what you deserve.

> **39.** When you're finally wealthy, you'll realize that it wasn't what you were seeking in the first place. But that's for another day.

> **40.** Wealth creation is an evolutionarily recent positive-sum game. Status is an old zero-sum game. Those attacking wealth creation are often just seeking status.

## Glossary

### Finance & Business

- **wealth** assets that produce income without your active work | *servet (uyurken kazandıran varlık)*
- **asset** something you own that has economic value, especially one that earns money | *varlık*
- **equity** ownership share in a company; a piece of the business | *hisse, ortaklık payı*
- **capital** money or financial resources used to start or run a business | *sermaye*
- **labor** work done by people; workforce | *emek, işgücü*
- **leverage** a force multiplier; using a small input to produce a large output (financial, mechanical, or strategic) | *kaldıraç, çarpan*
- **compound interest** interest earned on both the original amount and on previously accumulated interest | *bileşik faiz*
- **marginal cost** the cost of producing one additional unit of something | *marjinal maliyet*
- **scale (at scale)** operating at very large size; reaching many people with low extra cost | *büyük ölçek*
- **hourly rate** how much money your time is worth per hour | *saatlik ücret*
- **outsource** to hire someone else to do work for you | *dışarıya yaptırmak*
- **positive-sum game** situation where everyone can win at the same time | *pozitif toplamlı oyun*
- **zero-sum game** situation where one person's gain is another's loss | *sıfır toplamlı oyun*

### Concepts

- **specific knowledge** knowledge unique to you, not teachable in schools, found through genuine curiosity | *kişiye özgü bilgi*
- **accountability** willingness to take responsibility publicly, under your own name | *hesap verebilirlik, sorumluluk üstlenme*
- **judgement** the ability to make good decisions based on experience and reason | *muhakeme, karar verme yetisi*
- **permissioned leverage** leverage that requires someone else's approval (e.g., capital from investors, labor from employees) | *izinli kaldıraç*
- **permissionless leverage** leverage anyone can use without permission (code, media) | *izinsiz kaldıraç*
- **iterated game** a game played many times with the same people, where reputation accumulates | *tekrarlanan oyun*
- **integrity** being honest and having strong moral principles | *dürüstlük, ahlaki bütünlük*
- **status** your social rank or position relative to others | *statü, sosyal konum*
- **rat race** *(Naval'ın doğrudan kullanmadığı ama bu fikre yakın terim bkz. [[Rich-Dad-Poor-Dad-Ch01-Two-Fathers]])* endless competition for money without progress | *fare yarışı*

### Verbs & Phrases (Faydalı Fiil Kalıpları)

- **to elude (someone)** to escape or avoid being grasped/understood | *(bir şey) elinden kaçmak*
- **to rent out (your time)** to sell hours of your life for a wage | *(zamanını) kiraya vermek*
- **to play long-term games** to participate in activities whose rewards come over many years | *uzun vadeli oynamak*
- **to figure out** to understand or discover something | *çözmek, anlamak*
- **to arm yourself with** to equip yourself with (a tool, knowledge, a weapon) | *kendini ... ile donatmak*
- **to embrace (something)** to accept and welcome it willingly | *kucaklamak, gönüllü kabul etmek*
- **to chase (after something)** to pursue, run after | *peşinden koşmak*
- **to redefine** to define again, change the meaning of | *yeniden tanımlamak*
