# 03 Leverage

## Giriş

Specific knowledge'ı buldun. Peki onu nasıl çarparsın? Naval'ın cevabı: **kaldıraç (leverage)**. Archimedes'in "Bana yeterince uzun bir kol ver, dünyayı oynatayım" cümlesiyle açar. Kaldıraç, az girdiyle büyük çıktı almak demektir iş dünyasında bu dört biçimde gelir: **sermaye, emek, kod, medya**.

Bu bölümdeki en kritik ayrım: ilk ikisi *permissioned* (izinli birinin sana izin vermesi lazım), son ikisi *permissionless* (izinsiz hiç kimse sana "hayır" diyemez). İnternetin değiştirdiği şey budur.

---

## Core Aphorisms Temel Aforizmalar

> **1.** "Give me a lever long enough, and a place to stand, and I will move the earth." Archimedes

*(Bu Archimedes alıntısıyla başlar Naval. Kaldıracın evrensel prensibi.)*

> **2.** Fortunes require leverage. Business leverage comes from capital, people, and products with no marginal cost of replication (code and media).

> **3.** Capital means money. To raise money, apply your specific knowledge with accountability, and show resulting good judgment.

> **4.** Labor means people working for you. It's the oldest and most fought-over form of leverage. Labor leverage will impress your parents, but don't waste your life chasing it.

*(Emek, en eski ve en çok savaşılan kaldıraç biçimidir. Anne-babana gösteriş yapar ama ömrünü bunun peşinde harcama.)*

> **5.** Capital and labor are permissioned leverage. Everyone is chasing capital, but someone has to give it to you. Everyone is trying to lead, but someone has to follow you.

> **6.** Code and media are permissionless leverage. They're the leverage behind the newly rich. You can create software and media that works for you while you sleep.

> **7.** An army of robots is freely available it's just packed in data centers for heat and space efficiency. Use it.

*(Ücretsiz bir robot ordusu var sadece veri merkezlerinde sıkıştırılmış hâlde. Onu kullan.)*

> **8.** If you can't code, write books and blogs, record videos and podcasts.

> **9.** Leverage is a force multiplier for your judgement.

> **10.** Judgement especially demonstrated judgement, with high accountability and a clear track record is incredibly valuable.

## Extended Passages Genişletilmiş Açıklamalar

---

> We live in an age of infinite leverage. The question isn't "Can I get leverage?" The question is "Am I applying it to the right thing?" If you have good judgement and a lot of leverage, you can be wildly right. But if you have poor judgement and a lot of leverage, you can be catastrophically wrong.

*(Sonsuz kaldıraç çağındayız. Soru "kaldıraç alabilir miyim?" değil, "onu doğru şeye mi uyguluyorum?" İyi muhakeme + yüksek kaldıraç = büyük kazanç. Kötü muhakeme + yüksek kaldıraç = yıkım.)*

---

> The most interesting form of leverage is products with no marginal cost of replication. If I write a book, the 100,000th copy costs me nothing more than the first copy. A podcast episode, a YouTube video, a piece of software these are assets that can work for millions of people simultaneously.

*(Kopyalamanın ek maliyeti olmayan ürünler: kitap, podcast, YouTube, yazılım. 100.000. kopya sana ilk kopya kadar mal olur.)*

---

> You're not going to get rich with a salaried job. You're just going to be upper middle class at best. That's fine if you want a certain lifestyle, but if you want real wealth, you need an equity stake ownership. And ownership only comes when you apply leverage.

---

> Labor is the worst form of leverage. Why? Because humans are incredibly high-maintenance. They have politics, feelings, salaries, legal obligations. The best founders I know minimize employees. They use capital or code or media as much as possible instead.

*(Emek, en kötü kaldıraç biçimidir. İnsanlar çok fazla bakım gerektirir: siyaset, duygular, maaşlar, yasal yükümlülükler. En iyi kurucular çalışan sayısını minimize eder.)*

---

> The new generation of leverage is different from the old generation. Old-school leverage was: raise money, hire an army, build a factory. New-school leverage is: learn to code, start a podcast, write a newsletter. The new leverage is available to anyone with a computer and an internet connection.

---

> Permissionless leverage is the most powerful concept in wealth creation right now. Nobody has to give you permission to write a blog. Nobody has to give you permission to record a podcast. Nobody has to give you permission to put code on GitHub. You don't have to wait for anyone.

*(İzinsiz kaldıraç blog yazmak için kimsenin izni gerekmez. Podcast kaydetmek için kimsenin izni gerekmez. Kimseyi beklemenize gerek yok.)*

---

> Tools of leverage are freely available. A computer and the internet give you access to all the leverage in the world. The gap between someone who uses these tools and someone who doesn't is getting larger every year.

---

> If you have specific knowledge in a leveraged area, you can be paid well for it. If you have specific knowledge in an unleveraged area, you'll be paid according to how many hours you work. The difference between the two compounds over a lifetime.

*(Kaldıraçlı alandaki specific knowledge = iyi ödeme. Kaldıraçsız alandaki specific knowledge = saat ücretiyle sınırlı. Bu fark bir ömür boyunca bileşik faizle büyür.)*

---

> Code is the most powerful form of permissionless leverage today. A good software engineer can outperform an entire department. Not because engineers are smarter, but because code scales infinitely while people don't.

---

> The internet has democratised leverage. In the old world, if you wanted media leverage, you had to be a journalist at a big newspaper. Now, a teenager in their bedroom can build an audience of millions. That's leverage that didn't exist before.

## Glossary

### Core Concepts

- **leverage** a tool or mechanism that multiplies the effect of your effort | *kaldıraç az girdiyle büyük çıktı*
- **permissioned leverage** leverage that requires another person's approval before you can use it (capital from investors, employees following you) | *izinli kaldıraç*
- **permissionless leverage** leverage you can use without anyone's approval (code, media) | *izinsiz kaldıraç*
- **marginal cost** the additional cost of producing one more unit of something | *marjinal maliyet bir birim daha üretmenin maliyeti*
- **marginal cost of replication** the cost of making one more copy of a product | *kopyalama maliyeti dijital ürünlerde sıfıra yakın*
- **equity stake** ownership percentage in a company | *hisse payı*
- **track record** a record of past achievements or failures used to judge future performance | *geçmiş başarı sicili*
- **accountability** public responsibility; standing behind your decisions with your name and reputation | *hesap verebilirlik*
- **judgement** the ability to make good decisions; sound reasoning | *muhakeme, karar verme yetisi*

### Business & Finance

- **capital** money used to invest or start a business | *sermaye*
- **labor** work done by employees | *emek, işgücü*
- **salaried job** a job that pays a fixed monthly salary | *maaşlı iş*
- **upper middle class** the social tier above middle class but below wealthy | *üst orta sınıf*
- **data center** a large facility containing computer servers | *veri merkezi*
- **GitHub** a platform for hosting and sharing software code | *kod paylaşım platformu*

### Adjectives & Phrases

- **high-maintenance** requiring a lot of attention, effort, or care | *çok bakım isteyen, zahmetli*
- **catastrophically** in a way that causes a sudden, large-scale disaster | *yıkıcı biçimde*
- **wildly** to a great, often surprising degree | *son derece, aşırı biçimde*
- **simultaneously** at the same time | *aynı anda*
- **democratised** made available to everyone equally | *demokratikleştirilmiş*
- **infinitely** without limit | *sonsuz biçimde*

### Verbs

- **to compound** to grow by adding returns on top of returns over time | *bileşik büyümek*
- **to scale** to grow proportionally without proportional cost increase | *ölçeklenmek*
- **to minimize** to reduce to the smallest possible amount | *en aza indirmek*
- **to outperform** to do better than expected, or better than others | *daha iyi performans göstermek*
- **to democratise** to make something available to all people | *demokratikleştirmek*
