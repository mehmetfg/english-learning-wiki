# 02 Specific Knowledge

## Giriş

Naval'ın tüm servet felsefesinin en özgün kavramı budur: **Specific Knowledge**. Diğer yatırımcılar "iyi iş bul, sıkı çalış" der. Naval, bu tavsiyenin neden işe yaramadığını açıklar ve yerine somut bir mekanizma önerir: eğitimle kopyalanamayan, sana özel, gerçek merakından doğan bilgi.

Bu bölümdeki metinler Almanack'in birden fazla bölümünden derlenmiştir tweetstorm'daki aforizma çekirdekleri, Naval Podcast (Ep. 1–2) ve Tim Ferriss Show röportajlarından genişletilmiş açıklamalar. Orijinal İngilizce korunmuştur.

---

## Core Aphorisms Temel Aforizmalar

> **1.** Specific knowledge is knowledge you cannot be trained for. If society can train you, it can train someone else and replace you.

> **2.** Specific knowledge is found by pursuing your genuine curiosity and passion rather than whatever is hot right now.

> **3.** Building specific knowledge will feel like play to you but will look like work to others.

*(Başkalarına iş gibi görünen, sana oyun gibi gelen alan orası senin specific knowledge alanın.)*

> **4.** When specific knowledge is taught, it's through apprenticeships, not schools.

> **5.** Specific knowledge is often highly technical or creative. It cannot be outsourced or automated.

> **6.** The most important skill for getting rich is becoming the best at something. But what's the right something?

## Extended Passages Genişletilmiş Açıklamalar

Aşağıdaki metinler Naval'ın podcast ve röportajlarından alınmış, tweetstorm aforizmalarını açıklayan doğrudan alıntılardır.

---

> The key is to look back at what you've done effortlessly, that others have struggled with. That's usually specific knowledge. Not a formal subject in school more like a natural tendency.

---

> Specific knowledge can't be taught, but it can be learned. There's a difference. You have to go through the experience. You can't shortcut it by reading a textbook.

*(Öğretilemez ama öğrenilebilir. Fark önemli: ders kitabıyla kısayol yok deneyimden geçmek zorundasın.)*

---

> The world is changing so fast that the specific knowledge areas shift. But your specific knowledge is built on curiosity and curiosity doesn't shift. Follow it relentlessly.

---

> Almost all biases come from people wanting to look good to their peers. A lot of "hot careers" are just status games. People study finance or law not because they love it but because their peers respect it. That's the worst possible reason to choose a field.

*(Akranlar ne düşünür kaygısıyla seçilen kariyerler statü oyunudur. Bu, bir alan seçmek için mümkün olan en kötü sebeptir.)*

---

> The internet enables niche interests to turn into large businesses. Things that were too specific to be profitable in your local area are now enough to build an audience worldwide. Forty years ago, you needed to go to a big city to be around people who shared your specific interest. Now you don't.

---

> You want to be the best in the world at what you do. But you have to keep redefining "what you do" until that becomes true. Become the best in the world at being you.

*(Yaptığın şeyin tanımını, en iyi sen olana kadar yeniden tanımlamaya devam et. Sonunda, "sen olmak"ta dünyanın en iyisi olursun.)*

---

> If it entertains you now but will bore you in five years, it's not specific knowledge. But if you want to do it for ten years, it might be your calling.

---

> Specific knowledge is the intersection of three things: what you love, what you're good at, and what the world will pay for. You don't find this by looking at salaries. You find it by paying attention to what you do when you have no obligations.

*(Üç şeyin kesişimi: sevdiğin + iyi olduğun + dünyanın para ödeyeceği. Bunu maaş tablolarına bakarak bulamazsın yükümlülüğün olmadığında ne yaptığına bakarak bulursun.)*

---

> Don't copy someone else's specific knowledge. The reason they're doing well is because they built it over many years. You can learn from them, but you can't just replicate them.

---

> Schools are great at filtering and signaling. They're terrible at actually teaching specific knowledge. The education system was designed to produce factory workers, not specific knowledge workers.

*(Okullar: filtreleme ve sinyal vermekte başarılıdır. Ama specific knowledge öğretmekte berbattır. Eğitim sistemi fabrika işçisi yetiştirmek için tasarlandı specific knowledge işçisi değil.)*

## Glossary

### Core Concepts

- **specific knowledge** unique expertise you built through genuine curiosity; cannot be taught in school or replicated by training others | *kişiye özgü bilgi eğitimle kopyalanamaz, meraktan doğar*
- **apprenticeship** learning by working closely with an expert over time, not by sitting in a classroom | *çıraklık, usta-çırak ilişkisi*
- **calling** a strong sense that a particular job or career is right for you; a vocation | *çağrı, yaşam amacı, meslek*
- **niche** a small, specialized segment; a specific area with a focused audience or market | *niş, özel alan*
- **signal** something that communicates information; in careers, a credential or achievement that tells others about your abilities | *sinyal yeteneklerini başkalarına gösteren işaret*
- **intersection** the point where different things meet or overlap | *kesişim noktası*
- **obligation** a duty or responsibility you are required to fulfil | *yükümlülük*

### Adjectives & Phrases

- **effortlessly** without effort; easily and naturally | *zahmetsizce, kolaylıkla*
- **relentlessly** without stopping or slowing down; persistently | *durmaksızın, ısrarla*
- **automated** done by machines or computers without human effort | *otomatikleştirilmiş*
- **outsourced** given to an outside person or company to do | *dışarıya yaptırılmış*
- **profitable** producing a financial gain; making money | *kârlı*
- **replicate** to make an exact copy of; to reproduce | *kopyalamak, çoğaltmak*

### Verbs

- **to filter** to select or remove items from a group based on criteria | *filtrelemek, elemek*
- **to shortcut** to take a faster route that bypasses normal steps | *kısayol almak*
- **to redefine** to define again in a new way | *yeniden tanımlamak*
- **to shift** to change position, direction, or focus | *kaymak, değişmek*
