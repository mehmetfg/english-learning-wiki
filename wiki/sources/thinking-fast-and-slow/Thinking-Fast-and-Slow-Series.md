## Kitap Hakkında

**Thinking, Fast and Slow** is a book about the human mind. It was written by Daniel Kahneman and published in 2011. Kahneman is a psychologist a scientist who studies how people think and feel. He won the Nobel Prize in Economics in 2002, even though he was never an economist. He won it because his research changed the way we understand how people make decisions.

The book is about one big idea: your mind has two different systems for thinking. One system is fast. It works automatically, without effort. It makes quick decisions based on feelings and patterns. The other system is slow. It is careful, logical, and requires a lot of mental energy.

These two systems often disagree. And very often, the fast system wins even when it is wrong. This causes many mistakes in our thinking. Kahneman calls these mistakes "cognitive biases." Understanding these biases can help you think more clearly, make better decisions, and avoid many common traps.
