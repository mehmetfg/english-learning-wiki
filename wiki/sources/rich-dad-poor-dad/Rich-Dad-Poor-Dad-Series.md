## Kitap Hakkında

**Rich Dad Poor Dad** is a book about money, work, and how people think about their financial lives. It was written by Robert T. Kiyosaki and Sharon Lechter, and it was first published in 1997. The book became one of the best-selling personal finance books in history.

The story begins when Robert is nine years old. He has two very important men in his life. One is his real father his biological father, a smart and educated man. Robert calls him his "poor dad." The other is his best friend's father a businessman with no university degree. Robert calls him his "rich dad."

These two men teach Robert completely different things about money. His poor dad believes that education and a good job are the keys to success. His rich dad believes that understanding how money works is the real key. The book follows Robert as he learns from both men and slowly understands why some people become rich and others do not.

The word "rich" in this book does not only mean having a lot of money. It means having freedom the freedom to choose how you spend your time, where you live, and how you live. The book teaches that this kind of freedom is possible for anyone who learns the right lessons about money.
