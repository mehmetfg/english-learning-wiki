# Decameron
## Day 1, Story 10: Master Alberto and the Leeks
### A2–B1 Version · Faithful Retelling
*Anlatan: Pampinea · Birinci Günün Son Hikâyesi*

---

## 📘 Türkçe Özet

Bologna'da **Master Alberto** adlı yaşlı ve ünlü bir doktor vardır. Yetmişine yaklaşmıştır. Ama zihni tıraşlı bir bıçak gibi keskindir ve kalbi hâlâ sıcaktır.

Bir bahar festivalinde **Madonna Malgherida dei Ghisolieri** adlı güzel, genç ve zeki bir dul kadın görür. Kalbi atlar. O günden sonra Master Alberto o kadını düşünür, kapısının önünden geçer, penceresinin önünde durur.

Malgherida ve arkadaşları bunu fark eder. "Bu yaşlı adam bize aşık mı?" diye gülerler.

Bir gün Malgherida onu akşam yemeğine davet eder. Sofrada genç bir kadın nazikçe ama biraz acımasızca sorar: "Ustam, yaşlı bir adam neden genç bir kadına aşık olur? Bu biraz saçma değil mi?"

Herkes sessizleşir. Master Alberto'nun nasıl yanıt vereceğini merak eder.

Doktor hiç kızmaz. Sakin bir şekilde şunu söyler: "Pırasa gibidir yaşlılık: başı beyaz ama en lezzetli kısmı içinde saklıdır. Genç adamlar güzelliğe bakıp geçer. Yaşlı bir adam ise onun ne kadar nadir ve değerli olduğunu bilir. Ben Madonna Malgherida'yı gerçekten görüyorum. Bu yaşta bu kadar güçlü bir şey hissetmek aslında onur verici bir şeydir."

Sofrada tam bir sessizlik.

O genç kadın artık gülmemektedir. Malgherida Master Alberto'ya nazikçe bakar.

Hiç kimse o gece yaşlılıkla alay etmez.

**Hikâyenin özü:** Birinci Günün son hikâyesidir. Boccaccio burada bir denge kurar: kötülük ve ikiyüzlülük sonunda değil, zarafet ve bilgelik ile bitirir. Gülen her zaman güçlü olan değildir.

## 🇬🇧 The Story in English (A2–B1)

### Part One: An Old Doctor in Love

In the city of Bologna, there was a learned doctor named **Master Alberto**. He was famous throughout the university and the city. He knew medicine, philosophy, and history. Everyone respected him.

He was around seventy years old — white-haired, slow in his walking. But his mind was sharp and his heart was still warm.

One spring day, Master Alberto went to a festival in the city. A long line of people walked through the streets. He stood on the side of the road and watched.

And among the crowd, he saw a woman.

Her name was **Madonna Malgherida dei Ghisolieri**. She was a widow — her husband had died the previous year. She was perhaps thirty-five years old. She was elegant, intelligent, and very beautiful.

Master Alberto looked at her and felt something unexpected. His old heart, which he had believed was finished with such things, jumped in his chest.

From that day, he thought about her.

---

### Part Two: The Old Man at the Window

Malgherida's house was in a good part of the city. Master Alberto began to walk past it every day. Sometimes twice a day. Sometimes he stopped across the road and looked at her window for a few moments.

Malgherida noticed him. Her neighbors noticed. Her friends noticed. They all knew who he was — the famous old doctor. They thought it was funny. And a little ridiculous.

One afternoon, Malgherida decided to invite him. She was having a small tea party for several friends. Master Alberto arrived and sat down. He was charming and intelligent, as always.

After a while, one of the younger women at the table spoke. She had a smile on her face — not entirely a kind one.

"Master Alberto," she said, "we are honoured by your visits. You are a great man of learning. But tell me — is it not a little strange for a man of your age to be in love? Does it not embarrass you? Surely love is a young person's feeling."

She looked around the table. Some of the other women were smiling.

---

### Part Three: The Leeks

Everyone at the table turned to look at Master Alberto. They were curious. Some were ready to laugh.

Master Alberto looked at the young woman. He was not embarrassed. He was not angry. He was thinking.

Then he said: "My dear, let me tell you something about leeks."

The women looked at each other.

"You know the vegetable called a leek?" he continued. "It grows in the garden. It has a white head — the part you see above the ground. But the best and most flavourful part is the root, down below. Cooks know this. The white head is pleasant but ordinary. The root is where the real taste is."

He looked around the table slowly.

"Old men are a little like leeks. Our heads are white, I admit. Perhaps not very attractive to the eye. But inside — inside we have lived through everything. Love, loss, joy, difficulty, regret, and happiness. We know the true value of beauty better than any young man. A young man sees a beautiful woman and takes her for granted. An old man knows that beauty like this is rare. He is grateful for it. He sees it clearly."

He looked at Malgherida directly.

"A woman as wonderful as Madonna Malgherida deserves, at the very least, to be seen. Truly seen, and truly admired. That is what I offer. And I offer it with my whole heart."

---

### Part Four: The Room Went Quiet

The room was silent.

The young woman who had asked the question was looking at the table. Her smile had disappeared completely.

Some of the others looked at their cups. One woman looked at the window. Nobody laughed.

Malgherida looked at Master Alberto for a long moment. Her expression was difficult to read. Then she said, quietly: "Thank you, Master Alberto. You do me great honour."

She meant it.

Master Alberto drank his tea. He stayed for another hour and spoke about books and the spring weather and a new building in the city. He was pleasant and calm and in no hurry.

Then he walked home slowly through the Bologna streets, in the last warm light of the afternoon.

His steps were slow. But his heart was very light.

---

### Epilogue: The End of Day One

This was the last story of the first day.

When Pampinea finished, the other nine listeners sat quietly for a moment. Then they praised the story. They talked about it together. Some said the old doctor was right. Some said the young woman had learned a lesson.

Then the sun began to set behind the hills.

The queen of the first day — Pampinea — stood up. She announced that the first day was complete. Tomorrow, a new king or queen would be chosen. A new theme would begin.

The ten young people stood up from the circle of grass where they had been sitting. They walked toward the beautiful house in the hills — the house they had chosen to escape the plague in Florence. They laughed and talked as they walked. The evening was warm and full of the smell of flowers.

One hundred stories waited. Ninety remained.

## 📒 Mini Dictionary (English–English, 10 Words)

1. **widow** *(noun)* a woman whose husband has died and who has not married again.
2. **ridiculous** *(adjective)* so silly or unusual that it makes people want to laugh; something that seems impossible to take seriously.
3. **leek** *(noun)* a long green and white vegetable with a mild onion-like flavour, often used in cooking soups and stews.
4. **flavourful** *(adjective)* having a strong and pleasant taste; full of good flavour.
5. **take for granted** *(phrase)* to not appreciate something or someone because you expect them to always be there; to not notice their value.
6. **regret** *(noun/verb)* a sad feeling about something you did or did not do in the past; "I regret that" means you wish it had been different.
7. **admire** *(verb)* to look at or think about someone with great respect and pleasure; to find someone impressive or beautiful.
8. **elegant** *(adjective)* graceful and attractive in appearance or style; elegant clothes or an elegant person look polished and refined.
9. **ordinary** *(adjective)* normal and not special or unusual in any way; the opposite of remarkable.
10. **epilogue** *(noun)* a short section at the end of a book or story that tells what happened after the main events.
