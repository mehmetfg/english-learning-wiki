# Uzak Şehir Episode 61: A Story of Danger and Courage

> **Level:** A2 | **Type:** TV Series Reading | **Aired:** 11 May 2026, Kanal D  
> **Characters:** Cihan · Alya · Deniz (Alya's son) · Feyyaz · Engin · Nare · Şahin · Sadakat · Kadir · Pakize · Meryem

---

## 📺 The Story

### Scene 1 The Kidnapping

It was a dark night. Alya and her young son Deniz were not at home. They were gone. Someone **took** them.

Cihan **found out** quickly. He **felt** very scared. He **loved** Alya and Deniz. He **could not** sit and wait. He **had to** find them.

> **Cihan** *(on the phone, voice shaking)*:
> "They are not here. Someone took them. I need to find them now."

He **ran** out of the house. He **started** to look for clues. Every minute **was** important.

---

### Scene 2 Cihan Confronts Feyyaz

Cihan **followed** the clues carefully. One name **came** to his mind: **Feyyaz**.

Feyyaz **was** a dangerous man. He **had** many enemies. Cihan **went** to him.

> **Cihan** *(standing close, eyes serious)*:
> "Where is she, Feyyaz? Where is Alya?"

> **Feyyaz** *(calm, not moving)*:
> "I didn't take her. I don't know where she is."

> **Cihan** *(angry)*:
> "Don't lie to me."

> **Feyyaz** *(looking straight at Cihan)*:
> "I am telling the truth. It wasn't me."

Cihan **watched** Feyyaz's face carefully. He **listened** to his words. Slowly, he **understood**: Feyyaz was telling the truth. He **did not** take Alya.

Cihan **left** the room quickly. He **needed** another answer.

---

### Scene 3 Nare's Tragedy

In a quiet hospital room, **Nare** was lying in bed. She **was** very weak. Her face **was** pale and sad.

The doctors **said** her condition was serious. Şahin and his mother Sadakat **talked** quietly outside the room.

> **Sadakat** *(whispering)*:
> "She cannot keep the baby. It is too dangerous for her."

> **Şahin** *(silent for a moment)*:
> "Then… we must decide. Now."

They **made** the decision without telling Nare. The doctors **took** away her baby.

When Nare **woke up**, she **felt** something was wrong. She **looked** at the nurse.

> **Nare** *(quietly, with fear)*:
> "Where is my baby?"

The nurse **could not** look at her eyes. Nare **understood** everything in that moment.

> **Nare** *(tears falling)*:
> "They took my baby… without asking me."

She **was** heartbroken. A deep anger **grew** inside her anger at Şahin. She **could not** forgive him.

---

### Scene 4 Following Engin

Cihan **stopped** thinking about Feyyaz. He **remembered** another name: **Engin**.

Engin **was** clever and quiet. He **worked** in the shadows. Cihan **started** to follow him secretly.

> **Cihan** *(to himself)*:
> "If Feyyaz didn't take her, then it was Engin. I'm sure of it."

He **watched** Engin's movements for a long time. Then he **saw** something: a small building outside the city.

> **Cihan's friend** *(calling)*:
> "Cihan, I checked the address. It belongs to Engin."

> **Cihan**:
> "That's it. That's where they are."

His heart **beat** fast. He **drove** to the building as quickly as possible.

---

### Scene 5 Alya's Brave Act

Inside the building, Alya and Deniz **were** in a small, dark room. Alya **was** scared, but she **did not** show it.

She **looked** at her son's face. Deniz **was** very afraid.

> **Alya** *(holding Deniz's hand)*:
> "Don't be scared. I am here. We are going to be okay."

> **Deniz** *(crying softly)*:
> "Mum, I'm scared. When is Cihan coming?"

> **Alya**:
> "Soon. Very soon. But right now, I need you to be brave."

Then the door **opened**. One of Engin's men **came** in. He **moved** toward Deniz.

Alya **did not** think. She **stepped** in front of her son.

> **Alya** *(strong voice)*:
> "Don't touch him. Take me instead."

She **put** herself in danger to protect Deniz. It **was** a brave and dangerous act.

---

### Scene 6 Cihan Saves Alya

Cihan **arrived** at the building. He **did not** wait. He **went** inside fast.

There **was** a fight. Cihan **was** strong and fast. He **fought** the men one by one.

> **Engin** *(backing away)*:
> "You can't win. There are too many of us."

> **Cihan** *(breathing hard but not stopping)*:
> "Watch me."

Cihan **found** Alya and Deniz. Alya **was** injured but alive.

> **Cihan** *(kneeling next to Alya)*:
> "I'm here. I've got you. Can you walk?"

> **Alya** *(weak smile)*:
> "I knew you would come."

He **helped** them out of the building. They **were** safe at last.

---

### Scene 7 A Happy Moment: Kadir and Pakize

Later that day, there **was** a small celebration at a family home. **Kadir** sat across from Pakize's family. He **was** nervous but smiling.

> **Kadir** *(formal, respectful)*:
> "I love your daughter. I want to marry her. I promise to take care of her."

Pakize's mother **smiled** and **looked** at her husband.

> **Pakize's Father**:
> "Kadir, you are a good man. You have our blessing."

**Pakize** walked in. When she **saw** Kadir, she **smiled** brightly. It **was** a sweet and happy moment.

> **Pakize** *(quietly, to Kadir)*:
> "I can't believe this is happening."

> **Kadir** *(smiling)*:
> "Believe it. It's real."

The family **laughed** and **talked** together. People **drank** tea and **ate** sweets. Everyone **was** happy.

---

### Scene 8 The Shocking Ending 💥

Pakize **was** carrying coffee cups. Alya and Meryem **were** standing nearby. They **were** all smiling and talking.

Then a loud noise.

A big truck **came** around the corner. It **was** going very fast. The driver **did not** stop.

People **screamed**. They **ran**. But Pakize, Alya, and Meryem **could not** move in time.

The truck **hit** them.

Everything **went** quiet.

Pakize **was** on the ground. Alya **was** on the ground. Meryem **was** on the ground. All three **were** hurt. Blood **was** on the street.

Kadir **ran** to Pakize.

> **Kadir** *(shouting, terrified)*:
> "Pakize! Pakize, open your eyes! Please!"

Cihan **ran** to Alya.

> **Cihan**:
> "Alya! Can you hear me? Alya!"

Nobody **answered**.

The episode **ended** there. Three women. Three lives. Unknown.

## 📚 Grammar Structures

### 1. Simple Past Telling a Story

> **What it is:** We use Simple Past to talk about finished actions in the past.  
> **Form:** verb + -ed (regular) / irregular form (go → went, take → took, find → found)

| Rule | Example from the episode |
|------|--------------------------|
| Affirmative | Cihan **found** out quickly. He **ran** out of the house. |
| Negative | He **did not** take Alya. She **did not** show her fear. |
| Question | **Did** Feyyaz take Alya? **Did** Cihan find them? |

> **Note:** Most story events use Simple Past. It is the default tense for telling a story.

---

### 2. Past Continuous Background Actions

> **What it is:** We use Past Continuous for actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past. It gives background information.  
> **Form:** was/were + verb-ing

| Use | Example from the episode |
|-----|--------------------------|
| Action in progress | Alya and Deniz **were hiding** in a dark room. |
| Background scene | Nare **was lying** in bed. |
| Two actions at the same time | While Kadir **was talking**, Pakize **was smiling**. |

> **Note:** Simple Past interrupts Past Continuous.  
> *Pakize **was carrying** the cups when the truck **hit** her.*

---

### 3. Modal Verbs: must / had to Obligation

> **What it is:** We use **must** for strong obligation now. We use **had to** for obligation in the past.  
> **Note:** "must" has no past form we use "had to" instead.

| Modal | Example |
|-------|---------|
| must (now) | "We **must** decide now." Sadakat |
| had to (past) | Cihan **had to** find them fast. |
| didn't have to | He **didn't have to** fight alone but he did. |

---

### 4. Modal Verbs: could / couldn't Past Ability

> **What it is:** **Could** = was able to. **Couldn't** = was not able to.

| Modal | Example |
|-------|---------|
| couldn't (no ability) | The nurse **couldn't** look at her eyes. |
| couldn't (no ability) | Pakize **couldn't** move in time. |
| could (ability) | Cihan **could** see the fear in her face. |

---

### 5. Connectors: because / so / but / when

> **What they are:** These words connect two ideas.

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **because** | gives a reason | He ran **because** he was scared. |
| **so** | gives a result | He found the clue, **so** he drove there fast. |
| **but** | shows contrast | She was scared, **but** she did not show it. |
| **when** | shows time connection | **When** Nare woke up, she felt something was wrong. |

---

### 6. Adjectives Describing Feelings and Situations

> **What they are:** Adjectives describe nouns. In this episode, many adjectives describe emotions and danger.

| Adjective | Simple meaning | Example |
|-----------|----------------|---------|
| scared | feeling fear | Deniz was very **scared**. |
| brave | not afraid, strong | Alya did a **brave** act. |
| desperate | very worried, no other choice | Cihan was **desperate** to find them. |
| pale | white face, unhealthy | Nare's face was **pale**. |
| heartbroken | very, very sad | Nare was **heartbroken**. |
| terrified | extremely scared | Kadir was **terrified** when he saw Pakize. |
