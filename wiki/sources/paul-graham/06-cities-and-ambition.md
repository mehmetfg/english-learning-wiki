## The Essay Key Passages

### Part 1: Cities Send Messages

Great cities attract ambitious people. You can tell how serious a city is by the quality of the people who want to live there. A city that's truly great will draw people from all over the world who are willing to leave their homes to live there.

But I've noticed something more specific. Each city sends a different message. Not just "come here to succeed" but a particular version of success that the city values.

*(Her büyük şehir farklı bir mesaj gönderiyor. "Buraya gel ve başar" değil şehrin değer verdiği başarının belirli bir versiyonu.)*

---

### Part 2: New York, Boston, Silicon Valley

New York sends the message: **you should be richer.** Whatever you're earning, there are people around you who are earning more, spending more, doing more. The city whispers it constantly: more, more, more.

*(New York'un mesajı: daha zengin olmalısın. Ne kadar kazanıyor olursan ol, çevrende daha fazla kazanan, daha fazla harcayan insanlar var. Şehir bunu sürekli fısıldar: daha fazla, daha fazla, daha fazla.)*

Cambridge and Boston send the message: **you should be smarter.** The city has the highest density of PhDs in the world. You can't go to a dinner party without meeting someone who does research that changes something. The city whispers: are you smart enough? Have you thought hard enough?

Silicon Valley says: **you should be more powerful.** Not powerful in the old sense not politically powerful. Powerful in the sense of having a large effect on the world. Having users, having impact, changing how people work and live.

*(Silikon Vadisi'nin mesajı: daha güçlü olmalısın. Eski anlamda güçlü değil siyasi olarak güçlü değil. Dünyada büyük etki yaratma anlamında güçlü.)*

---

### Part 3: Other Cities

London says: **you should be more sophisticated.** Culture, history, taste. Not making new things but understanding old things deeply.

Paris says: **you should appreciate beauty.** The French concept of "joie de vivre" the joy of living. Not wealth, not power, not fame. Just the ability to live well in the present moment.

*(Paris'in mesajı: güzelliği takdir etmelisin. Zenginlik, güç, şöhret değil sadece şu anda iyi yaşama yeteneği.)*

Washington DC says: **you should be more important.** Not successful in business, not famous in the arts important in the eyes of the powerful. Proximity to power is the currency.

---

### Part 4: Why It Matters

Why does this matter? Because the message a city sends shapes what you value. And what you value shapes what you do. And what you do shapes who you become.

If you live in New York long enough, you start measuring success in money, even if that was never your original intention. If you live in Cambridge long enough, you start measuring success in publications and citations. If you live in Silicon Valley long enough, you start measuring success in users and valuations.

*(Bir şehirde yeterince uzun yaşarsanız, başarıyı onun ölçütüyle ölçmeye başlarsınız bu hiçbir zaman asıl niyetiniz olmasa bile.)*

None of these is wrong. But they're different. And if you want to work on something that doesn't fit the message of the city you're in, you'll be swimming upstream the whole time.

---

### Part 5: Choosing Your Environment

This means environment matters more than most people think. You don't just choose a job or a career. You choose an environment. And the environment you choose will change who you are.

You are not a fixed entity that can be placed anywhere. You are partly a product of your environment. The people around you, the conversations you have, the things that are considered normal these all shape what you think is possible and what you think is worth doing.

*(Siz sabit bir varlık değilsiniz, her yere yerleştirilebilecek. Kısmen çevrenizin ürünüsünüzdür. Çevrenizdeki insanlar, yaptığınız konuşmalar, normal kabul edilen şeyler bunların hepsi neyin mümkün olduğunu ve neyin yapılmaya değer olduğunu düşündüğünüzü şekillendirir.)*

So the question to ask is not just "where can I find a job?" The question is: "what kind of person do I want to become, and which environment will push me in that direction?"

---

### Part 6: Online Communities

There's a new kind of place that sends messages: online communities. A forum for a particular kind of programming sends a message. A community of artists sends a different message.

The internet has made it possible to choose your environment without changing your location. You can be physically in one city but intellectually in another. The messages you receive can be chosen.

*(İnternet, konumunuzu değiştirmeden çevrenizi seçmeyi mümkün kıldı. Fiziksel olarak bir şehirde olabilirsiniz ama entelektüel olarak başka bir yerde. Aldığınız mesajlar seçilebilir.)*

This is a new freedom. But it's also a new responsibility. If the messages you receive shape who you become, then the question of which communities you join online and offline is a very serious one.

## Glossary

- **ambition** a strong desire to achieve something | *hırs, büyük hedef*
- **density** the amount of something in a given area; how closely packed | *yoğunluk*
- **sophisticated** having a refined understanding of culture and ideas | *sofistike, kültürlü, incelikli*
- **proximity** nearness in space or relationship | *yakınlık*
- **currency** *(metaphorical)* something valuable in a particular environment | *para birimi (mecazi: değer taşıyan şey)*
- **upstream** against the current; with extra difficulty | *akışa karşı, zor biçimde*
- **entity** a thing with its own distinct existence | *varlık, birim*
- **valuation** an estimate of how much a company is worth | *değerleme*
- **citation** a reference to a published work; used to measure academic influence | *atıf (akademik)*
- **to whisper** to say something quietly; used here metaphorically for invisible influence | *fısıldamak (mecazi: sessizce etkilemek)*
- **joie de vivre** (French) the joy of living; enthusiasm for life | *yaşam sevinci (Fransızca)*
- **to shape** to influence the form or direction of something | *şekillendirmek*
- **to measure success in** to judge how successful something is using a particular standard | *başarıyı ... ile ölçmek*
