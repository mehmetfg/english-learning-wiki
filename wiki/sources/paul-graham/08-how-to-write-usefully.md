# 08 How to Write Usefully

> *Source: paulgraham.com/useful.html Paul Graham, February 2020*
> *Okuma süresi: ~10 dakika | Kelime sayısı: ~2000 | Zorluk: B1+*

---

## Giriş

Son essay ve en meta olanı: **iyi yazı nedir?** PG'nin kendi yazısını nasıl değerlendirdiği, neden bazı essayların okunmaya değer olduğunu nasıl belirlediği.

Bir yazı doğru olabilir ama öğretici olmayabilir. Öğretici olabilir ama herkes zaten biliyordur. Yeni olabilir ama yanlış olabilir. PG dört boyutlu bir çerçeve kuruyor: **doğru × yeni × önemli × güçlü yazılmış.**

Bu essay aynı zamanda wiki'deki tüm okuma deneyiminin manifestosu. Neden bazı cümleler yapışıyor, neden bazıları yok oluyor bu essay yanıtlıyor.

---

## The Essay Key Passages

### Part 1: The Formula

Useful writing tells people something true and important that they didn't already know, and tells it to them in a way that makes them believe it.

Notice how many conditions that imposes. A lot of writing fails on at least one of them.

*(Kullanışlı yazı, insanlara zaten bilmedikleri doğru ve önemli bir şeyi söyler ve bunu onlara inanmalarını sağlayacak şekilde söyler. Bunun ne kadar çok koşul getirdiğine dikkat edin. Pek çok yazı en az birinden başarısız olur.)*

If you write something that's true but not important, people read it and say "interesting, I suppose." If you write something that's important but not true, you're lying. If you write something true and important but everyone already knows it, why write it? And if you write something true, important, and novel but write it poorly, people won't believe it.

---

### Part 2: Novelty Is the Key

Of the four components true, important, novel, well-written novelty is the most important. Because if something is genuinely novel, the other properties matter more.

When you tell people something they didn't know, it changes their model of the world. Even a small change is valuable. But most writing doesn't do this. Most writing restates what people already know, in different words. That's not useful it's decoration.

*(İnsanlara bilmedikleri bir şeyi söylediğinizde, dünya modellerini değiştiriyor. Küçük bir değişiklik bile değerlidir. Ama çoğu yazı bunu yapmıyor. Çoğu yazı insanların zaten bildiğini farklı kelimelerle yeniden ifade ediyor. Bu kullanışlı değil süsleme.)*

The test for novelty: have you said something that would surprise the reader? Not shock surprise. A mild "hm, I hadn't thought of it that way" is enough.

---

### Part 3: The Importance Test

Not everything novel is worth writing. Something can be new but trivial. The question is: does this matter?

PG's test: imagine telling someone about what you wrote. If their response is "oh, interesting" maybe. If their response is "wait that would change things" that's the target.

*(PG'nin testi: Birine yazdıklarınızı anlattığınızı hayal edin. Cevapları "oh, ilginç" ise belki. Cevapları "bir dakika bu her şeyi değiştirir" ise işte hedef bu.)*

Important writing also has a useful property: it is remembered. People share it. They come back to it. Trivial writing, even if correct, disappears.

---

### Part 4: Truth Is Harder Than It Looks

Most writers try to be interesting. Very few try to be true. By "true" I don't mean "correct" I mean genuinely honest about what you think, including the uncertain parts.

The most useful writing acknowledges complexity. It says "on the one hand... but on the other hand..." It doesn't pretend to resolve everything. It moves the reader forward without claiming to have all the answers.

*(En kullanışlı yazı karmaşıklığı kabul eder. "Bir yandan... ama öte yandan..." der. Her şeyi çözdüğünü iddia etmez. Okuyucuyu ileriye taşır ama tüm yanıtlara sahip olduğunu iddia etmez.)*

Dishonest writing writing that sounds confident but isn't is one of the most common failures. You can usually feel it: the author is performing certainty they don't have.

---

### Part 5: Writing Well

The last component is being well-written. This is the most mechanical. It can be learned. But it requires attention.

Good writing is short. Every sentence that doesn't add information should be cut. Long sentences that could be short sentences are waste.

Good writing is concrete. Abstract writing is forgettable. Concrete writing with specific examples, real names, actual numbers stays with the reader.

*(İyi yazı somuttur. Soyut yazı unutulabilir. Somut yazı belirli örnekler, gerçek isimler, gergerçek sayılarla okuyucuda kalır.)*

Good writing is honest. It doesn't use language to obscure it uses language to reveal. If you find yourself using long words where short ones would do, or passive voice where active voice would be clearer, you're probably trying to hide something.

---

### Part 6: The Surprising Test

PG's most useful heuristic: write something that will surprise people. Not shock. Not offend. Surprise.

If you read back what you wrote and none of it surprises you, you haven't said anything new. You've just restated the obvious. Delete it and start again.

*(Yazdıklarınızı okuduğunuzda hiçbiri sizi şaşırtmıyorsa, yeni bir şey söylememişsinizdir. Sadece belirgin olanı yeniden ifade ettiniz. Silin ve baştan başlayın.)*

The target: a reader who is smart but doesn't know your specific subject should feel, after reading, like they learned something real. Not just information a new way of seeing something.

---

### Part 7: PG's Own Practice

PG writes his essays by exploring. He doesn't start with a thesis he starts with a question. He writes to find out what he thinks, not to demonstrate what he already thinks.

This is why his essays feel alive. He's not performing knowledge he's discovering it in real time on the page.

*(PG deneme yazılarını keşfederek yazar. Tez ile başlamaz soru ile başlar. Ne düşündüğünü bulmak için yazar, zaten bildiklerini göstermek için değil.)*

The most useful essays are the ones written by someone who had a genuine question and followed it. Not someone who had an answer and worked backwards.

---

## ⭐ Sticky Sentences Ezberlenecek 7 Cümle

1. **Useful writing tells people something true and important they didn't already know.**
2. **Most writing restates what people already know in different words. That's decoration, not writing.**
3. **Novelty is the key component. Even a small "hm, I hadn't thought of it that way" is enough.**
4. **Important writing is remembered. Trivial writing disappears.**
5. **Good writing is short, concrete, and honest.**
6. **If none of what you wrote surprises you, you haven't said anything new.**
7. **Write to find out what you think not to demonstrate what you already think.**

## Glossary

- **novel** *(adjective)* new and original; not seen before | *yeni, özgün*
- **novelty** the quality of being new or unusual | *yenilik, özgünlük*
- **trivial** not important; of little significance | *önemsiz*
- **to restate** to say something again in different words | *yeniden ifade etmek*
- **decoration** something that looks nice but adds no substance | *süsleme (mecazi: içeriksiz)*
- **to obscure** to make unclear or hard to understand | *karartmak, gizlemek*
- **to reveal** to make something previously unknown visible or known | *açığa çıkarmak, ortaya koymak*
- **concrete** *(writing)* specific, with real examples and details | *somut, belirli*
- **abstract** *(writing)* general, without specific details | *soyut, genel*
- **thesis** the central argument or claim of a piece of writing | *tez, ana iddia*
- **to acknowledge** to accept or admit the truth of something | *kabul etmek*
- **passive voice** grammatical structure where the subject receives the action | *edilgen çatı*
- **active voice** grammatical structure where the subject performs the action | *etken çatı*
- **to pretend** to act as if something is true when it isn't | *gibi davranmak*
- **heuristic** a practical rule used to make decisions quickly | *pratik kural, sezgisel yol*
- **performing certainty** acting more confident than you actually are | *kesinlik performansı göstermek*

## Pratik Egzersiz

1. **Kullanışlılık testi:** Bugün öğrendiğin bir şeyi düşün. 4 soruyu sor:
   - *"Is it true? Is it important? Is it novel to me? Could I explain it well?"*

2. **Sürpriz yaz:** Şu an aklında olan bir fikri 3 cümleyle yaz. Seni şaşırttı mı?
   *"I just wrote: '________.' This surprised me because ________."*

3. **PG testi:** Bu essay'lerden öğrendiğin en önemli şey neydi?
   *"The most novel idea I got from PG essays was ________. It changed how I think about ________."*
