BE SUPPOSED TO — Gereklilik & Beklenti

İngilizce gramerde zorunluluk, beklenti, planlama ve sosyal normları ifade etmek için kullanılan temel bir modal ifade.

B1–B2 Seviye Modal Expression Obligation & Expectation Social Rules
Yapı (Structure)
Olumlu (Affirmative)
Subject + am / is / are + supposed to + V(base form)
Olumsuz (Negative)
Subject + am / is / are + NOT supposed to + V(base form)
Soru (Question)
Am / Is / Are + Subject + supposed to + V(base form)?
Zaman Çekimleri (Tenses)
Zaman Yapı Örnek Anlam
Present am/is/are supposed to She is supposed to call me tonight. Bu gece beni araması gerekiyor / bekleniyor.
Past was/were supposed to He was supposed to arrive at 9. 9'da gelmesi gerekiyordu (ama gelmedi — ima var).
Present Neg. am/is/are not supposed to You aren't supposed to smoke here. Burada sigara içmemen gerekiyor / yasak.
Past Neg. wasn't/weren't supposed to I wasn't supposed to tell you. Sana söylemem gerekmiyordu / yasaktı.
Question Is/Are ... supposed to? Are we supposed to wear uniforms? Üniforma giymemiz gerekiyor mu?
Past Question Was/Were ... supposed to? Was she supposed to finish it? Onu bitirmesi gerekiyordu mu?
Past Supposed To — Önemli Nokta
Was/Were supposed to genellikle gerçekleşmemiş bir beklentiyi veya ihlal edilen bir kuralı ima eder. Örneğin: "He was supposed to call" → "ama aramadı" anlamı taşır. Bu, İngilizcede çok önemli bir anlam farkıdır.
İpucu: "was/were supposed to" + but... kalıbı çok yaygındır: "She was supposed to meet us, but she forgot."
6 Temel Kullanım Anlamı
1. Beklenti / Expectation
Birinin bir şeyi yapmasının beklendiğini ifade eder. Çoğunlukla sosyal veya kurumsal bir beklentidir.
Students are supposed to submit their homework on time.
Öğrencilerin ödevlerini zamanında teslim etmesi beklenir.
2. Kural / Sosyal Norm
Toplumsal kuralları, yasaları veya yönetmelikleri anlatır. "Yasak" veya "zorunlu" manasında kullanılır.
You aren't supposed to park here.
Buraya park etmemen gerekiyor / yasak.
3. Plan / Arrangement
Önceden yapılmış planları veya düzenlemeleri ifade eder. "Planlandı, kararlaştırıldı" anlamı taşır.
The train is supposed to leave at 6:30.
Trenin 6:30'da kalkması planlanmış.
4. İnanış / İtibar (Reputed to)
Bir şeyin yaygın inanışa göre doğru veya öyle olduğunu söyler. "Denilir ki, biliniyor ki" manasındadır.
This restaurant is supposed to be excellent.
Bu restoranın mükemmel olduğu söylenir.
5. Geçmişte Gerçekleşmemiş Eylem
Olması gereken ama olmayan bir şeyi ifade eder. Hayal kırıklığı, eleştiri veya özür içerir.
He was supposed to fix it yesterday. (But he didn't.)
Dün tamir etmesi gerekiyordu. (Ama etmedi.)
6. Görev / Rol (Role-based duty)
Birinin rolü veya görevi gereği yapması gereken şeyleri anlatır. Profesyonel veya sosyal roller için kullanılır.
Doctors are supposed to keep patient information confidential.
Doktorların hasta bilgilerini gizli tutması gerekir.

YapıGüçKaynakTürkçeÖrnek
must ●●● Çok güçlü Konuşanın kendi kuralı -meli / -malı (kesin) You must wear a seatbelt.
have to ●●● Güçlü Dışsal zorunluluk zorunda olmak I have to finish this report.
should ●● Orta Tavsiye / ahlaki beklenti -meli / öneri You should eat more vegetables.
be supposed to ●● Orta Dışsal beklenti / kural / plan -ması gerekiyor / bekleniyor You're supposed to be here at 9.
ought to ●● Orta Ahlaki zorunluluk (resmi) -malı (ahlaki) You ought to apologize.
had better ●●● Uyarı Tehdit içeren tavsiye ...sana göre yap / yoksa... You'd better not be late again.
need to ●● Güçlü Pratik gereklilik gereksinim duymak You need to see a doctor.
Fark nedir? "You must be quiet" → konuşan kural koyuyor. "You are supposed to be quiet" → dışarıdan gelen bir kural var (kütüphane kuralı, vs.). Bu ayrım çok önemlidir!
Should vs. Be supposed to: "You should call her" = benim tavsiyem. "You are supposed to call her" = bir anlaşma ya da kural var ve bunu yapmak senin görevin.

Benzer ve Bağlantılı Yapılar

"Be supposed to" ile aynı kategoride ya da bağlantılı olan yapılar — bunları öğrenmek tüm gereklilik sistemini pekiştirir.

be meant to
Amaçlanan, tasarlanan anlamı taşır. "supposed to"ya çok yakın ama "tasarım/amaç" odaklıdır.
This button is meant to save the file.
be expected to
Beklenti daha resmi ve kurumsal. Genellikle yazılı kurallardan, iş bağlamından gelir.
Employees are expected to arrive on time.
be required to
Resmi gereklilik — yasal, kurumsal veya akademik zorunluluk. En güçlü dışsal zorunluluktur.
Passengers are required to show ID.
be allowed to
İzin verildiğini gösterir. "supposed to"nun izin boyutunu tamamlar.
Students are allowed to use dictionaries.
be permitted to
"Allowed to"nun daha resmi hali. Yasal veya resmi bağlamlarda kullanılır.
Visitors are not permitted to take photos.
be obliged / obligated to
Ahlaki ya da yasal yükümlülük ifade eder. Resmi İngilizce'de yaygındır.
The company is obligated to pay taxes.
be due to
Zamanlanmış bir olayı veya son teslim tarihini anlatır. "is due" = son tarihi var.
The report is due on Friday.
had better (not)
Tavsiyenin ötesinde, sonuçlarla birlikte gelen zorunluluk hissi verir. Tehdit içerebilir.
You'd better not forget the meeting.
Önceki Konularla Bağlantılar
Önceki KonuBağlantıÖrnek
Modal Verbs (can, must, should) "supposed to" semi-modal'dır; "must/should"la karşılaştırarak öğrenilir You must vs. You're supposed to
Passive Voice "supposed to" fiil çekimi be + past participle yapısına benzer is supposed ↔ is required / is expected
Conditionals Past "was supposed to" → gerçekleşmemiş koşul gibi işlev görür She was supposed to come but didn't.
Reported Speech "He said he was supposed to go" — reported speech'te was/were kullanılır She told me she was supposed to leave.
Question Tags "You're supposed to be here, aren't you?" tag question'larla kullanılır They're supposed to help, aren't they?
Not to / Not supposed to farkı: "be supposed to" ile "not" her zaman "supposed" dan önce gelir: "aren't supposed to" — asla "supposed not to" değil!
Okuma Parçası (~350 kelime)
Office Rules Nobody Follows

Marcus had been working at the company for exactly three weeks when he realized that nobody actually followed the employee handbook. The handbook was a thick, laminated document that spelled out exactly what everyone was supposed to do. According to the handbook, employees were supposed to arrive by 8:45 AM. In reality, most people strolled in after 9:15, coffee in hand, without a care in the world.


The kitchen was perhaps the best example of this gap between rules and reality. A large sign on the refrigerator said, "You are supposed to label your food." Below it, in smaller letters, it read: "Unlabeled items will be thrown away every Friday." Yet every Monday morning, the fridge was packed with mystery containers. Nobody was supposed to leave dishes in the sink overnight, but there were always three or four mugs sitting there by Tuesday morning.


Marcus approached his manager, Diana, about it. "I thought we were supposed to follow the handbook strictly," he said. Diana laughed kindly. "In theory, yes. Employees are supposed to attend all team meetings too, but Victor hasn't been to one in six months and he's our best developer." She paused and added, "You're supposed to use the project management software to log your hours. That one, actually, do follow — HR checks it."


Marcus nodded, beginning to understand the invisible hierarchy of rules. Some rules were supposed to be followed absolutely. Others were more like gentle suggestions printed on laminated paper. The difficult part was figuring out which was which.


By the end of his first month, Marcus had learned to tell the difference. He wasn't supposed to be late for client calls — that was non-negotiable. He was supposed to document every decision in writing. But skipping the 8:45 start time? That rule, it seemed, was supposed to exist mainly so that someone could point to it if things ever went wrong.

handbook = rehber / el kitabı laminated = plastik kaplı strolled in = salına salına girdi label = etiketlemek unlabeled = etiketsiz strictly = sıkı biçimde in theory = teoride invisible = görünmez hierarchy = hiyerarşi gentle suggestion = nazik öneri non-negotiable = tartışmasız, kesin document = kayıt altına almak skipping = atlayarak point to = işaret etmek, göstermek
Dikkat edin: Metin boyunca mavi işaretli her "supposed to" farklı bir anlam bağlamında kullanılmıştır — kural, plan, beklenti ve ihlal.

Bölüm 3 — Cümle Dönüşümü
Cümleyi "be supposed to" kullanarak yeniden yazın. Cevabı görmek için "Göster" butonuna tıklayın.
→ "The rules say employees must not take work items home."
Employees aren't supposed to take work items home.
→ "The plan was for the package to arrive yesterday, but it didn't."
The package was supposed to arrive yesterday.
→ "People say this film is very funny."
This film is supposed to be very funny.
