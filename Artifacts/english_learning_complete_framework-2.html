AParts of SpeechSözcük Türleri▶
  • Nouns İsimler
    • Count vs uncount — sayılabilir/sayılamaz
    • Proper vs common / Abstract vs concrete
    • Collective: team, staff, family (tekil mi çoğul mu?)
    • Compound nouns; Irregular plurals
    • Genitive: John's, the students', the edge of the city
  • Pronouns Zamirler
    • Personal: I/me/my/mine — subject/object/possessive adj/possessive pronoun
    • Reflexive: myself, yourself, himself, itself...
    • Relative: who, which, that, whose, whom
    • Demonstrative: this/that/these/those
    • Indefinite: somebody, anyone, nothing, everywhere...
    • Reciprocal: each other, one another
  • Verbs Fiiller
    • Action vs stative — eylem/durum (stative verbs not in continuous)
    • Transitive / intransitive / linking
    • Primary auxiliaries: be, do, have
    • Modal auxiliaries: can/could, may/might, will/would, shall/should, must
    • Phrasal verbs: verb + particle(s)
    • Infinitive (to-/bare), Gerund (-ing as noun), Participle (-ing/-ed)
  • Adjectives Sıfatlar
    • Attributive (before noun) vs predicative (after be/seem/look)
    • Gradable vs ungradable: very hot ✓ / very unique ✗
    • Comparison: -er/-est, more/most, irregular (good→better→best)
    • Order: opinion→size→age→shape→colour→origin→material→purpose+NOUN
    • Participial adjectives: interesting vs interested
  • Adverbs Zarflar
    • Manner: quickly, well, hard, fast | Place: here, outside, nearby
    • Time: now, already, still, yet, soon | Frequency: always→never
    • Degree: very, quite, rather, fairly, extremely, enough, too
    • Sentence adverbs: unfortunately, clearly, frankly
    • Adverb placement: front/mid/end position rules
  • Articles & Determiners Tanımlayıcılar
    • a (consonant sound) / an (vowel sound) — first mention
    • the — specific, unique, shared knowledge, second mention
    • Zero article — general plural/uncount, proper nouns
    • Quantifiers: some/any, much/many, few/little, both/all/each/every
  • Prepositions Edatlar
    • Place: in/on/at, under, above, between, among, beside, behind...
    • Time: in (months/years), on (days), at (times), for/since/during/by/until
    • Direction: to, into, from, out of, through, across, along
    • Colligations: interested IN, good AT, afraid OF, depend ON
  • Conjunctions Bağlaçlar
    • Coordinating (FANBOYS): for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so
    • Subordinating: because, although, when, if, since, unless, while...
    • Correlative: both...and, either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also
BThe Tense SystemZaman Sistemi — 12 Temel Zaman▶
12 zaman = 3 dönem (present/past/future) × 4 görünüş (simple/continuous/perfect/perf.continuous)
  • Present Simple Geniş Zaman
    • Habits & routines, permanent facts, scheduled future
    • Form: V / V+s(3rd sg) | do/does questions & negatives
  • Present Continuous Şimdiki Zaman
    • Actions now, temporary situations, future arrangements, annoying habits (always)
    • Stative verbs NOT used: know, want, understand, love, see...
  • Present Perfect Geçmiş-Şimdi Zamanı
    • Past with present relevance — life experience (ever/never)
    • Recent: just/already/yet | Duration: for/since
    • Form: have/has + past participle
  • Present Perfect Continuous Süregelen Geçmiş-Şimdi
    • Ongoing activity + visible result: I've been studying for 3 hours.
  • Past Simple Geçmiş Zaman
    • Completed action at specific past time, narrative sequence
    • Regular: -ed | Irregular: go→went, see→saw, take→took...
  • Past Continuous Geçmişte Süren Eylem
    • Background, interrupted action (while/when), parallel actions
  • Past Perfect Geçmiş Öncesi Zaman
    • "Past before past": By the time she arrived, he had already left.
  • Past Perfect Continuous Geçmiş Öncesi Süregelen
    • Duration before past event: She had been waiting for 2 hours when...
  • Future: will Gelecek Zaman (will)
    • Spontaneous decisions, predictions (no evidence), promises, offers
  • Future: be going to Planlı/Kanıtlı Gelecek
    • Intentions/plans, evidence-based predictions (Look at those clouds...)
  • Future Continuous Gelecekte Süren Eylem
    • This time tomorrow, I'll be flying to Istanbul.
  • Future Perfect Gelecek Öncesi Tamamlanmış
    • By 2030, AI will have transformed education.
  • Future Perfect Continuous Gelecek Öncesi Süregelen
    • By June, she'll have been studying for 3 years.

  • Sentence Types Cümle türleri
    • Simple → Compound → Complex → Compound-complex
  • Question Types Soru türleri
    • Yes/No (inversion), Wh- questions, Question tags, Indirect questions, Negative questions

  • Can / Could Yetenek / Olasılık / İzin / Rica
  • May / Might Olasılık (%50/%30) / Resmi izin
  • Will / Would Gelecek / Alışkanlık / Kibarca rica / Koşullu
  • Shall / Should Tavsiye / Beklenti / Öneri

EAdvanced Structuresİleri Düzey Yapılar▶
  • Conditionals Koşul Cümleleri
    • Zero: Pres+Pres — genel gerçekler
    • First: Pres+will — olası gelecek
    • Second: Past+would — hayali şimdi/gelecek
    • Third: Past Perf+would have — pişmanlık (unreal past)
    • Mixed: Past Perf+would — geçmişin şimdiki sonucu
    • Without if: unless, provided that, as long as, on condition that, in case
    • Conditional inversion: Had I known... / Were I to... / Should you need...
  • Passive Voice Edilgen Yapı
    • Formation: be + pp — tüm 12 zamanda
    • By-agent, Get-passive (informal), Double passive
    • Passive infinitive: wants to be seen | Passive gerund: dislikes being watched
    • Impersonal: It is said that... / He is thought to be...
  • Reported Speech Dolaylı Anlatım
    • Statements: tense backshift — "I am" → he said he WAS
    • Questions: if/whether (yes/no) | wh-word + S+V order
    • Commands: told/asked + obj + to-inf
    • Modal backshift: will→would, can→could, may→might, must→had to
    • Time/place shifts: now→then, today→that day, here→there
    • No-backshift: general truths, modal already past, present reporting verb
  • Causatives Ettirgen Yapılar
    • have + obj + bare inf (arrangement): I had him fix my car.
    • get + obj + to-inf (persuasion): I got her to help me.
    • make + obj + bare inf (force): She made me laugh.
    • let + obj + bare inf (permission): He let us use his office.
    • have/get + obj + pp (passive causative): I had my hair cut.
  • Relative Clauses İlgi Cümleleri
    • Defining: who/which/that/whose/where/when — virgülsüz, zorunlu bilgi
    • Non-defining: who/which/whose — virgüllü, ek bilgi, that kullanılmaz
    • Reduced: the man LIVING next door / the book WRITTEN by Pamuk
    • Quantified: some of whom, all of which, most of which
  • Wishes & Hypothetical Dilek & Varsayım
    • Wish + Past Simple: I wish I knew. (şimdiki hayal)
    • Wish + Past Perfect: I wish I had studied. (geçmiş pişmanlık)
    • Wish + would: I wish he would stop. (sinir bozucu alışkanlık)
    • If only... | It's time + past | Would rather + past
  • Inversion Devrik Cümle
    • Neg adverbials: Never have I..., Not only did he..., Seldom, Hardly, No sooner
    • Only: Only when I arrived did I realise...
    • So/Such: So beautiful was the view that...
  • Cleft Sentences Yarık Cümleler
    • It-cleft: It was John who broke the window.
    • Wh-cleft: What I need is a holiday.
  • Participle Clauses Ortaç Cümleleri
    • -ing: Walking home, I saw an accident.
    • -ed: Exhausted by the heat, she rested.
    • Having + pp: Having finished, he left.
  • Other Advanced Features
    • Emphatic do: I DO understand. She DOES care.
    • Ellipsis & substitution: I'll help if I can. / "Do you like it?" "I think so."
    • Nominalization: destroy→destruction, develop→development
    • Question tags: It's warm, isn't it? / She never lies, does she?

IV Four Skills Dört Beceri Alıcı + Üretici ▶
Receptive (alıcı): Reading + Listening → input. Productive (üretici): Writing + Speaking → output. Döngü: input → intake → output → feedback.
AReadingOkuma▶
  • Reading Strategies
    • Skimming — tarama: genel fikri kavrama
    • Scanning — özgül bilgi arama: specific facts, numbers, names
    • Intensive reading — yoğun okuma: word-by-word, maximum comprehension
    • Extensive reading — geniş okuma: pleasure/fluency, large quantities
  • Reading Skills
    • Main idea, supporting details, inference (çıkarım), text structure
    • Author's purpose & tone, vocabulary in context
  • Text Types Metin türleri — Narrative / Descriptive / Expository / Argumentative / Instructional
BWritingYazma▶
  • Writing Process Yazma süreci
    • Prewriting → Drafting → Revising → Editing → Publishing
  • Text Types & Genres
    • Argumentative / Descriptive / Narrative / Expository essay
    • Formal & informal letter/email | Report | Summary | Review | Story
  • Paragraph Structure Paragraf yapısı — Topic sentence → Supporting sentences → Concluding sentence
  • Cohesion Devices Bağdaşıklık araçları
    • Reference: he/she/this/these/the former/the latter
    • Substitution: do/one/so | Ellipsis: omission
    • Transition: moreover, however, therefore, as a result, subsequently
  • Punctuation Noktalama — period, comma, semicolon, colon, apostrophe, dash, hyphen, quotation marks
CListeningDinleme▶
  • Listening Types
    • Global (gist), Selective (specific info), Detailed (all), Inferential (implied meaning)
  • Listening Skills
    • Predicting, note-taking, listening for opinion/attitude
    • Understanding reduced speech: contractions, weak forms, connected speech
  • Input Sources
    • Authentic: podcasts, films, TED Talks, news, songs
    • TTS-based (hızlandırılmış): DAMITMA döngüsündeki bisiklet+ses yöntemi
DSpeakingKonuşma▶
  • Pronunciation System Telaffuz sistemi
    • 44 phonemes: 12 vowels + 8 diphthongs + 24 consonants
    • Minimal pairs: ship/sheep, live/leave, bet/bat
  • Suprasegmental Features Üst bölümsel özellikler
    • Word stress, sentence stress, rhythm, intonation
    • Connected speech: linking, assimilation, elision, weak forms
  • Fluency vs Accuracy Akıcılık vs Doğruluk — Hedef: communicative competence (ikisini de)
  • Discourse & Interaction
    • Turn-taking, hedging, clarification, repair (söylem düzeltme)
    • Monologue vs dialogue contexts

AAcquisition PrinciplesDil Edinim İlkeleri▶
  • Input Hypothesis (Krashen) Girdi hipotezi — Comprehensible input i+1: anlayabileceğinden biraz üstteki girdi
  • Noticing Hypothesis (Schmidt) Fark etme hipotezi — Bilinçli dikkat olmadan edinim olmaz
  • Output Hypothesis (Swain) Çıktı hipotezi — Üretmek, bilgi boşluklarının farkına varmayı zorlar
  • Affective Filter Duygu filtresi — Kaygı↑ = edinim↓. Özgüven + motivasyon + düşük kaygı = daha iyi edinim
  • Explicit → Implicit Knowledge Kural bilgisi → Otomatik kullanım
