Mert's Day Out in London

A Saturday in late April · ~485 words · 8 grammar structures · daily-life vocabulary

🔊It was a bright Saturday morning in late April, and Mert had been waiting for this day all month. His mother was arriving at Heathrow at noon, and there were a hundred small things to do before then. He picked up his Oyster card, checked the weather, and left the flat at eight.

🔊The Tube was quieter than usual. Mert stood on the platform at Walthamstow Central. "Does this train go to Paddington?" he asked a stranger. "Yes, but you have to change at Oxford Circus," the man replied. Mert thanked him and stepped onto the carriage.

🔊At the airport, he bought a cheap coffee. "Can I get a flat white, please?" he asked the barista. "Have you tried our oat milk version? It's much smoother than the regular one." Mert had never tried oat milk before, but he decided to be brave. He paid with his card, tapped, and waited.

🔊His mother came through the gate twenty minutes late. She looked tired but happy. "How was the flight?" Mert asked. "The food was terrible, but the film was lovely," she said, laughing. They took the Heathrow Express back to central London. The journey was shorter than Mert had remembered — only fifteen minutes.

🔊They had lunch at a small market in Borough. Mert picked up a sample of cheese. "Would you like to try this one?" the stallholder offered. "It's the strongest we have." His mother made a face — too strong for her. They chose a milder one and sat on the wooden steps by the river.

🔊In the afternoon, they went into a clothing shop. "Where is the changing room?" his mother asked. "Just over there, madam," the assistant said, pointing. She tried on a blue cardigan. "Does it fit?" Mert asked. "A little too tight," she said. The assistant brought a bigger size, which fit perfectly.

🔊Later, Mert had to stop by the post office. He had been promising to send a parcel to his cousin for weeks. He filled out the customs form, paid the fee, and walked out feeling lighter. A quick visit to the pharmacy followed — his mother needed a prescription filled.

🔊That evening, they sat on the bus home. "I have never walked so much in my life," his mother whispered. "You'll sleep well tonight," Mert smiled. Outside, the sky was turning pink. London was at its best — busy, kind, and strangely familiar to her now.

Görev: Mert'in günü gibi, sen de bir gününü kısa anlat. 5-7 cümle. Aşağıdaki 5 yapıdan en az birer kez kullan:

  • Past Simple — ne yaptın
  • Phrasal Verb — pick up / try on / fill out / stop by / walk out gibi
  • Prepositions of Place — at / on / in / to / from
  • Articles — a / an / the / ∅
  • Comparison — quieter than / smaller than / the best
