Hikaye nerede başlıyor? Jean Valjean bir ekmek çaldığı için 19 yıl cezaevinde kaldı. Şimdi serbest. Ama elinde sarı bir kâğıt var — bu kâğıt herkese "bu adam tehlikeli" diyor. Hiçbir han onu almıyor, hiç kimse yardım etmiyor. Sonunda küçük bir kasabada Piskopos Myriel'in kapısını çalıyor.

Jean Valjean walked slowly into the town of Digne. He was very tired and very hungry. He had not eaten anything for two days. In his pocket, he had a little money — not much, but enough for a meal and a bed.

He went to the first inn. The owner looked at his yellow passport and shook his head. "We have no room," he said. Valjean tried the second inn. Same answer. He tried a third place, and a fourth. Nobody would let him in. A yellow passport meant one thing: ex-prisoner. If you showed that passport, people closed their doors.

It was getting dark. Valjean sat down on a stone near the road. He could not sleep outside — it was November, and the night was cold. An old woman who was passing looked at him.

"You should try the Bishop's house," she said. "He never turns anyone away."

Valjean found the house and knocked on the door. A woman opened it. He told her his name and showed her the yellow passport. She did not close the door.

The Bishop was sitting at the table when Valjean came in. He looked up and smiled. "Sit down," he said. "You must be hungry. Dinner will be ready soon."

Valjean sat down slowly. He looked at the table. The plates and cups were made of silver. They were more beautiful than anything he had ever seen in his life.

That night, the Bishop gave him food, a warm bed, and clean sheets. Valjean lay in the dark. He could not sleep. He was thinking about the silver. The Bishop trusted him — but nobody had ever trusted him before. If he took the silver, he could sell it for a lot of money.

At two in the morning, he got up, put the silver in his bag, and left.

In the morning, three policemen brought him back. They found the silver in his bag. Valjean was waiting for the Bishop to say: "Yes, he stole it. Take him to prison."

But the Bishop walked towards them and smiled. "I am happy you are here," he said to Valjean. "I gave you the silver. But why did you not take the candlesticks? They are silver too. You forgot them."

The policemen looked at each other. They could not arrest a man who had received a gift. They left.

The Bishop came close to Valjean and spoke quietly. "Jean Valjean, my brother. You are not a bad man. You are a good man who made a bad choice. If you use this silver well, your life can change. I have bought your soul. I give it back to you — but now it belongs to God."

Valjean took the candlesticks. His hands were trembling. He walked out of the house. The sky was getting light.

Something inside him had changed — although he did not yet understand how.

💭 Felsefe — İngilizce Kelimelerle

Kavramları İngilizce öğrenmek için kullanıyoruz

The Bishop does something surprising. Valjean stole (çaldı) the silver — this is a fact (gerçek). But the Bishop lies (yalan söyler) to the police. He says the silver was a gift (hediye).

Why? Because he sees something different (farklı) in Valjean. He sees a man who has suffered (acı çekmiş) for nineteen years. He sees a man who is hungry (aç olan), tired (yorgun), and afraid (korkmuş).

The Greek philosopher Aristotle talked about good character (iyi karakter). He said: a person becomes (olur) good by doing good things. The Bishop gives Valjean a chance (şans, fırsat) — a chance to start again.

Yeni kelimeler: steal (çalmak) / gift (hediye) / fact (gerçek) / lie (yalan söylemek) / suffer (acı çekmek) / afraid (korkmuş) / chance (şans) / character (karakter) / become (olmak) / trust (güvenmek)

DilCümleDikkat
Français Je vous ai donné les chandeliers aussi. "ai donné" = Present Perfect (Fransızca). "aussi" = too / also.
English I gave you the candlesticks too. Simple Past. "too" cümle sonunda = "also" ile aynı anlam.
Türkçe Şamdanları da sana verdim. "da" = too / also. Fiil sonda — İngilizcede fiil ortada!

Yazarlık notu: Hugo "He walked out of the house" ile bölümü bitirmiyor. Bunun yerine: "Something inside him had changed — although he did not yet understand how." Bu son cümle okuyucuya bir soru bırakıyor: Nasıl değişecek? Bu tekniğin adı: hook (kanca — okuyucuyu sıradaki bölüme çeken cümle).
