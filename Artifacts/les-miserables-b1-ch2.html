Bu bölümde ne oluyor? Valjean piskoposun evinden çıktıktan sonra yollarda yürüyor. Yolda küçük bir çocukla karşılaşıyor — Petit-Gervais adında bir sokak çocuğu. Çocuğun bir gümüş parası düşüyor. Valjean farkında olmadan ayağını paranın üstüne koyuyor. Sonra ne olduğunu anlıyor. Bu an, hikayenin dönüm noktasıdır.

Valjean walked out of the Bishop's house slowly. He carried the silver candlesticks in his hands. The morning was cold, and the road was empty. He did not know where he was going. He just walked.

For nineteen years, he used to wake up every morning in a prison cell. He used to hear the sound of keys and metal doors. Now there was only silence and birds. It felt strange — almost too quiet.

After walking for an hour, he sat down by the road. He put the candlesticks on the ground next to him. He looked at them for a long time. The Bishop had given him these things. Nobody had ever given him anything before.

While he was sitting there, he heard a sound. A small boy was coming along the road. He was singing and jumping. In his hand, he had a small bag. A coin fell out of the bag and rolled past Valjean's foot. Automatically, without thinking, Valjean put his foot on the coin.

The boy stopped. He looked at Valjean. "Monsieur," he said, "that is my coin. Could you please move your foot?"

Valjean did not answer. He was looking at the ground.

"Monsieur, please," the boy said again. "It's my money. You can see it under your foot, can't you?"

Still no answer. The boy did not know what to do. He was too afraid to touch Valjean's foot. He waited for a moment. Then he quickly picked up his bag and ran away. He ran into the trees and disappeared.

Valjean stayed there until the sound of the boy's feet was gone. Then he looked down. The coin was still under his foot.

Something hit him hard — not from outside, but from inside. He looked at his hands. These were the same hands that had taken the Bishop's silver. Now they had taken a poor child's only coin.

He stood up suddenly. "Petit-Gervais!" he shouted. "Petit-Gervais!" But the road was empty. The boy was gone. Valjean ran into the trees, shouting the name again and again. No answer.

He came back to the road and sat down. He put his head in his hands.

He told himself that he was a bad man. He had not changed. The Bishop had trusted him, and he had stolen from a child. He was not good enough for the Bishop's kindness.

When the sun went down, a woman walked past. She saw a large man sitting alone on the road, crying.

She did not stop. But she looked back once, nervously.

That night, Valjean made a decision. He used to think only about himself — about surviving, about getting free. From that day on, things would be different. They had to be.

💭 Felsefe — İngilizce Kelimelerle

Kavramları İngilizce öğrenmek için kullanıyoruz

This scene is about conscience (vicdan). Valjean does not plan to steal the coin. He does it automatically (otomatik olarak, düşünmeden). Then he realizes what he has done — and feels ashamed (utanmış, mahcup).

Aristotle said that a person's character (karakter) is like a habit (alışkanlık). For nineteen years, Valjean's habit was to take (almak) things to survive. This is why he put his foot on the coin — without thinking. But the Bishop's kindness created something new in him: a feeling of guilt (suçluluk hissi).

The important thing: Valjean cries (ağlıyor). This is new. In prison, he used to feel only anger (öfke). Now he feels regret (pişmanlık). This is the beginning of change (değişim).

📚 Çeviri Karşılaştırması

Aynı an — üç dilde
DilCümleDikkat
Français Il posa le pied dessus machinalement. "machinalement" = otomatik olarak, düşünmeden. İngilizce: "automatically" veya "without thinking".
English Automatically, without thinking, he put his foot on the coin. İki zarf yan yana: "automatically" + "without thinking". Aynı fikri iki farklı şekilde söylüyor — güçlü etki.
Türkçe Düşünmeden, otomatik olarak ayağını paranın üstüne koydu. Türkçede sıra ters: zarf önce, fiil sonda. İngilizcede zarf sona veya başa gelebilir.

Yazarlık notu: Hugo bu bölümde Valjean'ın içini göstermiyor — sadece davranışını gösteriyor. "He put his head in his hands." Neden? Hugo söylemiyor. Sen anlıyorsun. Bu tekniğin adı: show, don't tell (anlatma, göster). Yazar olarak en değerli araçlardan biri.
