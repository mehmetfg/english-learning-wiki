Apple's First Foldable Phone

📰 Arka Plan: Samsung ve diğer şirketler zaten katlanabilir telefon satıyor. Apple henüz satmıyor — ama çalışıyor. Bu haber o çalışmanın nerede olduğunu anlatıyor.

📱 What Is the iPhone Fold?

Apple is working on a new phone. It is called the iPhone Fold. This phone can fold in half — like a small book. Many people want to buy it. But Apple has not started making it yet.

⚠️ What Are the Problems?

Making a foldable phone is difficult. Apple wants the screen to have no lines when you open it. This is called crease-free. Right now, the screen still has this problem. Also, there is a special hinge inside the phone. A hinge connects the two parts. But this hinge is very expensive to make. Apple is still trying to fix these problems.

📅 When Can We Buy It?

Experts think that Apple will start making the phone in July. If this happens, the phone will come out later this year. Apple wants to make 11 million phones for the first time. But we do not know for sure because Apple never talks about its future plans.

🤔 A Big Question

Apple wants the screen to be perfect. But a perfect screen takes more time. The question is: should Apple wait for a perfect phone, or release it sooner — even if it is not perfect?

  • 🔵 Apple has not yet started making the iPhone Fold.
  • 🔵 The main problems are the screen and the hinge.
  • 🔵 Production may start in July 2026.

Present Continuous — Şu An Olan Eylem
Parantez içini present continuous ile tamamla. (am/is/are + verb-ing)
1. Apple ______ (work) on a new phone right now.
💡 Apple = he/she/it → is + working
2. Engineers ______ (try) to fix the screen problem.
💡 Engineers = they → are + trying
3. Apple ______ (not make) the phone yet.
💡 Olumsuz: is not + making

Future with "will" — Gelecek Zaman Tahminleri
Doğru seçeneği tıkla.
1. "Production starts in July" — bu cümleyi WILL ile tahmine çevir.
2. "Apple will ______ 11 million phones." — doğru fiili seç.

Yet / Still / Already — Zaman Zarfları
Boşluğu doldur: yet / still / already
1. Apple has not started making the phone ______.
💡 "not ... yet" = henüz değil. Cümlenin sonuna gelir.
2. Apple is ______ trying to fix the screen problem.
💡 "still" = hâlâ. Fiilden önce gelir.
3. Samsung has ______ made foldable phones.
💡 "already" = zaten, çoktan. have ile birlikte gelir.

📰 Haberi Nasıl Okuruz?

İngilizce teknoloji haberlerinde sık kullanılan yapılar
Kalıp: "... has not yet ..." "Apple has not yet started making the phone." — Bu yapı çok yaygın haber dilinde. has/have + not + yet = henüz yapmadı ama belki yapacak. "Yet" cümlenin sonuna da gelebilir: "Apple has not started yet."
Kalıp: "Experts think that ..." Haberlerde kesin bilgi yoksa bu kalıp kullanılır. "Experts think / analysts say / sources suggest" → tahmin veya belirsiz bilgi anlamına gelir. Kesin değil!

Bu iki kalıbı ezberle. İngilizce haber okurken çok sık göreceksin. "Experts think that..." ile başlayan her cümle = bu kesin değil, sadece bir tahmin.

💬 Görüşünü Söyle — B1 Yapıları

Bu haber hakkında ne düşünüyorsun? Bu kalıpları kullan.
Görüş Bildirme Kalıpları (B1) I think that... = Bence...  ·  In my opinion,... = Benim görüşüme göre...  ·  I believe that... = İnanıyorum ki...  ·  It seems to me that... = Bana öyle geliyor ki...

Örnek: "I think that Apple should wait. A perfect screen is more important than a fast release."

Ya da: "In my opinion, Apple should release the phone sooner. People are waiting."
