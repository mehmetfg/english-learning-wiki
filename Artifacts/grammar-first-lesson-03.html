A Day in London

Camden Market & Regent's Park — Mert + his mother

¶ 1 — The Plan

The night before their trip, Mert and his mother made a plan. "We're going to visit Camden Market first," said his mother, pointing at the map carefully. "Then we'll walk north to Regent's Park." Mert always loves exploring new places, so he nodded eagerly.

¶ 2 — Arrival

The next morning, they arrived at Camden Town station early. The streets were already busy. "Look at that queue — it's going to be packed today," said Mert excitedly. His mother smiled warmly. "I think we'll find some wonderful things. Let's go!"

¶ 3 — The Market

Camden Market was founded in 1974. Today, it is visited by more than 100,000 people every weekend. The stalls are organised neatly by category — food, clothes, art, and music. Fresh food is prepared daily, and colourful decorations are displayed everywhere. Mert walked slowly through the narrow paths, looking at everything carefully.

¶ 4 — The Jacket

"Look at this vintage jacket!" he cried. "If I had enough money, I would definitely buy it." His mother laughed. "If the jacket were cheaper, I would buy it for you right now," she said gently. They moved on.

¶ 5 — Food Time

They were hungry, so they stopped at the food area. There were many choices: Japanese ramen, Ethiopian stews, and Mexican tacos, but Mert chose something simple. "I'd like some rice and a piece of cake," he said. His mother ordered some soup and a sandwich. "They use fresh ingredients here, yet the prices are surprisingly high," she said softly. Mert nodded but said nothing — the food was delicious.

¶ 6 — Regent's Park

After lunch, they walked north to Regent's Park. The park is maintained by the Royal Parks organisation, and its famous rose garden is visited by millions of tourists every year. It is considered one of the most beautiful green spaces in London.

¶ 7 — By the Lake

They sat quietly beside the lake. Mert usually reads in peaceful places, but today he just watched the ducks move gracefully across the water. "Mum," he said softly, "if we lived in London, we would come here every single weekend." His mother put her arm around him. "We would need a lot of money for that," she laughed. "But yes — it would be perfect."

¶ 8 — The Promise

The sun began to set slowly behind the trees. Mert stood up quickly. "I'll carry the bag, Mum," he said. "And I'll tell you something — today was one of the best days of my life." His mother hugged him warmly. "For me too," she said. "We're going to do this again next summer. That's a promise."

🔍 Grammar Breakdown — 7 Structures
1
Future Will
Gelecek Zaman (Will)
Anlık kararlar, tahminler, vaatler, teklifler ve doğal akış ifadeleri için kullanılır. Planlanmamış, o an oluşan gelecek.
Subject + will + base verb
Negative: Subject + won't + base verb
From the story:
"…we'll walk north to Regent's Park."
→ Tahmini/doğal plan (spontaneous)
"I think we'll find some wonderful things."
→ Tahmin (prediction)
"I'll carry the bag, Mum."
→ Anlık teklif (spontaneous offer)
Key signal: "I think / probably / I'm sure / I promise" → will
2
Future Going To
Gelecek Zaman (Going To)
Önceden yapılmış planlar ve mevcut kanıta dayalı tahminler için kullanılır. Zaten karar verilmiş, ya da şu an görülen bir şeyden çıkarılan sonuç.
Subject + am/is/are going to + base verb
From the story:
"We're going to visit Camden Market first."
→ Önceden yapılmış plan (prior plan)
"It's going to be packed today."
→ Kanıta dayalı tahmin (queue = evidence)
"We're going to do this again next summer."
→ Güçlü niyet / söz (firm intention)
Key signal: "Look!" / "I've already decided" / context of prior planning → going to
3
Second Conditional
2. Tip Koşul Cümlesi
Gerçek olmayan veya gerçekleşmesi düşük olan şimdiki/gelecek durumlar için kullanılır. Hayal, varsayım, tavsiye anlatımı.
If + past simple, would + base verb
Note: "were" for all persons (formal)
From the story:
"If I had enough money, I would definitely buy it."
→ Gerçek değil — param yok (unreal)
"If the jacket were cheaper, I would buy it."
→ Varsayım — ceket pahalı (hypothetical)
"If we lived in London, we would come here every weekend."
→ Hayal — Londra'da yaşamıyoruz (dream)
vs. 1st Cond: "If it rains, I'll stay." (real/possible) ↔ "If it rained, I would stay." (unreal)
4
Passive Voice
Edilgen Çatı
Eylemin nesilini (kim yaptı) değil, sonucunu ön plana çıkarmak için kullanılır. Genel gerçekler, resmi metin ve nesne odaklı anlatıda çok sık görülür.
Subject + be (conjugated) + past participle
Agent: + "by + person"
From the story:
"Camden Market was founded in 1974."
→ Past Simple Passive
"It is visited by more than 100,000 people."
→ Present Simple Passive + "by"
"The stalls are organised neatly by category."
→ Present Simple Passive
Pattern: Active: "People visit the park." → Passive: "The park is visited by people."
5
Adverbs of Manner & Frequency
Hal ve Sıklık Zarfları
Manner (Hal): nasıl yapıldığını anlatır → fiil/sıfat sonra gelir. Frequency (Sıklık): ne sıklıkla yapıldığını anlatır → genel olarak yardımcı fiilden önce, ana fiilden önce.
Manner: verb + adverb (slowly, warmly…)
Frequency: always > usually > often > sometimes > rarely > never
From the story:
"He walked slowly through the paths."
→ Manner: walk + slowly (nasıl?)
"She said gently / smiled warmly."
→ Manner: verb + adverb
"Mert always loves exploring."
→ Frequency: always (her zaman)
"Mert usually reads in peaceful places."
→ Frequency: usually (genellikle)
Tip: Most manner adverbs = adjective + -ly (quick→quickly, warm→warmly). Exceptions: fast, hard, late (no -ly)
6
Coordinating Conjunctions
FANBOYS — Sıralama Bağlaçları
İki bağımsız cümleyi, kelimeyi ya da ifadeyi bağlar. Virgül + bağlaç kuralına dikkat edin: iki tam cümle birleştiğinde araya virgül girer.
For · And · Nor · But · Or · Yet · So
From the story:
"…food, clothes, art, and music."
→ And: ekleme (addition)
"They were hungry, so they stopped."
→ So: sonuç (result)
"…tacos, but Mert chose something simple."
→ But: zıtlık (contrast)
"Fresh ingredients, yet surprisingly expensive."
→ Yet: beklenmedik zıtlık (surprising contrast)
Comma rule: Two independent clauses → use comma before FANBOYS. Single words/phrases → no comma needed.
7
Countable & Uncountable Nouns
Sayılabilir ve Sayılamaz İsimler
Sayılabilir (Countable): tekil veya çoğul olur, a/an alabilir. Sayılamaz (Uncountable): çoğul olmaz, a/an almaz, "some/much/a lot of" ile kullanılır.
Countable: a/an · the · many · a few · several
Uncountable: some · much · a lot of · a piece of
From the story:
"I'd like some rice and a piece of cake."
→ rice (unc.) / piece of cake (cnt.)
"some soup" / "a sandwich"
→ soup (unc.) / sandwich (cnt.)
"There were many choices."
→ choices (cnt. → many ✓)
"We would need a lot of money."
→ money (unc. → a lot of ✓)
Partitives: a piece of cake/bread/advice · a cup of tea · a glass of water → make uncountable countable
