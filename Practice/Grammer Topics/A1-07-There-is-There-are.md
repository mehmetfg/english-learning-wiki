**Konu Haritası**

| Yapı | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tekil | There is / There's | There isn't | Is there…? |
| Çoğul | There are | There aren't | Are there…? |
| Sayılamayan | There is (some) | There isn't (any) | Is there (any)…? |
