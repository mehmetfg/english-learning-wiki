**Konu Haritası**

| Özne | İyelik sıfatı | İyelik zamiri |
|---|---|---|
| I | my | mine |
| you | your | yours |
| he | his | his |
| she | her | hers |
| it | its | — |
| we | our | ours |
| they | their | theirs |

> **'s (saxon genitive):** Ali's car (Ali'nin arabası), the dog's tail (köpeğin kuyruğu).

# 🧩 BÖLÜM 3 — İngilizce Boşluk Doldurma Testi

1. This is ______ car. (ben) ( my / mine / me )
2. ______ name is Ayşe. (o, kadın) ( His / Her / Hers )
3. ______ phone is new. (o, erkek) ( His / Her / He )
4. This is ______ house. (biz) ( our / ours / us )
5. Is this book ______? (sen) ( your / yours / you )
6. ______ dog is very cute. (Ali) ( Ali / Ali's / Alis )
7. This bag is ______. (onlar) ( their / theirs / them )
8. The cat licks ______ tail. (o, hayvan) ( it / its / it's )
9. This pen isn't mine, it's ______. (sen) ( your / yours / you )
10. My ______ eyes are blue. (kız kardeş) ( sister / sister's / sisters )
11. That is ______ teacher. (biz) ( our / ours / us )
12. The book is ______. (ben) ( my / mine / me )
