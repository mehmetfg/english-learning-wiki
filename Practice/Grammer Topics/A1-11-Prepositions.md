> 📖 **Konu anlatımı:** [[Prepositions]]

**Konu Haritası**

| Edat | Yer (place) | Zaman (time) |
|---|---|---|
| in | büyük alan: in Turkey, in a box, in the room | aylar/yıllar/mevsim: in May, in 2026, in summer |
| on | yüzey: on the table, on the wall | gün/tarih: on Monday, on 5th June |
| at | nokta: at home, at the door, at school | saat/an: at 7 o'clock, at night |
