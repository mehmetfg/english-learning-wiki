> 📖 **Konu anlatımı:** [[Present Simple]]

**Konu Haritası**

| Özne | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru |
|---|---|---|---|
| I/You/We/They | work | don't work | Do you work…? |
| He/She/It | work**s** | doesn't work | Does he work…? |

> **-s eki kuralı:** he/she/it → +s (works), -ch/-sh/-s/-x/-o → +es (goes, watches), ünsüz+y → ies (study→studies).
> **Sıklık zarfları:** always, usually, often, sometimes, never.
